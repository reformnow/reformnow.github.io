In the theological heritage of the historic Christian faith and orthodox catholic theology, the title "Son of Man" used by Jesus Christ for Himself is far from a humble self-deprecation. Rather, it represents the most profound and authoritative designation in the entire history of redemption.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在大公教会的历史信仰与正统神学的传承体系中，耶稣基督自称为“人子”（The Son of Man），绝非一个简单的谦称。相反，它是整部救赎历史中神学含金量最高、最具有主权宣示性的自称。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Throughout the four Gospels, the "Son of Man" is the designation Jesus uses most frequently to refer to Himself. Orthodox catholic theology unpacks this mystery from three essential dimensions: the fulfillment of prophetic eschatology, the union of the two natures in Christology, and the covenantal representation of the second Adam.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在四部福音书中，“人子”是耶稣最常用来指代自己的称呼。大公教会正统信仰从以下三个核心维度来解答这个神学奥秘：先知预言的终末应验、基督论中神人两性的联合，以及第二个亚当在盟约中的代表性。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 1. The Prophetic Fulfillment: The Divine Monarch of the Heavenly Kingdom
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、 先知预言的应验：至高神圣的“天国君王”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Many modern readers mistakenly assume that the title "Son of Man" emphasizes Jesus' humanity while "Son of God" emphasizes His deity. Within the historic Christian faith, this represents a fundamental misunderstanding. In the context of first-century Second Temple Judaism, the "Son of Man" was a title of staggering divine majesty.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

许多现代读者误以为“人子”强调的是耶稣的人性，“神子”强调的是神性。在历史基督教信仰中，这是一个典型的误解。在公元一世纪第二圣殿时期的犹太背景中，“人子”实际上是一个极具震撼力的神圣弥赛亚头衔。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This title derives directly from the prophetic vision recorded in the Book of Daniel, where a celestial figure is brought before the Ancient of Days:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这个称呼直接源于《但以理书》第七章中所记录的先知异象，在其中一位天上的位格被领到亘古常在者面前：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"I kept looking in the night visions, And behold, with the clouds of heaven One like a Son of Man was coming, And He came up to the Ancient of Days And was presented before Him. And to Him was given dominion, Glory and a kingdom, That all the peoples, nations and men of every language Might serve Him. His dominion is an everlasting dominion Which will not pass away; And His kingdom is one Which will not be destroyed."
— Daniel 7:13-14
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“我在夜间的异象中观看，见有一位像人子的，驾着天云而来，被领到亘古常在者面前，得了权柄、荣耀、国度，使各方、各国、各族的人都事奉祂。祂的权柄是永有的，不能废去；祂的国必不败坏。”
—— 但以理书 7:13-14
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In this prophetic landscape, the "Son of Man" is not a mere mortal, but a pre-existent, supernatural figure who rides upon the clouds—a privilege reserved solely for Yahweh in Hebrew Scripture—and receives absolute, everlasting worship and sovereign judgment over all creation.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在这幅先知异象的宏伟图景中，“人子”绝非普通凡人，而是一个在创造以先便已存在、驾云而来的超自然神圣位格——在希伯来圣经中，驾云而行是唯独耶和华神才享有的特权。祂接受绝对且永恒的敬拜，并对万有执掌至高的审判权。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

From the standpoint of biblical truth, when Jesus designated Himself as the Son of Man, He was declaring to the religious elite that He was the cosmic Judge prophesied by Daniel. This is why when Jesus stood before the high priest and explicitly quoted this Danielic prophecy, the Sanhedrin accused Him of blasphemy and rent their garments, recognizing His claim as an assertion of co-equal divinity with God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

从圣经真理的角度来看，当耶稣自称人子时，祂是在向当时的宗教精英宣告：祂就是但以理所预言的那位跨越时空、在万有之上执掌绝对主权的审判官。因此，当耶稣在法庭上面对大祭司并明确引用这段预言时，公会立即控告祂说了篡夺神权的“僭妄的话”并撕开衣服，因为他们清楚地意识到这一称呼是在宣示与上帝同等的至高神性。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Jesus said to him, 'You have said it yourself; nevertheless I tell you, hereafter you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming on the clouds of heaven.' Then the high priest tore his robes and said, 'He has blasphemed! What further need do we have of witnesses?'"
— Matthew 26:64-65
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“耶稣对他说：‘你说的是。然而，我告诉你们，后来你们要看见人子坐在那大能者地右边，驾着天上的云降临。’大祭司就撕开衣服，说：‘祂说了僭妄的话，我们何必再用见证人呢？’”
—— 马太福音 26:64-65
```

## 2. The Ecumenical Christology: The Hypostatic Union of Two Natures
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、 大公教会的基督论：真神大能与真实人性的结合
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

According to the Chalcedonian Definition (AD 451), which holds supreme authority in orthodox catholic theology, Jesus Christ is "truly God and truly man," possessing two distinct natures in one divine person—"without confusion, without change, without division, without separation." The title "Son of Man" serves as an anchor for this delicate and glorious mystery.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

根据在大公教会中具有无上权威的《迦克墩信经》（公元 451 年），耶稣基督是“真神又是真人”，在一位格中拥有两个截然不同的性情——“不相混乱，不相交换，不能分开，不能离散”。“人子”这一自称正是维系这一尊贵且荣耀之奥秘的稳固锚点。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

By calling Himself the Son of Man, Christ asserted the absolute reality of His human nature against early docetic heresies (such as Gnosticism) which suggested His flesh was a mere illusion. The Scripture records that as the Son of Man, He hungered, wept, bled, and suffered real, physical death. His humanity was not a costume; it was real and complete.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

耶稣通过自称“人子”，向早期否认主真实肉身的“幻影说”（如诺斯底主义）宣告了其人性的绝对真实性。圣经确凿地记录了这位“人子”会饥饿、会哭泣、会流血，并经历了真实且痛苦的肉体死亡。祂的人性绝非一件临时的戏服，而是真实、完整且没有罪污的。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This complete humanity is indispensable for the legality of our salvation. As the early church father Gregory of Nazianzus famously noted, the unassumed is the unredeemed. If Christ did not possess a true human soul and body, He could not represent humanity on the cross, and our sins would remain unatoned.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这种完整的人性对于我们救赎的合法性来说是不可或缺的。正如早期教主拿先斯的贵格利所留下的名言，未被取受的，就不能被救赎。如果基督没有真正的人类灵魂与肉身，祂就无法在十字架上代表人类承受惩罚，我们的罪也依旧得不到赎买。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"For that which He has not assumed He has not healed; but that which is united to His Godhead is also saved. If only half of Adam fell, then that which Christ assumes and saves may be half also; but if the whole of his nature fell, it must be united to the whole nature of Him that was begotten, and so be saved as a whole."
— Gregory of Nazianzus
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“因为凡未被祂取受的，就不能被祂医治；唯有与祂的神性相结合的，才能得救。如果亚当只有一半堕落了，那么基督取受并拯救的一半即可；但如果是他的整个天性都堕落了，就必须与那生者（基督）的整个天性相结合，从而使整个人性整体得救。”
—— 拿先斯的贵格利
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. The Federal Covenant: The Second Adam and Representative Salvation
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、 历史信仰的盟约神学：末后的亚当与救赎的代表
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the covenantal framework of the historic Christian faith, the title "Son of Man" bears profound legal and representative weight, specifically within the doctrine of the Covenant of Works and the Covenant of Grace.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在历史基督教信仰的盟约框架中，“人子”这一头衔在法律和代表性上具有深不可测的分量，特别是在“行为之约”与“恩典盟约”的教义中显得尤为关键。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Adam was the first "Son of Man" in the sense of being the federal head of the original creation. Placed under the Covenant of Works, he failed the probation of obedience, plunging all his natural descendants into spiritual death and total depravity.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

从受造界联邦首领的角度来看，亚当是第一个“人子”（人子一词在希伯来语和阿拉姆语中亦意为“亚当之子”）。他被置于行为之约下，却在顺服的试炼中失败了，从而将他所有的自然后嗣带入了属灵的死亡与全然的败坏之中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"For as in Adam all die, so also in Christ all will be made alive."
— 1 Corinthians 15:22
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“在亚当里众人都死了；照样，在基督里众人也都要复活。”
—— 哥林多前书 15:22
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Jesus Christ, as the ultimate "Son of Man," enters history as the Second Adam and the federal head of a new, redeemed humanity under the Covenant of Grace. Where the first Adam disobeyed in a perfect garden, the Second Adam obeyed perfectly in a fallen, wilderness world, fulfilling every precept of the moral law on behalf of His chosen people.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

耶稣基督作为终极的“人子”，以“末后的亚当”这一身份进入历史，成为恩典盟约下被救赎之新人类的联邦首领。第一个亚当在完美的乐园中选择悖逆，而这第二个亚当却在堕落荒凉的旷野中完全顺服，代表祂的选民履行了道德律法的一切诫命。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Through His life of active obedience and His death of passive obedience, the Son of Man accomplishes the grand reality of double imputation. Our sins and guilt were imputed to Him, and He bore the wrath of God on the cross; His perfect obedience and active righteousness are imputed to us by faith alone, securing our legal justification before a holy God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

通过祂主动顺服的一生和被动顺服的受死，这位人子成就了“双重归算”（Double Imputation）的伟大法律事实：我们的罪与刑罚被归算在祂身上，使祂在十字架上代受了上帝的烈怒；而祂完美的顺服与义，则通过唯独信心被白白地归算给我们，确立了我们在圣洁上帝面前称义的合法地位。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"He made Him who knew no sin to be sin on our behalf, so that we might become the righteousness of God in Him."
— The Apostle Paul (2 Corinthians 5:21)
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“神使那无罪的，替我们成为罪，好叫我们在祂里面成为神的义。”
—— 使徒保罗（哥林多后书 5:21）
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This covenantal union is the unshakeable foundation for the perseverance of the saints. Because the Son of Man has ascended bodily into heaven and is seated at the right hand of the Majesty on high, the eternal security of every believer united to Him is legally guaranteed. The representative Head has triumphed; therefore, His body will never perish.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这种盟约性的联合是圣徒蒙保守（圣徒坚忍）永不摇摆的磐石。既然这位人子已经带着肉身升入高天，坐在至高大能者的右边，那么每一个通过信心与祂联合的选民的永恒安全就得到了法律上的终极保障。代表性的元首已经得胜，祂的身体就绝不会失丧。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. The Messianic Wisdom: The Sovereign Cryptic Title
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、 弥赛亚的智慧：神圣奥秘的“双刃剑”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Why did Jesus systematically favor the title "Son of Man" over more direct and popular terms like "Messiah" or "King of Israel"? In the pastoral theology of the historic Christian faith, this choice reflects the profound redemptive wisdom often called the Messianic Secret.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

为什么耶稣系统性地偏爱“人子”这一称呼，而不是直接使用当时更流行、更直白的“弥赛亚”或“以色列的王”？在历史基督教信仰的教牧神学中，这一选择反映了极其深邃的救赎智慧，即常说的“弥赛亚秘密”。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

During the Second Temple period, popular Jewish expectations had warped the biblical concept of the Messiah into a militaristic, political deliverer who would overthrow Roman occupation. Had Jesus openly proclaimed Himself as a political King, He would have fueled nationalistic rebellions contrary to His spiritual mission.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在第二圣殿时期，犹太民间普遍的期待已经将圣经中的弥赛亚歪曲为一个军事化、政治化的解放者，企图以此推翻罗马的铁腕统治。如果耶稣公开宣称自己是政治性的君王，势必会引燃属世的民族主义暴动，这与祂属灵的救赎使命背道而驰。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"So Jesus, perceiving that they were intending to come and take Him by force to make Him king, withdrew again to the mountain by Himself alone."
— John 6:15
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“耶稣既知道众人要来强逼祂作王，就独自又退到山上去了。”
—— 约翰福音 6:15
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The title "Son of Man" was a double-edged sword of divine wisdom. To the crowds blinded by worldly desires, it sounded like a simple, ambiguous reference to His humanity. Yet to those illuminated by the Holy Spirit, it carried the weight of Daniel's vision and the Isaianic Suffering Servant. It allowed Jesus to reveal His identity progressively, keeping the focus entirely on the necessity of the cross rather than political glory.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

“人子”这个词是神圣智慧的双刃剑。对于那些被属世欲望蒙蔽双眼的群众来说，这听起来像是一个关于祂人性的谦卑且模糊的代称；然而，对于那些被圣灵光照的人来说，它承载着但以理异象的至高权能与以赛亚受苦仆人的双重分量。这使得耶稣能够渐进地显明祂的真实身份，将聚焦点完全锁定在十字架的救赎工作上，而非地上的政治荣耀。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
