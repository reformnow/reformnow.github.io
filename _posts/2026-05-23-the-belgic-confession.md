## 1. Introduction
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Voice from the Shadows
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 幽暗中的声音
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the quiet hours of an autumn night in 1561, a small package flew over the stone wall of the castle of Tournai in the Low Countries. Inside was a newly printed booklet, accompanied by a letter addressed to King Philip II of Spain. It was not a call to rebellion, but a confession of faith, written on behalf of thousands of persecuted Christians who chose to speak the truth even under the shadow of the gallows.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

1561年秋夜的静谧时分，一个包裹从石墙飞过，落入了低地国家图尔奈城堡的围墙内。包裹里是一本刚刚印制好的小册子，以及一封写给西班牙国王腓力二世的信。这绝非一份叛乱的宣言，而是一份信仰的宣誓，代表着数以千计在绞刑架阴影下选择宣告真理的受迫害基督徒。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. A Living Confession
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 活泼的信条
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This document, which became known as the Belgic Confession, is one of the most beautiful and enduring statements of the historic Christian faith. Written by the pastor Guido de Brès, who would eventually seal his testimony with his own blood, the confession was not born in the safety of academic halls, but in the heat of active persecution. It was designed to show that these believers were not anarchists, but faithful heirs of orthodox catholic theology.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这份被称为《比利时信条》的文献，是历史基督教信仰中最为优美且经久不衰的信仰宣告之一。它由牧师吉多·德·布雷（Guido de Brès）撰写，他最终用自己的鲜血盖印了自己的见证。这本信条并非诞生于安全的学术殿堂，而是淬炼于烈火般的迫害之中。它的设立旨在表明，这些信徒并非无政府主义者，而是大公教会正统神学的忠实继承者。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 2. Historical Background: A Faith Born in the Fire
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、历史背景：在烈火中诞生的信仰
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Low Countries Under Spanish Terror
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 西班牙恐怖统治下的低地国家
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

During the mid-16th century, the Low Countries (encompassing modern-day Belgium, the Netherlands, and Luxembourg) were under the absolute rule of Philip II of Spain. Philip was determined to eradicate any deviation from Roman Catholic orthodoxy. The Inquisition was active, and anyone caught holding to the biblical truth apart from Rome was branded a heretic and faced execution. The authorities often conflated these peaceful believers with the radical Anabaptists who had seized Münster in a violent, apocalyptic uprising decades earlier.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

16世纪中叶，低地国家（包括今天的比利时、荷兰和卢森堡）处于西班牙国王腓力二世的专制统治之下。腓力二世决心根除任何背离罗马天主教正统的行为。宗教裁判所横行，任何被发现持守圣经真理、脱离罗马教廷的人，都会被贴上异端的标签并面临死刑。当局往往将这些爱好和平的信徒与几十年前在明斯特发动暴力、末日论起义的激进再洗礼派混为一谈。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To clear their name of sedition and to provide a unified voice for the scattered house churches, Guido de Brès drafted the confession. He wanted to demonstrate to the Spanish crown that their beliefs were in complete alignment with the teachings of the Apostles and the historic ecumenical creeds.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

为了洗刷背负的“叛乱”罪名，并为四散的家庭教会提供一个统一的信仰声音，吉多·德·布雷起草了这份信条。他希望向西班牙王室证明，他们的信仰与使徒的教导以及历史上大公会议的信经完全一致。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Guido de Brès and the Castle Wall
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 吉多·德·布雷与城堡的围墙
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The act of throwing the confession over the castle wall was a daring plea for justice. Attached to the confession was a solemn address to King Philip II. The authors made it clear that while they were law-abiding citizens who prayed for the king's government, they would never compromise on the truth of God's Word.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

将信条抛过城堡围墙的举动，是一次大无畏的公义呼吁。信条附有一封致国王腓力二世的庄严请愿书。作者们表明，尽管他们是守法的公民，并为国王的政府祷告，但他们绝不会在上帝话语的真理上妥协。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The cover letter contained one of the most courageous statements in church history, expressing their willingness to suffer for Christ rather than recant.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这封随信的陈情书包含了教会历史上最勇敢的誓言之一，表达了他们宁愿为基督受苦也绝不背道的决心。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"We offer our backs to stripes, our tongues to knives, our mouths to gags, and our whole bodies to the fire, rather than deny the truth of your Word."
— Guido de Brès, Letter to Philip II (1561)
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“我们宁可将脊背献给鞭打，将舌头献给刀刃，将口舌献给塞口物，将整个身体献给烈火，也决不否认您话语的真理。”
—— 吉多·德·布雷，致腓力二世的信（1561 年）
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Price of Faith
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 信仰的代价
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The response from the Spanish authorities was not mercy, but intensified persecution. In 1567, after the city of Valenciennes fell to the Spanish troops, Guido de Brès was captured. He spent his final days in a dark dungeon, writing letters of comfort to his wife and congregation. On May 31, 1567, he was led to the gallows and hanged. De Brès sealed the confession with his life, leaving behind a legacy that would shape the church for centuries to come.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

西班牙当局的回应非但没有仁慈，反而加剧了迫害。1567年，在瓦朗谢讷城陷落于西班牙军队之手后，吉多·德·布雷被捕。他在黑暗的死囚牢房中度过了最后的日子，给妻子和会众写信带去安慰。1567年5月31日，他被带上绞刑架处以绞刑。德·布雷用自己的生命为这份信条盖印，留下了塑造此后数百年教会的属灵遗产。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. Theological Core: Continuity and Apologetic Separation
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、神学核心：大公传承与护教分野
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Continuity with the Universal Church
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 与大公教会历史的正统相承
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

One of the central arguments of the Belgic Confession is that the faith of the Protestant reformers is not a \"new theology,\" but the restoration of the historic Christian faith. Articles 1 through 9 outline the doctrine of God and the Holy Trinity, expressing complete agreement with the ancient ecumenical creeds (Apostles', Nicene, and Athanasian).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《比利时信条》的核心论点之一是，新教改革者的信仰绝非“新发明的神学”，而是对历史基督教信仰的恢复。信条第1条至第9条详述了上帝论和圣三一教义，表达了与古代大公信经（使徒信经、尼西亚信经、亚他那修信经）的完全一致。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

By grounding their faith in the historical consensus of the early church, the confession argues that those who stand on biblical truth are the true spiritual heirs of the early church fathers, while Rome had drifted into innovation.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

通过将他们的信仰根植于早期教会的历史共识，信条论证了那些站立在圣经真理之上的人才是早期教父真正的属灵后嗣，而罗马教廷则在偏离中走入了歧路。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Correction of the Errors of Rome
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 纠正罗马教廷的偏差
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

While maintaining continuity, the confession does not shy away from correcting the serious errors that had accumulated in the medieval Roman church. It rejects the authority of the Apocrypha as equal to the inspired canonical scriptures (Article 6), affirms justification by faith alone apart from human merit (Article 22-24), and rejects the Catholic doctrine of the mass as a re-sacrifice of Christ (Article 35).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在保持大公继承性的同时，该信条并未回避纠正中世纪罗马教会所积累的严重偏差。它拒绝将次经视作与受默示之正典圣经同等权威的文献（第6条），确认人称义是唯独因着信而与人的功德无关（第22至24条），并拒绝了天主教将弥撒视为基督重复献祭的圣礼观（第35条）。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. Separation from Revolutionary Sects
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 与激进革命派的分野
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Crucially, the Belgic Confession was an apology directed against the radical Anabaptists. In Article 36 (on Civil Government) and Article 34 (on Baptism), the confession explicitly separates the orthodox Protestant movement from revolutionary groups. It rejects the radical rejection of civil authority and the denial of infant baptism, proving that historic Protestantism supports social order and the rule of law.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

至关重要的一点是，《比利时信条》也是一份针对激进再洗礼派的辩护书。在关于政府的第36条和关于洗礼的第34条中，该信条明确将正统新教运动与革命激进团体区分开来。它拒绝了对世俗政府权柄的激进否定，也反对拒绝婴儿洗礼的做法，以此证明历史上的新教信仰完全支持社会秩序和法治。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. Why the Modern Church Universal Needs the Belgic Confession
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、为什么现代大公教会依然需要《比利时信条》
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Guarding Against the Plague of Vagueness
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 抵御神学模糊与教义虚无
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Today, many Protestant churches suffer from a severe crisis of identity. In an attempt to be inclusive and culturally relevant, many have abandoned clear doctrinal boundaries, resulting in a vague, therapeutic faith that cannot withstand the storms of modern secularism.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

今天，许多新教教会面临着严重的身份认同危机。为了追求文化上的关联度与包容性，许多教会抛弃了清晰的教义界限，导致了一种模糊的、心理治疗式的信仰，这种信仰根本无法抵御现代世俗主义的狂风暴雨。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Belgic Confession provides a robust antidote to this vagueness. By articulating biblical truth systematically, it anchors the believer's mind in objective, historical reality rather than subjective experience.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《比利时信条》为这种教义上的模糊提供了一剂强效解药。通过系统地阐明圣经真理，它将信徒的心智锚定在客观的历史事实上，而非主观的个人体验之中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Beloved, while I was making every effort to write to you about our common salvation, I felt the necessity to write to you appealing that you contend earnestly for the faith which was once for all handed down to the saints."
— Jude 1:3
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“亲爱的弟兄啊，我想尽心写信给你们，论我们同得救恩的时候，就不得不写信劝你们，要为从前一次交付圣徒的真道竭力地争辩。”
—— 犹大书 1:3
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Restoring the Marks of a True Church
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 重拾真教会的标志
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the modern consumerist culture, churches are often judged by their programs, musical style, or the charisma of the preacher. The Belgic Confession redirects our attention to what God Himself requires of His church. Article 29 famously outlines the three marks of a true church: the pure preaching of the Gospel, the pure administration of the sacraments, and the exercise of church discipline.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在现代消费主义文化中，教会往往根据其活动项目、音乐风格或牧师的个人魅力来被评估。而《比利时信条》则将我们的注意力重新引向神自己对祂教会的要求。其著名的第29条列出了真教会的三个标志：纯正福音教义的传讲、纯正圣礼的施行以及教会规戒的执行。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"The marks by which the true Church is known are these: If the pure doctrine of the gospel is preached therein; if she maintains the pure administration of the sacraments as instituted by Christ; if church discipline is exercised in punishing of sin..."
— Belgic Confession, Article 29
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“认出真教会的标志如下：如果其中传讲纯正的福音教义；如果她维持由基督设立的纯正圣礼管理；如果执行教会规戒以惩罚罪恶……”
—— 《比利时信条》，第 29 条
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If a church lacks these marks, no matter how large or popular it is, it fails to be a true church under Christ's lordship. The Belgic Confession serves as a vital diagnostic tool for modern Protestant congregations to assess their health and align themselves with the Word of God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

如果一个教会缺乏这些标志，无论它规模多么宏大或多么受欢迎，在基督的主权治理下，它就称不上是真正的教会。《比利时信条》是现代新教会众评估自身健康状况、并使自身重新归正于上帝话语之下的关键诊断工具。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. Aligning Church and State in Truth
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 重整政教关系的真理界线
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In an age of intense political polarization and ideological pressure, the relationship between church and state has become a major point of confusion. Some advocate for a radical separation that excludes faith from the public square, while others seek to weaponize the state for religious supremacy.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在一个政治高度两极分化和意识形态压力巨大的时代，教会与国家政权的关系已成为混乱的主要源头。有些人主张将信仰完全排除在公共领域之外的激进分离；另一些人则试图将国家政权工具化，以实现某种宗教霸权。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Article 36 of the Belgic Confession provides a balanced biblical framework: it recognizes that the civil government is instituted by God to curb lawlessness, and that Christians owe obedience to magistrates in all things that do not violate God's law. At the same time, it establishes that Christ is the ultimate King over both the church and the state, drawing a clear line that protects the freedom of the church to obey God rather than men when conflict arises.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《比利时信条》第36条提供了一个平衡的圣经框架：它承认世俗政府由神设立以遏制不法行为，基督徒在不违背神律法的前提下理应顺从官长。同时，它确立了基督才是教会和国家的最高君王，画出了一条清晰的界线，保护教会在冲突发生时能自由地顺从神而非顺从人。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. Conclusion
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、结语
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Belgic Confession is not a relic of the 16th century to be forgotten in libraries. It is a living testament of the historic Christian faith, sealed with the blood of martyrs like Guido de Brès. Its clear exposition of the Holy Scriptures, its continuity with the universal church, and its sharp apologetic boundaries are precisely what the modern Protestant church needs to navigate the confusion of our times.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《比利时信条》绝不是被遗忘在图书馆里的16世纪文物。它是一份由像吉多·德·布雷这样的殉道者用鲜血浇灌的历史基督教信仰的活见证。它对圣经真理的清晰阐释、它与大公教会的历史连续性，以及它锐利的护教学界线，恰恰是现代新教教会在冲破时代迷雾时所迫切需要的。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

By reclaiming this confession, modern churches can stand once again on the firm foundation of biblical truth, testifying to the world with courage and clarity.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

通过重拾这一信条，现代教会可以重新站立在圣经真理的坚固磐石上，以勇气和清晰向世界发出见证。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"but in case I am delayed, I write so that you will know how one ought to conduct himself in the household of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and support of the truth."
— 1 Timothy 3:15
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“倘若我延搁日久，你也可以知道在神的家中当怎样行。这家就是永生神的教会，真理的柱石和根基。”
—— 提摩太前书 3:15
```
