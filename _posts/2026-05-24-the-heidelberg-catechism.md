## 1. Introduction
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Comfort of Belonging
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 归属的安慰
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Amidst the political and theological turmoil of 16th-century Europe, Elector Frederick III of the Palatinate faced a deeply divided territory. Lutherans, followers of Swiss theologians, and Roman Catholics constantly clashed over the interpretation of the Lord's Supper and other key doctrines. Seeking to bring peace to his land and a reliable guide for the instruction of youth, Frederick commissioned a document that would become one of the most widely translated, beloved, and comforting summaries of historic Christian theology: the Heidelberg Catechism.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在16世纪欧洲的政治与神学动荡中，普法尔茨选帝侯腓特烈三世面临着一个分裂的领土。路德宗信徒、瑞士神学家的追随者以及罗马天主教徒在圣餐礼的理解和其他核心教义上不断发生冲突。为了给他的土地带来和平，并为年轻人的教育提供一份可靠的指南，腓特烈委任撰写了一份文献，它后来成为历史上翻译最广、最受喜爱且最给人带来安慰的基督教神学总结之一：《海德堡要理问答》。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. A Pastoral Tone
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 牧养的基调
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

While many theological documents of the era were structured as dry, academic definitions, the Heidelberg Catechism begins with an intensely personal and pastoral question: "What is your only comfort in life and in death?" This opening question sets the tone for the entire catechism, framing all of Christian doctrine not as mere intellectual assent, but as a source of deep, existential comfort.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

虽然那个时代的许多神学文献都被结构化为枯燥的、学术性的定义，但《海德堡要理问答》却以一个极具个人色彩和牧养关怀的问题开始：“你生死的唯一安慰是什么？”这一开篇问题为整个问答奠定了基调，将所有的基督教教义并非框定为纯粹的智力认同，而是将其作为深层存在性安慰的源泉。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Q. What is your only comfort in life and in death?
A. That I am not my own, but belong—body and soul, in life and in death—to my faithful Savior Jesus Christ."
— Heidelberg Catechism, Q&A 1
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“问：你生死的唯一安慰是什么？
答：我无论在肉体还是灵魂，在生还是在死，都不属于自己，而是属于我信实的救主耶稣基督。”
—— 《海德堡要理问答》，第一问
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 2. Historical Background: Unity in a Divided Land
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、历史背景：分裂国土中的合一呼声
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Elector Frederick III and the Palatinate
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 选帝侯腓特烈三世与普法尔茨
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In 1559, Frederick III became the Elector of the Palatinate, a strategic region in Germany. He inherited a territory racked by intense religious controversy. The Peace of Augsburg (1555) had allowed German princes to choose either Lutheranism or Catholicism for their lands, but Swiss theological developments were excluded from legal protection. Elector Frederick, convinced of the biblical truth of the Swiss theological insights, wanted to establish a unified expression of faith that would bridge the gaps and provide a peaceful path forward.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

1559年，腓特烈三世成为德意志战略重地普法尔茨的选帝侯。他接管的是一个被激烈宗教争端撕裂的领土。虽然《奥格斯堡和约》（1555年）允许德意志诸侯为其领地选择路德宗或天主教，但源自瑞士的神学发展却被排除在法律保护之外。选帝侯腓特烈确信瑞士神学见解符合圣经真理，因而希望确立一种统一的信仰表达，以弥合分歧，并提供一条和平的前行之路。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Young Scholars: Ursinus and Olevianus
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 年轻学者：乌尔西努斯与奥利维亚努斯
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

To achieve this, Frederick assembled a theological commission. The primary drafting was entrusted to Zacharias Ursinus, a twenty-eight-year-old professor of theology, and Caspar Olevianus, a twenty-six-year-old court preacher. Ursinus provided the systematic precision, drawing from both German and Swiss streams of the Protestant reformation. Olevianus brought pastoral warmth and eloquence, ensuring the text remained accessible and comforting for ordinary believers.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

为了实现这一目标，腓特烈召集了一个神学委员会。主要的起草工作被托付给二十八岁的神学教授扎卡里亚斯·乌尔西努斯（Zacharias Ursinus）和二十六岁的宫廷牧师卡斯帕·奥利维亚努斯（Caspar Olevianus）。乌尔西努斯提供了系统神学的精确性，汲取了德意志和瑞士新教改革的思想流派；奥利维亚努斯则带来了牧养的热忱与雄辩，确保文本对普通信徒而言依然易懂且富有安慰。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Augsburg Defense (1566)
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 奥格斯堡辩护 (1566)
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Published on January 19, 1563, the catechism immediately drew fierce criticism from both Roman Catholic authorities and strict Lutherans. In 1566, Frederick III was summoned before the Imperial Diet of Augsburg to defend the catechism against charges of heresy, which carried the threat of losing his electors. Frederick defended the document with such biblical conviction, stating he would rather lose his crown than deny the gospel, that the Emperor and princes desisted from taking action against him.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

该问答于1563年1月19日出版后，立即引来了罗马天主教当局和严格路德宗信徒的猛烈批评。1566年，腓特烈三世被传唤到奥格斯堡帝国议会，为问答免受异端指控进行辩护，这面临着失去选帝侯爵位的威胁。腓特烈以坚固的圣经确信为该文献辩护，宣称他宁愿失去王冠也不愿否认福音，这使皇帝和诸侯深受震撼，最终放弃了对他采取行动。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. Structure and Theological Heart: Comfort in Truth
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、结构与神学核心：真理中的安慰
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Roman Road of Salvation
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 救恩的“罗马书之路”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The catechism consists of 129 questions and answers, brilliantly structured into three main parts that mirror the Apostle Paul's argument in the Book of Romans:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

该问答由129个问题和回答组成，精妙地分为三个主要部分，呼应了使徒保罗在《罗马书》中的论证逻辑：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

1. **The Misery of Man** (Questions 3-11): How our sin and misery are revealed through the law of God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

1. **人类的惨状/罪**（第3-11问）：借着上帝的律法暴露出我们的罪恶与悲惨。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

2. **The Deliverance of Man** (Questions 12-85): How we are redeemed by Christ through faith, expounding the Apostles' Creed and the Sacraments.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

2. **人类的拯救/恩典**（第12-85问）：我们如何因信借着基督得赎，详细阐述了使徒信经和圣礼。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

3. **Thankfulness** (Questions 86-129): How we express our gratitude to God for salvation through obedience to the Ten Commandments and prayer through the Lord's Prayer.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

3. **人类的感恩**（第86-129问）：我们如何通过顺从十诫与主祷文的祈祷，向神表达我们对其救恩的感激之情。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Grace-Driven Obedience
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 恩典驱动的顺从
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

By placing the Ten Commandments under the section of "Thankfulness" rather than "Misery" or "Deliverance," the Heidelberg Catechism teaches that obedience is not a means to earn salvation, but the natural, grateful overflow of a heart already redeemed by grace. This preserves the believer from both legalism and lawlessness.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

通过将十诫置于“感恩”部分而非“惨状”或“拯救”部分，《海德堡要理问答》教导说，顺从律法绝非赚取救恩的手段，而是已被恩典救赎的心灵自然流露出的感激之情。这保护了信徒免于陷入律法主义和废弃律法主义的双重误区。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. Why the Modern Church Needs the Heidelberg Catechism
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、为什么现代教会需要《海德堡要理问答》
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Anchoring Identity in a Secular Age
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 在世俗时代中锚定身份认同
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

We live in an age characterized by expressive individualism and deep existential anxiety. Society tells us that we belong to ourselves, and that we must construct our own identity and worth. This places an crushing burden on the individual. The Heidelberg Catechism counters this by declaring that our greatest comfort is that we do not belong to ourselves, but to a faithful Savior who bought us with a price.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

我们生活在一个以表现主义个人主义和深层存在性焦虑为特征的时代。社会告诉我们，我们属于自己，必须自我构建身份与价值。这给个人带来了压碎性的重担。而《海德堡要理问答》则通过宣告我们最大的安慰是“我不属于自己，而是属于用重价买赎我的信实救主”来对此予以回击。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God, and that you are not your own? For you have been bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body."
— 1 Corinthians 6:19-20
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“岂不知你们的身子就是圣灵的殿吗？这圣灵是从神而来，住在你们里头的；并且你们不是自己的人，因为你们是重价买来的。所以，要在你们的身子上荣耀神。”
—— 哥林多前书 6:19-20
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Systematic Instruction Over Pragmatism
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 以系统化培育取代实用主义
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Many modern churches have replaced doctrinal training with pragmatic workshops or emotional entertainment. This has produced a generation of believers with shallow theological roots, easily swept away by cultural tides. The Heidelberg Catechism is divided into 52 weekly sections (Lord's Days), allowing churches to preach and teach the entirety of Christian doctrine over the course of a year. It provides a balanced, comprehensive, and tested curriculum for discipleship.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

许多现代教会用实用性的工作坊或情感化的娱乐取代了教义培训。这导致一代信徒的神学根基浅薄，极易被文化浪潮冲走。《海德堡要理问答》被划分为52个主日部分，允许教会在一年的时间里完整地宣讲和教导基督教核心教义。它为门徒训练提供了一个平衡、全面且经受过时间检验的课表。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. Correcting the Mind While Warring the Heart
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 理性的规正与心灵的温热
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Pure academic theology can sometimes become cold and intellectualized, while purely experiential movements can descend into emotionalism. The Heidelberg Catechism perfectly marries the mind and the heart. It demands intellectual precision (explaining the Trinity, the natures of Christ, and the sacraments) but always applies these truths directly to the believer's personal assurance and comfort.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

纯粹学术性的神学有时会变得冰冷和智力化，而纯粹体验性的运动则可能堕入情绪主义。《海德堡要理问答》将理智与心灵完美地结合在一起。它要求教义上的严谨（解释三位一体、基督的神人二性以及圣礼），但始终将这些真理直接应用到信徒个人的确据与安慰上。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. Conclusion
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、结语
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Heidelberg Catechism is not a museum piece from the 16th century, but a living guide for the church today. It shows us that theological depth does not require the sacrifice of pastoral warmth. In an age of anxiety, it reminds us of the secure anchor we have in Jesus Christ.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《海德堡要理问答》不是一件来自16世纪的博物馆陈列品，而是当今教会活泼的指引。它向我们证明，神学的深度不需要以牺牲牧养的温情为代价。在这样一个焦虑的时代，它提醒我们在耶稣基督里所拥有的安全之锚。
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

By recovering this historic summary of biblical truth, the modern church can find the theological stability and the spiritual joy necessary to witness faithfully in a hostile world.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

通过恢复这一对圣经真理的历史性总结，现代教会能够寻得在充满敌意的世界中作信实见证所必需的神学稳固性与属灵喜乐。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"For not one of us lives for himself, and not one dies for himself; for if we live, we live for the Lord, or if we die, we die for the Lord; therefore whether we live or die, we are the Lord’s."
— Romans 14:7-8
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“我们没有一个人为自己活，也没有一个人为自己死。我们若活着，是为主而活；若死了，是为主而死。所以，我们或活或死总是主的人。”
—— 罗马书 14:7-8
```
