## 1. Introduction
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Anchor of Divine Grace
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 神圣恩典的锚
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the winter of 1618, delegates from across Europe gathered in the historic Dutch city of Dordrecht. They did not assemble to debate minor ecclesiastical ceremonies, but to address a crisis that threatened to tear the Protestant world apart: the nature of salvation itself. The document they produced, the Canons of Dort, stands as one of the most precise and comforting defenses of the sovereign grace of God in church history.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

1618年的冬日，来自欧洲各地的代表聚集在荷兰历史悠久的重镇多德雷赫特（Dordrecht）。他们聚集在一起并非为了争论琐碎的教会礼仪，而是为了应对一场威胁要将新教世界撕裂的危机：救恩的本质。他们最终制定的文献——《多特信经》，作为教会历史上对上帝主权恩典最精准且最给人带来安慰的辩护之一而巍然屹立。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. A Pastoral Shield
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 牧养的盾牌
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Contrary to the modern caricature of this synod as a cold, intellectual exercise in fatalism, the Canons of Dort were written with a deeply pastoral heart. Their purpose was to assure anxious believers that their salvation is eternally secure because it depends entirely on the faithfulness of God, not on the shifting sands of human will.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

与现代人将该会议丑化为对宿命论所作的冰冷、智力化辩难截然相反，《多特信经》的撰写倾注了极为深切的牧者心肠。其目的在于向焦虑的信徒保证，他们的救恩是永远稳固的，因为救恩完全取决于上帝的信实，而非人意志那如流沙般的波动。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

---

## 2. Historical Background: The Arminian Challenge
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、历史背景：阿米念派的挑战
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Rise of the Remonstrants
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 抗辩派的兴起
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The controversy began with the followers of Jacobus Arminius, a theology professor at Leiden. In 1610, after his death, his followers published a document called the *Remonstrance*, which presented five theological articles. These articles challenged the historic understanding of salvation by introducing elements of human cooperation, making election conditional on foreseen faith, and asserting that saving grace could be resisted and lost.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

争端始于莱顿大学神学教授雅各·阿米念（Jacobus Arminius）的追随者。1610年阿米念去世后，他的追随者发表了一份名为《抗辩文》的文献，提出了五个神学条款。这些条款通过引入人的合作因素，使拣选取决于神预见人有信心，并声称拯救的恩典是可以被抗拒且可能失去的，从而挑战了历史上对救恩的理解。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. An International Synod
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 国际性的大公会议
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To resolve this theological crisis, the Dutch government called an international synod. The assembly was truly ecumenical, with delegates arriving not only from the Netherlands but also from Great Britain, Switzerland, and various German principalities. Over the course of six months and 180 sessions, the Synod meticulously examined the five Arminian points against the light of Holy Scripture.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

为了解决这一神学危机，荷兰政府召集了一次国际性的大公会议。这门会议是真正具有大公色彩的，代表们不仅来自荷兰本土，还来自大不列颠、瑞士和德意志的各个公国。在为期六个月、共180次会议的历程中，多特会议根据圣经的光照，极其详尽地审视了抗辩派的这五个要点。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. The Structure and Significance of the Canons
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、信经的结构与深层意义
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. A Responding Document
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 响应性的文献
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

It is important to note that the Canons of Dort are not a comprehensive confession of faith. Rather, they are a specific judicial response to the five points of the Remonstrants. This is why they are structured around five headers, which are often summarized today as the \"doctrines of grace.\"
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

值得注意的是，《多特信经》并不像其他信条那样是一份全面的信仰认信。相反，它们是针对抗辩派五个要点所作的特定司法性回应。这就是为什么它们围绕着五个核心要点展开，而这些要点在今天通常被总结为“主权恩典的教义”。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Thesis and Rejection of Errors
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 论点与拒绝谬误
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

For each doctrine, the Canons provide a positive explanation based on Scripture, followed by a detailed \"Rejection of Errors\" section. This dual structure allowed the synod to not only define the truth but also expose and correct the subtle errors that dilute the gospel.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

针对每一项教义，信经都基于圣经提供了正面的阐释，随后附有详尽的“拒绝谬误”部分。这种双重结构使会议不仅能够界定真理，还能揭露并纠正那些稀释福音的细微偏差。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Election is the unchangeable purpose of God, whereby, before the foundation of the world, He hath out of mere grace, according to the sovereign good pleasure of His own will, chosen from the whole human race... a certain number of persons to redemption in Christ."
— Canons of Dort, First Head of Doctrine, Article 7
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“拣选是上帝无始以来不变的旨意，在创立世界以前，祂出于纯然的恩典，按照祂自己主权所喜悦的意旨，从整个人类中……拣选了特定数量的人，在基督里得蒙救赎。”
—— 《多特信经》，第一要点，第七条
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. Why the Modern Church Needs the Canons of Dort
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、为什么现代每个教会都需要《多特信经》
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Reclaiming Soli Deo Gloria: All Glory to God
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 重申“唯独上帝荣耀”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the modern church, the purity of the gospel is constantly under threat from soft synergism—the belief that salvation is a partnership where God does His part and man must do the rest. This shift subtly places the final cause of salvation back on the human will, leaving believers with either pride (if they think they chose well) or despair (if they fear they might fall away).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在现代教会中，福音的纯洁性不断受到“温和神人合作说”的威胁——即认为救恩是一场合作，上帝完成祂的部分，而人必须完成其余的部分。这种转移悄然将救恩的终极因落在了人的自由意志上，导致信徒要么产生骄傲（如果他们认为自己做出了正确的选择），要么陷入绝望（如果他们害怕自己会从恩典中堕落）。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Canons of Dort protect the church by declaring that salvation is monergistic—a work of God's sovereign grace from beginning to end. It is God who elects, God who redeems, God who regenerates, and God who preserves. This guarantees that all glory belongs to God alone.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《多特信经》通过宣告救恩是单方发出的——从头到尾都是上帝主权恩典的工作，来保守教会。是上帝拣选、上帝救赎、上帝使人重生、上帝保守到底。这确保了所有的荣耀唯独属于上帝。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"just as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, that we would be holy and blameless before Him. In love He predestined us to adoption as sons through Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the good pleasure of His will..."
— Ephesians 1:4-5
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“就如神从创立世界以前，在基督里拣选了我们，使我们在他面前成为圣洁，无有瑕疵；又因爱我们，按着自己的意旨所喜悦的，预定我们借着耶稣基督得儿子的名分……”
—— 以弗所书 1:4-5
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Providing Unshakable Assurance of Salvation
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 提供永不动摇的救恩确据
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Many modern believers struggle with constant doubt, wondering if they have enough faith or if they will stumble and fall. The Canons address this directly in the Fifth Head of Doctrine (Perseverance of the Saints), showing that our security is anchored not in the strength of our hold on Christ, but in the strength of His hold on us. This truth provides true peace and drives the believer toward joyful holiness rather than anxious compliance.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

许多现代信徒都在不断的怀疑中挣扎，想知道自己是否有足够的信心，或者自己是否会跌倒流失。信经在第五要点（圣徒的坚韧）中直接回应了这一问题，表明我们的安全并不是锚定在我们抓紧基督的力量上，而是锚定在祂抓住我们的力量上。这一真理带来了真正的平安，并驱动信徒走向喜乐的圣洁，而非焦虑的顺从。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. Guarding Against the Errors of Decisionism
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 抵御“决志主义”的偏差
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In an era where evangelism is often reduced to emotional pressure and quick \"decisions,\" the Canons provide a necessary theological correction. They remind us that faith is not a product of human capacity or persuasion, but a supernatural gift of the Holy Spirit who raises spiritually dead hearts to life.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在传福音往往被简化为情感压力和快速“决志”的时代，该信经提供了一剂必要的神学矫正。它们提醒我们，信心并非人自身能力或说服力的产物，而是圣灵超自然的恩赐，祂使属灵死亡的心灵活过来。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"For by grace you have been saved through faith; and that not of yourselves, it is the gift of God; not as a result of works, so that no one may boast."
— Ephesians 2:8-9
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“你们得救是本乎恩，也因着信；这并不是出于自己，乃是神所赐的；也不是出于行为，免得有人自夸。”
—— 以弗所书 2:8-9
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. Conclusion
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、结语
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Canons of Dort are not a theological weapon designed to encourage dry speculation, but a pastoral shield to protect the sheep of Christ. They comfort the weak, assure the doubting, and glorify the Savior. Any church that desires to remain anchored in the biblical gospel of free and sovereign grace needs the clarity and protection that this historic document offers.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《多特信经》绝非用以助长枯燥思辨的神学武器，而是保护基督羊群的牧养盾牌。它安慰软弱者，确信怀疑者，并荣耀救主。任何渴望将自身锚定在白白得来且主权恩典之圣经福音中的教会，都需要这份历史文献所提供的清晰与保护。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
