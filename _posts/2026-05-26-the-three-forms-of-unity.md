## 1. Introduction
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. A Threefold Cord
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 三股合成的绳子
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Throughout church history, the preservation of biblical truth has never been a passive endeavor. In the fires of the 16th and 17th centuries, as the church sought to recover the simplicity of the apostolic gospel, three distinct documents emerged: the Belgic Confession (1561), the Heidelberg Catechism (1563), and the Canons of Dort (1619). Collectively known as the Three Forms of Unity (联合三信条), they form a robust, harmonious, and comprehensive standard of the historic Christian faith.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在教会历史中，对圣经真理的守护从来都不是一项被动的任务。在16和17世纪的烈火中，当教会努力恢复使徒福音的纯朴时，诞生了三份独特的文献：《比利时信条》（1561年）、《海德堡要理问答》（1563年）以及《多特信经》（1619年）。它们被统称为《联合三信条》，构成了历史基督教信仰中稳固、和谐且全面的信仰标准。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Creeds as Safeguards
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 作为保障的信条
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

These documents are not intended to rival or replace Holy Scripture. Rather, they serve as a faithful map, outlining what the church collectively believes the Bible teaches. In an era of theological shift and consumerist religion, these three standards remain as vital today as they were centuries ago.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这些文献并不是要与圣经竞争或取代圣经。相反，它们是一份忠实的地图，勾勒出教会集体认信的圣经教导。在这样一个神学变迁与消费主义宗教盛行的时代，这三项标准在今天依然与数百年前一样至关重要。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Creeds are not intended to be a substitute for the Bible, but to show what the church believes the Bible to teach."
— Charles Hodge
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“信经与信条绝非用来取代圣经，而是用以表明教会所认信的圣经教导究竟为何。”
—— 查尔斯·霍奇
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 2. The Collective Significance of the Three Forms
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、联合三信条的共同意义
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Belgic Confession: The Objective Confession
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 《比利时信条》：客观的教义宣誓
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Belgic Confession provides the objective foundation. It systematically outlines the entire counsel of God, from the nature of the Triune God, the creation and fall of man, the work of Christ, to the marks of the true church and the role of civil government. It establishes the theological boundaries that define historic, orthodox Christianity in continuity with the early ecumenical creeds.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《比利时信条》提供了客观的根基。它系统地勾勒出上帝完整的旨意——从三位一体上帝的本质、人类的创造与堕落、基督的工作，到真教会的标志以及世俗政府的角色。它确立了神学界线，界定了与早期大公信经一脉相承的历史性正统基督教信仰。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Heidelberg Catechism: The Warm Discipleship
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 《海德堡要理问答》：温热的门徒指引
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Heidelberg Catechism provides the subjective and pastoral application. Structuring its 129 questions around the themes of Guilt, Grace, and Gratitude, it translates systematic theology into personal comfort. It teaches the believer how the truth of the gospel applies to their life, death, and daily struggle, offering a weekly guide for discipleship and instruction.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《海德堡要理问答》提供了主观与牧养的应用。它围绕着“罪、恩典、感恩”的主题，将129个问答组织起来，把系统神学转化为个人的安慰。它教导信徒如何将福音的真理应用于自己的生存、死亡以及每日的挣扎中，为门徒训练和基督教教育提供了一份按周循序渐进的指南。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. Canons of Dort: The Defense of Grace
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 《多特信经》：对主权恩典的捍卫
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Canons of Dort provide the protective shield. In response to errors that sought to turn salvation into a cooperative venture between God and man, the Canons defined the monergistic purity of saving grace. They declare that salvation is entirely of the Lord, assuring the believer of God's preservation and ensuring that all glory belongs to Him alone.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《多特信经》提供了防御性的盾牌。为了回应那些试图将救恩变为神人合作冒险的谬误，多特信经界定了拯救恩典那“唯独上帝动工”的纯洁性。它们宣告救恩完全出于耶和华，向信徒保证了上帝必保守到底，并确保所有的荣耀唯独归于上帝。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Together, they form a perfect balance: the Belgic Confession gives us the *truth* we must believe; the Heidelberg Catechism gives us the *comfort* we must experience; the Canons of Dort give us the *grace* we must defend.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

它们结合在一起，构成了完美的平衡：《比利时信条》赐予我们必须坚信的**真理**；《海德堡要理问答》赐予我们必须体验的**安慰**；《多特信经》则赐予我们必须捍卫的**恩典**。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. Why Every Church Should Adhere to Them
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、为什么每个教会都应该遵循它们
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Protection Against Doctrinal Drift
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 防范教义的随流失去
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

No church remains doctrinally static without intentional guardrails. Without a clear, public confession of faith, a congregation will inevitably drift toward the dominant cultural and theological trends of its age. Adhering to the Three Forms of Unity provides a time-tested anchor that keeps the pulpit aligned with the biblical gospel.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

没有一个教会在缺乏刻意设立的护栏时，还能在教义上保持稳固。如果没有一份清晰、公开的信仰认信，会众势必会随从其时代主流的文化和神学潮流。《联合三信条》提供了一个经受过时间考验的锚，使讲台始终与圣经中的福音保持一致。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"As a result, we are no longer to be children, tossed here and there by waves and carried around by every wind of doctrine, by the trickery of people, by craftiness in deceitful scheming..."
— Ephesians 4:14
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“使我们不再作小孩子，中了人的诡计和欺骗的法术，被一切异教之风摇动，飘来飘去，就随从各样的异端。”
—— 以弗所书 4:14
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Safeguarding Church Unity and Order
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 保障教会的合一与秩序
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

True unity is built on shared truth, not on a vague compromise of beliefs. By adopting these standards, a church establishes a transparent covenant. The elders, pastors, and members all agree on the boundaries of truth, protecting the flock from the arbitrary opinions or personal idiosyncrasies of individual leaders.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

真正的合一是建立在共同承认的真理之上的，而不是建立在对信仰的模糊妥协上。通过采纳这些标准，教会建立了一个透明的圣约。长老、牧师和信徒在真理的界线上达成共识，从而保护羊群免受个别领袖任意想法或个人怪癖的左右。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. What It Means If a Church Abandons These Three Confessions
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、如果一个教会抛弃这三个信条，意味着什么
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Vulnerability to Doctrinal Tyranny
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 暴露于个人意志的“神学独裁”下
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

When a church throws out historic confessions under the slogan "no creed but Christ," it does not actually eliminate creeds. Instead, it replaces a public, historically tested confession with the private, unwritten confession of the current pastor or leadership. The church becomes vulnerable to doctrinal tyranny, where the theological opinions of the pastor become the absolute standard, and the congregation has no objective basis to evaluate or correct the teaching.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

当一个教会以“除基督外无信条”为口号抛弃历史性信条时，它其实并没有真正消灭信条。相反，它是用当前牧师或领袖个人私下的、未成文的信条，取代了公开的、经受过历史检验的认信。教会极易陷入神学独裁中——牧师个人的神学观点成了绝对标准，而会众则失去了评估或纠正教导的客观依据。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Slide Into Human-Centered Theology
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 走向以人为中心的神学滑坡
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

History shows that churches that abandon these historic standards almost always slide from a God-centered theology of sovereign grace (monergism) to a human-centered theology of cooperation (synergism, moralism, and decisionism). Without the sharp definitions of the Belgic Confession and the Canons of Dort, the pulpit gradually ceases to preach the total depravity of man and the sovereign work of God, replacing it with self-help advice, moral therapeutic deism, and emotional manipulation.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

历史表明，抛弃这些历史标准的教会几乎无一例外地从以上帝为主权的恩典神学（单方动工说）滑向以人为中心的合作神学（神人合作说、道德主义和决志主义）。失去了《比利时信条》和《多特信经》的清晰界定，讲台将逐渐不再宣讲人的完全败坏与上帝的主权工作，取而代之的是成功学建议、道德性的治疗型自然神论和情绪操控。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. Spiritual Isolation from the Communion of Saints
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 割裂与圣徒相通的历史纽带
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To abandon these standards is to separate the church from the historical communion of saints. It is an act of chronological snobbery, assuming that our modern age has nothing to learn from the giants of the past who suffered and died to preserve the gospel. An unconfessional church is an isolated church, cut off from the deep roots that feed the universal tree of God's people.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

抛弃这些标准意味着将教会与历史上圣徒相通的纽带割裂开来。这是一种时间上的虚荣，认为我们现代人不需要从那些为了保守福音而受苦甚至殉道的历史巨擘身上学习任何东西。一个不宣誓信条的教会是一个孤立的教会，它切断了滋养上帝子民这棵大公之树的深根。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Beloved, while I was making every effort to write to you about our common salvation, I felt the necessity to write to you appealing that you contend earnestly for the faith which was once for all handed down to the saints."
— Jude 1:3
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“亲爱的弟兄啊，我想尽心写信给你们，论我们同得救恩的时候，就不得不写信劝你们，要为从前一次交付圣徒的真道竭力地争辩。”
—— 犹大书 1:3
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. Conclusion
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、结语
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Three Forms of Unity are a precious heritage, a gift of God's providence to protect and nourish His flock. They provide the modern church with theological clarity, pastoral comfort, and a shield against the errors of our age.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《联合三信条》是极其珍贵的产业，是上帝主权眷顾中赐予的礼物，旨在保护和牧养祂的羊群。它们为现代教会提供了神学上的清晰性、牧养上的安慰，以及抵御我们这个时代种种谬误的盾牌。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Any church that desires to remain faithful to Christ, grounded in the scriptures, and united with the cloud of witnesses throughout history will find in these three documents an indispensable guide and anchor.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

任何渴望对基督保持信实、扎根于圣经、并与历史上的圣徒见证人云彩联结的教会，都将在这三份文献中找到不可或缺的指南与信仰之锚。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
