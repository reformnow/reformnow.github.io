## 1. Introduction
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Bedrock of Faith
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 信仰的基石
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The doctrine of the Holy Trinity is not an optional theological puzzle or a late philosophical invention. It is the very heartbeat of historic Christian faith and biblical truth. From the opening pages of Genesis to the closing doxologies of Revelation, Holy Scripture reveals one God who exists eternally as three distinct Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. To compromise this truth is to dismantle the Christian understanding of God, revelation, and salvation.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

圣三位一体的教义并非可有可无的神学谜题，亦非晚近的哲学发明。它是历史基督教信仰与圣经真理的根本脉搏。从创世记的开篇到启示录的颂赞，圣经启示了一位从无始之始便永恒存在于三个独特位格中的上帝：圣父、圣子与圣灵。妥协这一真理，就是拆毁基督徒对上帝、启示以及救恩的全部理解。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Defining Error of Modalism
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 形态论的定义性错误
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Among the many errors that arose in the early centuries of the church, none has proved more persistent than **Modalism** (also known as Modalistic Monarchianism or Sabellianism). Modalism is the belief that the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit are not three distinct Persons, but rather three temporary modes, masks, or roles assumed by a single divine Person. In this view, God is a solitary monad who wears different masks at different times in history to interact with humanity.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在教会早期世纪兴起的诸多谬误中，没有哪一个比**形态论**（亦称形态君王论或萨贝利主义）更为顽固。形态论主张，圣父、圣子与圣灵并非三个独特的位格，而是一个独一的上帝位格在不同时期所取用的三种临时形态、面具或角色。在这种观点下，上帝是一个孤立的单子（Monad），在历史的不同阶段戴上不同的面具来与人类互动。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"And the Catholic Faith is this: That we worship one God in Trinity, and Trinity in Unity; Neither confounding the Persons: nor dividing the Substance."
— The Athanasian Creed
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“大公教会信仰即是：我们敬拜一体三位，三位一体的上帝；既不混淆位格，也不分裂神性。”
—— 《亚他那修信经》
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 2. The Historical Origins of Modalism
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、形态论的历史起源
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Noetus and Praxeas: The Late Second Century
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 诺图与普拉克西亚：二世纪晚期
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Modalism first emerged as a distinct heresy in the late second century through the teachings of Noetus of Smyrna and Praxeas. Driven by a desire to defend the strict unity (monarchy) of God against what they falsely perceived as tritheism, they argued that the Father Himself was born, suffered, and died as the Son. This logical conclusion was labeled **Patripassianism** (from the Latin *pater* meaning father, and *passio* meaning suffering).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

形态论最初作为一种独特的异端，于二世纪晚期通过士每拿的诺图（Noetus of Smyrna）与普拉克西亚（Praxeas）的教导而产生。他们出于捍卫上帝绝对单一性（君王论）的强烈动机，去对抗他们误以为是“三神论”的正统教义，进而主张天父自己降生、受苦并作为圣子而死。这一逻辑结论被冠以**圣父受苦说**（Patripassianism，源自拉丁语 *pater* 即父亲，与 *passio* 即受苦）的恶名。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The early church apologist Tertullian fiercely confronted Praxeas in Rome. In his famous treatise *Against Praxeas*, Tertullian exposed the spiritual devastation of this teaching with sharp irony:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

早期教会辩护士特土良在罗马猛烈迎击普拉克西亚。在他著名的著作《驳普拉克西亚》中，特土良用锋利的讽刺揭露了这一教导在灵性上的毁灭性：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Praxeas did a double service for the devil at Rome: he drove away prophecy, and he brought in heresy; he put to flight the Paraclete, and he crucified the Father."
— Tertullian, *Against Praxeas*
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“普拉克西亚在罗马为魔鬼做成了双重的差役：他驱逐了预言，引进了异端；他赶走了保惠师，钉死了圣父。”
—— 特土良，《驳普拉克西亚》
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Sabellius: The Successive Modes of God
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 萨贝利：上帝的相继形态
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the early third century, a priest named Sabellius refined this doctrine in Rome, giving it its most systematic form. Sabellius argued that God is one individual entity (which he called the *Huiopator*, or "Son-Father") who reveals Himself in three successive operations. In the Old Testament, God revealed Himself in the mode of the **Father** (as Creator and Lawgiver). In the Gospels, God revealed Himself in the mode of the **Son** (as Redeemer). Finally, after Christ's ascension, God revealed Himself in the mode of the **Holy Spirit** (as Sanctifier and Sustainer).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在三世纪初期，一位名叫萨贝利（Sabellius）的祭司在罗马改进了这一教义，赋予其最系统的形式。萨贝利主张，上帝是一个单一的实体（他称之为 *Huiopator*，即“子父”），在三个相继的历史阶段中启示自己。在旧约中，上帝以**圣父**的形态显现（作为创造者与立法者）；在福音书中，上帝以**圣子**的形态显现（作为救赎者）；最后，在基督升天之后，上帝以**圣灵**的形态显现（作为成圣者与保守者）。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Under this system, the three names do not represent eternal realities within the divine nature, but merely chronological manifestations. Consequently, the historic church condemned Sabellianism at the Council of Rome in 262 AD and later at the Council of Constantinople in 381 AD, declaring it a subversion of biblical truth.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在此体系下，这三个名字并不代表神性本质中永恒的真实，而仅是时间线上的历史表象。因此，大公教会在公元 262 年的罗马会议以及随后的公元 381 年君士坦丁堡公会议上谴责了萨贝利主义，宣告其是对圣经真理的颠覆。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. The Theological Errors of Modalism
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、形态论的神学错误
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Denial of God's Eternal Love and Relational Nature
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 否定上帝永恒的爱与位格际关系
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Scripture declares that "God is love" (1 John 4:8). For God to be eternally love in His very essence—prior to and independent of creation—there must exist an eternal relationship of love. If God were a solitary monad, He could not have loved prior to creating the universe, because love requires an object. The biblical truth of the Trinity reveals that the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit have dwelt in perfect, personal communion of love for all eternity.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

圣经宣告“上帝就是爱”（约翰一书 4:8）。如果上帝在祂的本质中永恒是爱——在创造世界之前且独立于受造物——那就必须存在一个永恒的爱之关系。如果上帝是一个孤立的单子，那么在创造宇宙之前祂就无法实践爱，因为爱需要对象。三位一体的圣经真理启示我们，圣父、圣子与圣灵在永恒中便居住在完美、人称位格际的爱之契合中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Jesus highlights this eternal, relational reality in His high priestly prayer:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

耶稣在祂的大祭司祷告中凸显了这一永恒的位格际实在：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Father, I desire that they also, whom You have given Me, be with Me where I am, so that they may see My glory which You have given Me, for You loved Me before the foundation of the world."
— John 17:24
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“父啊，我在哪里，愿你赐给我的人也同我在那里，叫他们看见你所赐给我的荣耀；因为创立世界以前，你已经爱我了。”
—— 约翰福音 17:24
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Modalism destroys this reality. By reducing the Persons to mere temporary roles, Modalism turns God's relational love into a historical performance. It robs God of His eternal relational nature, reducing Him to a lonely entity who requires creation in order to express love or relate to another.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

形态论摧毁了这一实在。通过将位格降格为临时的角色，形态论将上帝位格间的爱变成了历史性的表演。它剥夺了上帝永恒的位格际本质，将其降格为一个孤立的个体，甚至必须依赖受造物才能表达爱或与他者建立联系。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Making Divine Self-Revelation a Deceptive Illusion
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 使上帝的自我启示成为虚假的幻影
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If Modalism is true, then the historical events recorded in the Gospels become a theatrical display or a puppet show where God plays different parts simultaneously. The most vivid demonstration of this occurs at the baptism of Jesus:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

如果形态论是真的，那么福音书中所记录的历史事件就成了一场戏剧表演或木偶戏，上帝在其中同时扮演着不同的角色。这一现象最生动的体现是在耶稣受洗的场景中：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"After being baptized, Jesus came up immediately from the water; and behold, the heavens were opened, and he saw the Spirit of God descending as a dove and lighting on Him, and behold, a voice out of the heavens said, 'This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well-pleased.'"
— Matthew 3:16-17
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“耶稣受了洗，随即从水里上来。天忽然为他开了，他就看见神的灵仿佛鸽子降下，落在他身上。从天上有声音说：‘这是我的爱子，我所喜悦的。’”
—— 马太福音 3:16-17
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In this historical event, we see the Son in the water, the Holy Spirit descending as a dove, and the voice of the Father speaking from heaven. Under a modalist framework, this event is a deception. There was no real Father speaking to a real Son, nor a real Spirit descending. Rather, it was a single person projecting a voice while simultaneously manifesting as a man and a dove. This undermines the truthfulness and integrity of God's revelation.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在这一历史事件中，我们看到圣子在水中，圣灵如鸽子般降下，而圣父的声音从天上传来。在形态论的框架下，这幕场景就成了一种欺骗。并没有真实的圣父对真实的圣子说话，也没有真实的圣灵降下。相反，只是一个单一的位格在发出声音的同时，又投影为一个人和一只鸽子。这损害了上帝启示的真实性与诚实性。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Collapse of the Gospel of Redemption
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 救赎福音的彻底崩塌
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The most devastating consequence of Modalism lies in its subversion of biblical redemption. According to the apostolic testimony, salvation is a relational work: the Father sends the Son to be a propitiation for our sins (1 John 4:10), the Son offers Himself to the Father through the Holy Spirit (Hebrews 9:14), and the Son acts as our Advocate before the Father (1 John 2:1).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

形态论最致命的后果在于它对圣经救赎论的颠覆。根据使徒的见证，救恩是一项位格际的工作：圣父差遣圣子为我们的罪作了挽回祭（约翰一书 4:10），圣子藉着永远的灵将自己献给圣父（希伯来书 9:14），并且圣子在圣父面前作我们的中保（约翰一书 2:1）。
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

If Father and Son are not distinct Persons, the entire structure of substitutionary atonement collapses. Jesus' agonizing prayer in Gethsemane ("Not My will, but Yours be done") becomes a bizarre exercise of one divine mind talking to itself and pretending to submit to its own will. On the cross, the Son did not offer Himself to satisfy the justice of the Father; instead, God simply engaged in a monologue. Without distinct Persons, there can be no mediator between God and man, because a mediator, by definition, must stand between two parties.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

如果圣父与圣子不是两个独特的位格，代替性代赎的整个架构就会坍塌。耶稣在客西马尼园那痛苦的祷告（“不要成就我的意思，只要成就你的意思”）就成了一个古怪的独角戏——一个独一的神圣心智在对自己说话，并假装顺服自己的意志。在十字架上，圣子并未献上自己以满足圣父的公义；相反，上帝只是在进行一场独白。如果没有独特的位格，上帝与人之间就绝不可能有中保，因为根据定义，中保必须立于两方之间。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"For there is one God, and one mediator also between God and mankind, the man Christ Jesus."
— 1 Timothy 2:5
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“因为只有一位神，在神和人中间，只有一位中保，乃是降世为人的基督耶稣。”
—— 提摩太前书 2:5
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. Contemporary Modalist Churches
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、当代的形态论教会
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Resurgence of Modalism in the Oneness Movement
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 独一神格运动中形态论的死灰复燃
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

While Sabellius was condemned in antiquity, his theological assumptions did not die. In the twentieth century, Modalism found its most prominent modern manifestation in the **Oneness Pentecostal** movement (also known as the "Jesus Only" movement). Emerging from the early Assemblies of God in the 1910s, Oneness theology explicitly rejected the historic orthodox doctrine of the Trinity in favor of a modern form of Modalism.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

尽管萨贝利在古代被定罪，但他的神学假设并没有消亡。二十世纪，形态论在**独一神格五旬节派**（Oneness Pentecostal，亦称“唯独耶稣”运动）中找到了最显著的现代温床。该派别于 1910 年代脱胎于早期的五旬节圣洁会体系，显式地拒绝了历史正统的大公三位一体教义，转而拥护一种现代形式的形态论。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Oneness theology argues that "Father," "Son," and "Holy Spirit" are not distinct Persons, but merely titles of the one God, whose personal name is "Jesus." According to their doctrine, Jesus' divinity is the Father, and His humanity is the Son. Consequently, they reject the biblical Trinitarian baptismal formula of Matthew 28:19 and insist that baptism must be performed "in the name of Jesus only" for salvation.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

独一神格神学主张，“圣父”、“圣子”与“圣灵”并非不同的位格，而仅是独一上帝的头衔，而这位上帝的个人名字是“耶稣”。根据他们的教义，耶稣的神性就是圣父，耶稣的人性就是圣子。因此，他们拒绝马太福音 28:19 中圣经所指示的三位一体洗礼公式，坚称洗礼必须“唯独奉耶稣的名”进行，方能得救。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Let's read their official doctrine regarding the Godhead to understand their position directly:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

让我们直接查看他们关于神性本源的官方教义，以了解其具体立场：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

> The one true God has revealed Himself as Father; in His Son, Jesus Christ; and as the Holy Spirit... Jesus Christ is the one true God manifested in the flesh. The Father was manifested in the Son, and the Holy Spirit is the Spirit of Christ.
> (Representative statement from Oneness Pentecostal articles of faith)
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

> 独一真神已启示自己为圣父；在祂的儿子耶稣基督里启示自己；并启示为圣灵……耶稣基督是独一真神在肉身显现。圣父在圣子中显现，而圣灵即是基督的灵。
> （独一神格五旬节派信仰告白的典型陈述）
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Major Contemporary Oneness Denominations
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 当代主要的独一神格教派
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Several active denominations and organizations today actively promote this modern form of Modalism:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

今天有数个活跃的教派与组织在积极推广这种现代形式的形态论：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

- **United Pentecostal Church International (UPCI)**: Established in 1945, the UPCI is the largest and most influential Oneness Pentecostal organization in the world, claiming millions of members globally.
- **Pentecostal Assemblies of the World (PAW)**: One of the oldest Pentecostal bodies, which adopted a Oneness position early in the twentieth century.
- **Assemblies of the Lord Jesus Christ (ALJC)**: A smaller but active association of churches adhering to Oneness theology and "Jesus Only" baptism.
- **Apostolic and Jesus-Only Congregations**: Many independent local churches use terms like "Apostolic Temple," "Apostolic Faith," or "Apostolic Church" in their names, reflecting Oneness theology rather than historic Christian orthodoxy.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

- **联合五旬节会 (United Pentecostal Church International, UPCI)**：成立于 1945 年，是当今世界上规模最大、最具影响力的独一神格五旬节派组织，在全球拥有数百万信徒。
- **世界五旬节会 (Pentecostal Assemblies of the World, PAW)**：最古老的五旬节派机构之一，在二十世纪初期便采纳了独一神格立场。
- **主耶稣基督教会联盟 (Assemblies of the Lord Jesus Christ, ALJC)**：规模较小但十分活跃的教会联盟，坚守独一神格神学与“唯独耶稣”洗礼。
- **使徒性与“唯独耶稣”独立会众**：许多独立的当地教会，常在名字中带用“使徒殿”（Apostolic Temple）、“使徒信仰”（Apostolic Faith）或“使徒教会”（Apostolic Church）等词，反映的是独一神格神学而非历史性大公教会正统。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Influence of Prominent Preachers
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 知名讲员的潜在影响
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Beyond official denominations, Modalist assumptions sometimes permeate broader evangelical culture through popular preachers. A prominent example is T.D. Jakes, the founder of The Potter's House. Jakes was ordained in a Oneness Pentecostal denomination (the PAW) and historically held Oneness views. While he has made public statements in recent decades indicating a shift toward orthodox Trinitarian language, his past writings and associations have contributed to a widespread confusion regarding the personal distinctions within the Trinity.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

除了官方教派之外，形态论的假设有时也会通过流行讲员渗透到更广泛的福音派文化中。一个突出的例子是“陶匠之家”（The Potter's House）的创始人 T.D. 杰克斯（T.D. Jakes）。杰克斯是在独一神格五旬节派教派（世界五旬节会，PAW）中被按立的，并且在历史上持有独一神格观点。尽管他在近几十年发表过公开声明，表明其语言向正统三位一体表述靠拢，但他过去的著作与背景仍在客观上促成了大众对三位一体位格区别的广泛混淆。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. Conclusion
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、结语
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Eternal Reality of the Triune God
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 三位一体上帝的永恒实在
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The historic Christian faith rejects the shifting masks of Modalism. God does not manifest Himself in temporary modes, nor does He trick His creatures with theatrical illusions of Father, Son, and Spirit speaking to one another. He is, in His eternal and unchangeable nature, the Triune God: three distinct Persons who co-exist in perfect unity of substance.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

历史基督教信仰坚决拒绝形态论那不断变换的面具。上帝不会在临时的形态中变化莫测，也绝不用圣父、圣子与圣灵彼此对话的戏剧幻影来愚弄祂的受造物。在祂永恒且不改变的本质中，祂就是三位一体的上帝：三个独特的位格，共同存在于完美的神性本质统一之中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. A Solid Hope
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 坚固的指望
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This truth is not a speculative philosophy but our only solid hope. In the cross of Christ, we do not witness a lonely monad engaging in a divine monologue. We behold the Son offering Himself as a spotless sacrifice to the Father through the eternal Spirit. Because the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are distinct Persons, our mediator is real, our justification is secure, and the eternal love that flows from the heart of the Triune God is true.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这一真理不是思辨哲学，而是我们唯一的坚固指望。在基督的十字架上，我们见证的不是一个孤立的单子在进行神圣的独白。我们注视着圣子藉着永远的灵，将自己无瑕无疵地献给圣父。因为圣父、圣子与圣灵是不同的位格，我们的中保是真实的，我们的称义是稳固的，从三位一体上帝心怀中流淌出来的永恒之爱也是真实无伪的。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
