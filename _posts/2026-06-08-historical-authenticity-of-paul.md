## 1. Introduction
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Anchor of Apostolic Witness
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 使徒见证的锚点
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Throughout the long development of church history and theological thought, the perception of the "historical authenticity" of the Apostle Paul has undergone a profound evolution. It moved from a self-evident foundation of faith in the pre-modern era, through intense waves of rationalist skepticism during the Enlightenment and the era of higher criticism, to a strong, multi-dimensional validation by modern historical-critical scholarship, textual analysis, and archaeological discoveries. Today, Paul stands as one of the most historically documented and secured figures of the ancient world.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在教会历史与神学思想的发展长河中，对使徒保罗（Apostle Paul）“历史真实性”的看待，经历了一个深刻的演变过程。这一过程从前现代时期那被视为理所当然的信仰基石，走过了启蒙运动与高等批判时代理性主义的猛烈质疑，最终在现代历史批判学术、文本分析与考古发现的多维印证下得到了强力确证。今天，保罗已被公认为古代世界中历史文献记录最清晰、历史轮廓最确凿的人物之一。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Theological and Historical Interlocking
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 神学与历史的交织
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

For the Christian church, the historical reality of Paul is not merely a matter of academic curiosity. Because the Christian faith is rooted in historical revelation rather than abstract mythology, the historical existence of the apostles, their letters, and their witness to the resurrected Christ forms the essential bridge between the history of redemption and the faith of the church. A mythologized Paul would inevitably lead to a mythologized gospel, rendering the core doctrine of justification by faith a groundless speculation.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

对于基督教会而言，保罗的历史真实性绝非仅仅是一个学术兴趣问题。因为基督信仰植根于历史性的启示，而非抽象的神话，所以使徒的真实存在、他们的书信以及他们对复活基督的见证，构成了救赎历史与教会信仰之间必不可少的桥梁。一个神话化的保罗势必导致一个神话化的福音，从而使“因信称义”这一核心教义沦为毫无根基的空中楼阁。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 2. The Early Church and the Middle Ages: Absolute Reality as Faith and Sacred Tradition (1st - 16th Centuries)
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、早期教会与中世纪：作为信仰与圣传的绝对真实（1世纪 - 16世纪）
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Witness of Contemporary and Subsequent Generations
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 同代与紧随其后的历史见证
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the long centuries preceding the rise of modern criticism, the church never doubted the historical existence of Paul as a real, living person. For the early church fathers and the medieval church, Paul’s historical reality was the very premise of the church's existence and theological legacy. His life and martyrdom were attested by those who knew him or lived immediately after him.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在现代批判主义兴起之前的漫长世纪中，教会从未怀疑过保罗作为一个真实、活生生的历史人物的存在。对于早期教父和中世纪教会来说，保罗的历史真实性是教会存在和神学遗产的前提。他的生命与殉道得到了那些认识他或紧随其后之人直接而明确的见证。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The apostolic fathers and early apologists frequently referred to Paul and his epistles as authoritative, historically grounded documents. For instance, Clement of Rome, writing in his first letter to the Corinthians around 95 AD, explicitly described Paul's endurance, his preaching to the ends of the Roman world, and his eventual martyrdom under the imperial authorities:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

使徒教父与早期辩护士频繁引用保罗及其书信，将其视为权威且具历史根基的文献。例如，罗马的克雷芒（Clement of Rome）在约公元 95 年写给哥林多教会的《克雷芒一书》中，便明确描述了保罗的坚忍、他向着罗马世界尽头的宣教旅程，以及他在帝国权力面前最终的殉道：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Through envy and jealousy, Paul showed the way to the prize of patient endurance... Having taught righteousness to the whole world, and having reached the farthest limits of the West, he bore his testimony before the rulers."
— Clement of Rome, *1 Clement*
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“因着嫉妒与纷争，保罗展示了忍耐到底的冠冕……他向整个世界传扬了公义，足迹直达西方的最远边界，在统治者面前作了见证。”
—— 罗马的克雷芒，《克雷芒一书》
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Similarly, Ignatius of Antioch (c. 110 AD) and Polycarp of Smyrna (c. 120 AD) wrote of Paul not as a theological abstraction, but as a towering historical apostle whose letters were treasured by the congregations they addressed.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

同样，安提阿的启克那丢（Ignatius of Antioch，约公元 110 年）与士每拿的坡旅甲（Polycarp，约公元 120 年）在论及保罗时，也绝非将他视为神学抽象，而是将其尊为一位令人敬畏的历史使徒，其书信被其所致的会众视为至宝。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Early Formation of the Canon
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 正典的早期确立与流传
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The historical footprint of Paul is also preserved in the early process of canonization. Even heterodox figures in the second century provided indirect evidence of Paul's historical prominence. Marcion of Sinope (c. 140 AD), despite his severe theological errors, compiled the first closed list of Christian scriptures (the *Apostolikon*), which centered on ten epistles of Paul. This collection demonstrates that by the early second century, Paul’s letters were already universally recognized as historical, apostolic writings circulated widely throughout the Mediterranean.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

保罗的历史足迹同样保存在正典确立的早期历程中。即便是在二世纪的异端人物，也为保罗的历史显赫地位提供了间接的证据。锡诺普的马吉安（Marcion of Sinope，约公元 140 年）虽然犯下了严重的神学谬误，但他编纂了历史上第一个封闭的基督教正典雏形（《使徒书集》），其中便以保罗的十本书信为核心。这一合集反向证明了在二世纪初，保罗的书信已经作为核心的历史性使徒文献，在环地中海的各地教会中广泛流传。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. Pilgrimage, Martyrdom, and Sacred Relics
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 朝圣、殉道遗址与圣徒遗骨
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Throughout the Middle Ages, the historical reality of Paul was physically anchored in the geography of Christian devotion. The church in Rome maintained a continuous tradition regarding Paul’s martyrdom and burial site at the Basilica of St. Paul Outside the Walls. Pilgrims traveled from all corners of Christendom to venerate his tomb, and the accounts of his missionary journeys in the Book of Acts were integrated into the sacred liturgy and tradition of the church, treated as absolute, unquestionable historical facts.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在整个中世纪，保罗的历史真实性在基督徒敬拜的地理分布中得到了有形的锚定。罗马教会一直保持着关于保罗殉道及安葬地点的连续传统，即如今的城外圣保罗大殿。来自基督教世界各个角落的朝圣者前往他的墓地表达敬意，而《使徒行传》中关于他宣教旅程的记载也被融入到大公教会的圣传与神圣礼仪之中，被视为无可置疑的历史事实。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. The Enlightenment and Higher Criticism: The Peak of Rationalist Skepticism (18th - 19th Centuries)
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、启蒙运动与高等批判：理性主义的怀疑巅峰（18世纪 - 19世纪）
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Challenge of the Tübingen School
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 图宾根学派的批判性挑战
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

With the dawn of the Enlightenment and the development of the "historical-critical method" in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, scholars began to examine the Bible with the same naturalistic assumptions applied to secular literature. During this period, the historical reliability of the New Testament records was subjected to unprecedented scrutiny, and the Pauline corpus was placed under the academic microscope.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

随着十八与十九世纪启蒙运动的兴起以及“历史批判方法”的发展，学者们开始以审视世俗文献的自然主义假设来审视圣经。在此期间，新约圣经记载的历史可靠性遭遇了空前的审视，而保罗书信集更是被置于学术显微镜之下。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The most influential challenge came from the Tübingen School in Germany, led by Ferdinand Christian Baur. Applying Hegelian dialectics—thesis, antithesis, and synthesis—to early church history, Baur reconstructed a narrative of profound conflict in the apostolic era. He argued that the early church was divided into two hostile camps: a Jewish Christian faction led by Peter and a Gentile Christian faction led by Paul.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

其中最具影响力的挑战来自德国的图宾根学派，其领袖为费迪南·巴尔。巴尔将黑格尔的辩证法——正题、反题与合题——应用于早期教会历史，重构了使徒时代内部存在剧烈冲突的历史叙事。他认为，早期教会分裂为两个互相对立的阵营：以彼得为首的犹太基督教派与以保罗为首的外邦基督教派。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To support this theory of conflict, Baur and his followers subjected the Pauline epistles to radical skepticism. While Baur did not deny the historical existence of Paul, he rejected the authenticity of almost the entire Pauline corpus. He accepted only four letters as genuinely written by Paul (Romans, 1 and 2 Corinthians, and Galatians), which he termed the *Hauptbriefe*. The remaining letters, along with the Book of Acts, were dismissed as second-century pseudonymous works written to reconcile the Petrine and Pauline factions.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

为了支持这种冲突假说，巴尔及其追随者对保罗书信施加了极其激进的怀疑。尽管巴尔并未否定保罗的历史存在，但他几乎否定了整部保罗书信集的真实性。他仅承认四部书信为保罗本人所写（罗马书、哥林多前后书、加拉太书），称之为“核心书信”（*Hauptbriefe*）。而其余书信以及《使徒行传》，则被斥为二世纪后人为了调和彼得派与保罗派的矛盾而编造的“伪作”与虚构历史。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Extreme Skepticism of Radical Critics
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 极端批判学者的彻底怀疑论
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

As the nineteenth century progressed, the skepticism of the historical-critical method was pushed to its logical extreme. A minority of radical critics, most notably Bruno Bauer and the Dutch Radical School, went far beyond Baur. Bruno Bauer argued that early Christianity was a product of Greco-Roman philosophy and syncretism, and that Paul was a completely fictional literary character created by second-century writers to give a narrative voice to Gentile Christian theology.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

随着十九世纪的发展，历史批判方法的怀疑论被推向了逻辑的极端。少数激进的批判学者，其中以布鲁诺·鲍尔及荷兰极端学派为代表，走得比巴尔还要远。布鲁诺·鲍尔主张早期基督教完全是希腊罗马哲学与混合宗教的产物，而保罗则是一个彻底虚构的文学形象，是由二世纪的作家为了给外邦基督教神学提供话语载体而创造出来的。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

During this phase, critical scholarship sought to decouple the historical Jesus and the historical Paul from the theology of the church, reducing the biblical narratives to pious myths. Yet, this peak of skepticism laid the groundwork for a counter-movement of rigorous scientific investigation that would ultimately dismantle these radical claims.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在这一阶段，批判性学术试图将历史耶稣与历史保罗同教会的神学割裂开来，将圣经叙事贬低为虔诚的神话。然而，这种怀疑论的巅峰也为后来的严谨科学调查奠定了基础，并最终瓦解了这些激进的声称。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. The 20th Century to the Present: Comprehensive Confirmation by Archaeology and Textual Science
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、20世纪至今：考古学与文本科学的“全面确证”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Archaeological Discoveries Aligning with the Biblical Narrative
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 考古发现与圣经叙事的完美吻合
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the twentieth and twenty-first centuries, the tide turned decisively. A series of remarkable archaeological discoveries provided concrete, material evidence that directly matched the historical, social, and political details recorded in the Pauline epistles and the Book of Acts.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在二十与二十一世纪，学术风向发生了决定性的转变。一系列引人注目的考古发现提供了具体的实物证据，直接印证了保罗书信与《使徒行传》中所记录的历史、社会及政治细节。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

One of the most significant discoveries is the Gallio Inscription, unearthed in Greece. Written by Emperor Claudius, the inscription mentions Gallio, the proconsul of Achaia. This text has allowed historians to date Gallio's term of office precisely to 51–52 AD. This matches the biblical account of Paul’s trial in Corinth:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

其中最重要的发现之一是希腊德尔斐出土的迦流碑文。这块由罗马皇帝克劳狄撰写的碑文提到亚该亚方伯迦流。该文献使历史学家能够将迦流的任期精确锁定在公元 51-52 年。这与圣经中关于保罗在哥林多受审的记载完美吻合：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"But while Gallio was proconsul of Achaia, the Jews with one accord rose up against Paul and brought him before the judgment seat,"
— Acts 18:12
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“到迦流作亚该亚方伯的时候，犹太人同心起来攻击保罗，拉他到审判台前，”
—— 使徒行传 18:12
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Gallio Inscription serves as an absolute chronological anchor, pinning Paul’s missionary journeys to a precise historical year.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

迦流碑文成为了判定保罗历史年表（Chronology）的关键“锚点”，将保罗的宣教旅程牢牢固定在了具体的历史年份中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Another discovery is the Erastus Inscription, found during excavations in Corinth in 1929. Archaeologists uncovered a Roman paved plaza with a Latin inscription stating that "Erastus, in return for his aedileship, laid the pavement at his own expense." This Erastus, a city official, corresponds directly to the individual mentioned by Paul in his letter written from Corinth:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

另一项发现是 1929 年在哥林多遗址发掘中出土的以拉都铺道碑文。考古学家发现了一处铺有拉丁碑文的罗马广场，上面刻着“以拉都为报答其市政官职，自费铺设了此路面”。这位担任城市官员的以拉都，直接对应了保罗在哥林多写信时提到的同名人物：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Gaius, host to me and to the whole church, greets you. Erastus, the city treasurer, greets you, and Quartus, the brother."
— Romans 16:23
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“那接待我、也接待全教会的该犹问你们安。城内管银库的以拉都和兄弟括土问你们安。”
—— 罗马书 16:23
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

These archaeological ties show that the authors of the New Testament were intimately familiar with the real municipal officials and political structures of first-century Roman cities, a level of detail impossible for a later second-century fabricator to achieve.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这些考古关联表明，新约圣经的作者对公元一世纪罗马城市的真实市政官员和政治结构了如指掌。这种细节的精确度，是二世纪晚期的虚构者根本无法伪造的。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Textual Consensus and the Undisputed Epistles
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 文本共识与“无争议书信”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In contemporary biblical scholarship, there is a broad and stable consensus that transcends theological differences. Even the most skeptical, secular, and non-believing historians agree that at least seven of the Pauline epistles are genuine, first-generation documents written by Paul himself between 50 and 60 AD. These are known as the Undisputed Epistles (Romans, 1 and 2 Corinthians, Galatians, Philippians, 1 Thessalonians, and Philemon).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在当代的圣经学术界中，存在着一个超越神学立场的广泛而稳定的学术共识。即便极度自由派、世俗主义或无神论的历史学家也一致同意，新约圣经中至少有七封保罗书信是保罗本人在公元 50 至 60 年代写下的第一代文献。这些书信被称为“无争议书信”（Undisputed Epistles，包括罗马书、哥林多前后书、加拉太书、腓立比书、帖撒罗尼迦前书和腓利门书）。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

These seven letters represent some of the earliest surviving documents of the Christian movement, written within two to three decades of Christ's crucifixion—predating the composition of the four Gospels. Through these texts, the historical personality of Paul—his training under Gamaliel, his Pharisaic background, his deep grasp of Roman citizenship, his physical afflictions, and his passionate pastoral heart—is revealed with immense historical clarity.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这七封书信构成了基督徒运动现存最古老的一手文献，写于基督受死复活后的二至三十年内，其成书时间甚至早于四卷福音书。通过这些文本，一个有血有肉的历史保罗——他在迦玛列门下受训的背景、法利赛人的出身、对罗马公民身份的娴熟运用、肉体上的软弱挣扎以及那充满激情的牧者心肠——都清晰地跃然纸上。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. The Systematic Theological Perspective: The Divine Purpose in History
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、大公教会的系统神学视角：历史背后的神圣护理
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Historicity of Redemption
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 救恩的历史性
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Within the framework of historic Christian faith and orthodox theology, the historical reality of Paul is understood not as an accident of history, but as an expression of divine providence. Historic Christian theology insists that salvation is not a timeless myth or a gnostic allegory, but a series of historical events. Just as the incarnation, death, and resurrection of Christ occurred in real space and time under Pontius Pilate, so too the calling of the apostles and the dissemination of the gospel were historically concrete events.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在大公教会正统神学的框架中，保罗的历史真实性不被视为历史的偶然，而被理解为神圣护理（Providence）的具体彰显。历史基督教信仰坚信，救赎并非超脱时间的神话或诺斯底式的寓言，而是一系列确凿的历史事件。正如基督的降生、受死与复活是在本丢·彼拉多手下具体的时间与空间中发生的一样，使徒的呼召与福音的传扬也是历史性的实体。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If Paul were a literary fiction, the theological truths he articulated—most notably the doctrine of justification by faith alone (*sola fide*)—would lose their foundation. In orthodox theology, the historical reliability of the messenger is inextricably linked to the truth of the message. The historical transformation of Paul on the road to Damascus is a tangible testament to the supernatural power of the risen Lord invading human history.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

如果保罗是一个文学虚构，他所系统阐述的神学真理——尤其是“唯独因信称义”（*sola fide*）的教义——便会失去其历史根基。在正统神学中，信使的历史真实性与信意（Message）的真实性是不可分割的。保罗在大马士革道路上的真实历史转变，正是复活之主以超自然大能介入人类历史的有形见证。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Divine Providence in the Instrument
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 器皿预备中的神圣预旨与护理
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Furthermore, historic theology sees the specific background of the historical Paul as a manifestation of the divine wisdom. God prepared a unique vessel: a man who possessed Jewish rabbinic training, Greco-Roman cultural literacy, and Roman citizenship.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

此外，正统神学将历史保罗的具体背景视为上帝智慧的完美彰显。上帝特意预备了一个独特的器皿：一个同时兼具严紧犹太拉比训练、深厚希腊罗马文化素养以及罗马公民身份的人。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This convergence of historical factors enabled Paul to act as the ideal instrument for the expansion of the gospel to the Gentile world. His rabbinic background allowed him to trace the continuity of the covenant from the Old Testament; his Greek education enabled him to engage the philosophers at the Areopagus; and his Roman citizenship protected him legally, allowing him to carry the gospel to the heart of the Roman Empire. This historical preparation is celebrated as a manifestation of God's sovereign oversight over the second causes of history.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这种历史因素的汇聚，使保罗成为将福音拓展至外邦世界的理想器皿。他的拉比背景使他能准确阐明旧约应许的连续性；他的希腊化教育使他能在亚略巴古与哲学家辩论；而他的罗马公民身份则在法律上保护了他，使他得以将福音带到罗马帝国的核心。这一历史性的预备，被历史基督教信仰颂赞为上帝主权对历史第二因完美护理的彰显。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 6. Conclusion
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 六、结语
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. From Saint to Secured History
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 从圣徒圣传到确凿历史
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Over the course of church history, the figure of Paul has transitioned through several phases of perception. In the pre-modern era, he was viewed primarily through the eyes of faith and liturgical tradition. In the nineteenth century, he was subjected to the severe, dissecting lens of rationalist skepticism. Today, however, Paul is recognized by secular and religious scholars alike as one of the most historically secure and richly documented figures of antiquity.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在教会历史 Genes 演变中，保罗形象在人们眼中的定位经历了几重跨越。在前现代时期，人们主要透过信仰与礼仪传统的视角来看待他；在十九世纪，他经历了理性主义怀疑论那冰冷而严苛的审视；然而在今天，无论是世俗学者还是信仰学者，都公认保罗是古代历史中轮廓最清晰、文献记录最确凿的人物之一。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. A Living Testimony
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 活生生的历史见证
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This scientific and textual validation serves to confirm what the church has confessed for two millennia: that the Christian gospel is not a collection of cleverly devised myths, but is grounded in the real, gritty realities of human history. The life of Paul—marked by suffering, tireless devotion, and a radical transformation—remains a historic monument to the power of the gospel of Jesus Christ.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这种学术与文本上的双重确证，印证了教会两千年来所宣告的真理：基督的福音绝非捏造的乖巧神话，而是深植于人类真实的历史泥土之中。保罗那充满磨难、坚韧不拔且经历根本性翻转的生命，至今仍是耶稣基督福音大能最伟大的历史丰碑。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
