## 1. Introduction
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Crucible of Skepticism
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 怀疑论的熔炉
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the fields of church history and New Testament archaeology, the debate surrounding the mention of \"King Aretas\" in the Pauline epistles represents a classic battlefield between higher criticism and biblical truth. For decades, rationalist skeptics pointed to this passage as a historical error that supposedly undermined the authenticity of 2 Corinthians and the historical existence of the Apostle Paul himself.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在教会历史与新约圣经考古学领域，关于保罗书信中提到“亚略达王（King Aretas）”的争论，是高等批判与圣经真理交锋的一个经典战场。几十年来，理性主义怀疑论者一直将这段经文视为一处明显的历史错误，并试图以此来推翻《哥林多后书》的可靠性以及使徒保罗本人的历史真实性。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. A Challenge Turned into Confirmation
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 质疑向确证的转化
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

However, through the progress of twentieth-century historical research and archaeological discoveries, what was once confidently labeled a biblical \"weak link\" has become one of the most powerful external confirmations of the Pauline narrative. By examining the shifting borders and complex geopolitics of the Roman East, historians have resolved the apparent contradiction, establishing an ironclad link between the text of Scripture and the realities of first-century history.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

然而，随着二十世纪历史研究与考古学的进展，这一曾被断言为圣经“软肋”的细节，反而成为了保罗叙事最强有力的外部确证之一。通过深入剖析罗马帝国东部边疆的边界变动与复杂的国际地缘政治，历史学家们完美消解了这一看似矛盾的冲突，并在圣经文本与公元一世纪的真实历史实在之间，建立了一条无可辩驳的坚固纽带。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

---

## 2. The Core of the Skeptic's Challenge
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、怀疑者质疑的核心
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Damascus Escape
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 大马士革的逃亡记述
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In his second letter to the Corinthians, Paul defends his apostleship by listing his numerous sufferings, concluding with a vivid description of his early escape from Damascus:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在《哥林多后书》中，保罗为了捍卫其使徒权柄而陈明了他所经历的诸般患难，并在结尾部分生动地记述了自己早年从大马士革脱险的经历：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"In Damascus the ethnarch under Aretas the king was guarding the city of the Damascenes in order to seize me, and I was let down in a basket through a window in the wall, and so escaped his hands."
— 2 Corinthians 11:32-33
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“在大马士革，亚略达王手下的提督把守大马士革城，要捉拿我，我就从窗户里，在筐子里，从城墙上被人缒下去，脱离了他的手。”
—— 哥林多后书 11:32-33
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Apparent Historical Anachronism
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 表面上的历史失实
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Skeptics asserted that this passage contains a fatal historical error. Historical records outside the Bible identify Aretas IV (who reigned from c. 9 BC to 40 AD) as the ruler of the Nabataean Kingdom, a sovereign Arab nation centered in the famous desert city of Petra (modern-day Jordan). Damascus, however, was a major city in the Roman province of Syria, under the direct authority of the Roman governor.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

怀疑论者断言这段经文包含了一个致命的历史硬伤。根据圣经之外的历史记载，亚略达四世（Aretas IV，约公元前 9 年至公元 40 年在位）是拿巴天王国（Nabataean Kingdom）的国王，这是一个以著名沙漠古城佩特拉（今约旦境内）为首都的阿拉伯主权国家。然而，大马士革却是罗马帝国叙利亚行省的重要城市，直接隶属于罗马总督的管辖。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The critics asked a seemingly unanswerable question: how could a Nabataean king appoint an \"ethnarch\" (governor or local official) to control gates inside a premier Roman provincial city? To them, this was clear evidence of a later fabricator making a blunder about ancient geography and politics, proving that the historical Paul was a literary invention who never wrote these words.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

批判者们提出了一个看似无法逾越的问题：一个拿巴天国王怎么可能在罗马帝国的省会直辖重镇设立“提督”并把守城门？在他们看来，这显然是后世伪造者在古代地理与政治细节上犯下的低级错误，从而“证明”了历史上的保罗只是一个文学虚构，从未亲手写下这些话。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. Geopolitical Realities: Resolving the Contradiction
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、地缘政治的真实还原：消解矛盾的钥匙
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Dynastic Marriage and Border Conflict
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 政治联姻与边防危机
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To resolve this issue, historians look to the turbulent dynastic politics of the Roman East in the late 30s AD. Aretas IV had married his daughter to Herod Antipas, the tetrarch of Galilee (infamous for executing John the Baptist). However, Antipas later divorced Aretas’s daughter to marry Herodias, the wife of his half-brother.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

为了消解这一矛盾，历史学家将目光投向了公元一世纪三十年代晚期罗马东部边疆动荡的王朝政治。亚略达四世曾将其女儿嫁给加利利分封王希律·安提帕（即杀害施洗约翰的希律）。然而，安提帕后来悍然休了亚略达的女儿，转而迎娶其同父异母兄弟的妻子希罗底。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This severe diplomatic insult led to open war. In 36 AD, Aretas’s army completely destroyed the forces of Herod Antipas. Antipas appealed to Rome, and Emperor Tiberius, furious at the unauthorized conflict, ordered Vitellius, the governor of Syria, to march against Aretas and bring him back dead or alive.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这一严重的国际外交羞辱最终引爆了公开的战争。公元 36 年，亚略达的军队全歼了希律·安提帕的防线。安提帕随即向罗马皇帝提比略求援，提比略对这场未经授权的边境冲突大为光火，当即命令叙利亚总督维特留斯出兵讨伐亚略达，誓要将其生擒或击杀。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Imperial Transition and the Damascus Power Vacuum
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 皇帝更迭与大马士革的“主权真空”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Just as Vitellius was mobilizing the Roman legions, a sudden shift in Roman leadership occurred. In March of 37 AD, Emperor Tiberius died. He was succeeded by Caligula, who completely reversed Rome\'s eastern policy. Caligula despised Herod Antipas (eventually banishing him) and preferred to utilize client kings to secure the empire’s borders, reducing direct Roman administrative costs.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

然而，正当维特留斯集结罗马军团准备进军时，罗马最高权力中心发生了一次突变。公元 37 年 3 月，皇帝提比略驾崩，卡利古拉继位。这位新皇帝彻底推翻了前任的东部政策。卡利古拉极度厌恶希律·安提帕（并最终将其流放），他更倾向于通过扶持傀儡外藩国王来稳固帝国边防，以降低罗马的直接行政成本。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Prominent historians of the Roman Empire, such as A. H. M. Jones, have concluded that during this transition (between 37 and 40 AD), Caligula transferred administrative control of Damascus to Aretas IV. This act served as a diplomatic concession to appease the Nabataean king and secure the eastern desert border against nomadic raids, explaining how a Nabataean ethnarch could be stationed in Damascus during this precise window.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

著名的罗马帝国历史学家（如 A. H. M. 琼斯）指出，正是在这一权力过渡期内（公元 37 年至 40 年之间），卡利古拉皇帝为了安抚拿巴天国王，并巩固罗马东部沙漠边疆免受游牧部落袭扰，将大马士革的管辖权让渡给了亚略达四世。这一外交妥协完美解释了为什么亚略达王的提督会在此特定的时间窗口内驻扎在大马士革。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Numismatic Evidence
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 古钱币学的无声铁证
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Archaeology has provided remarkable material verification of this political transfer through the study of ancient coins minted in Damascus.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

考古学通过对大马士革铸造的古代硬币进行研究，为这一政治主权的易手提供了极其震撼的实物佐证。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

During the reigns of Augustus and Tiberius, the Damascus mint regularly produced bronze coins bearing the portrait of the reigning Roman emperor. This practice resumed under the reign of Nero (from 54 AD onward). However, archaeologists have documented a conspicuous \"gap\" in imperial Roman coinage in Damascus starting around 34 AD and extending through the reign of Caligula (37–41 AD) and Claudius, with no Roman imperial coins minted during this entire window.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在皇帝奥古斯丁与提比略统治时期，大马士革造币厂定期铸造带有在位罗马皇帝头像的铜币。这一惯例在尼禄皇帝统治时期（公元 54 年起）得以恢复。然而，考古学家证实，大马士革的古罗马帝国钱币出现了一个显著的“断代真空”——它大约始于公元 34 年，一直延伸至卡利古拉（公元 37-41 年）和克劳狄统治时期。在此期间，大马士革没有铸造过任何带有罗马皇帝头像的钱币。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This absence of imperial coinage strongly indicates that Damascus was not under direct Roman administration during these years, having been temporarily transferred to local, non-Roman control. The historical timeframe fits the reign of Aretas IV and Caligula\'s border reorganization perfectly, providing archaeological validation of the biblical text.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

帝国钱币的缺失强有力地表明，大马士革在这些年份里并未处于罗马的直接行政管辖之下，而是临时落入了非罗马本土势力的控制。这一历史时间线与亚略达四世的统治以及卡利古拉的边疆调整完美重合，为圣经文本提供了确凿的考古学支持。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. Significance for the Historical Authenticity of Paul
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、对确证保罗历史真实性的重大意义
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. An Absolute Chronological Anchor
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 确立无可动摇的历史绝对锚点
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The resolution of the Aretas controversy yields a major historical breakthrough. Because Aretas IV died in 40 AD, and the Nabataean control of Damascus could only have begun after Caligula’s accession in 37 AD, Paul’s escape from Damascus is locked into a narrow historical window: between 37 and 40 AD.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

亚略达王争议的消解，为新约历史学带来了一个重大的突破。因为亚略达四世于公元 40 年去世，而拿巴天王国对大马士革的控制只能始于卡利古拉公元 37 年登基之后，这便将保罗从大马士革逃亡的历史事件死死锁定在了一个极窄的历史窗口中：即公元 37 年至 40 年之间。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This window provides the earliest and most precise chronological anchor for the chronology of the Apostle Paul. It pins the start of his missionary career directly to secular Roman history, offering a solid foundation upon which the timeline of early Christianity can be reconstructed.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这一窗口为使徒保罗的历史年表提供了最早、最精确的绝对历史锚点。它将保罗宣教生涯的起点直接固定在了世俗罗马历史的客观坐标上，为重构早期基督教的发展时间线奠定了无比坚实的基础。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Validation of the Epistle's Firsthand Authenticity
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 反证书信第一手文献的真实性
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Furthermore, this detail serves as a powerful defense of the authenticity of 2 Corinthians. The geopolitical shift of Damascus temporarily falling under Nabataean control was an extremely brief, obscure, and localized border event. A second-century fabricator or novelist, writing decades later, would have had no access to such specialized historical data and would have assumed Damascus was always under Roman control (as it was before 37 AD and after 54 AD).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

此外，这一细节也为《哥林多后书》的真实性提供了无可置疑的反证。大马士革主权短暂易手拿巴天王国这一地缘政治变动，是一个极其短暂、冷门且局限于边疆的微观事件。若书信是由二世纪的伪造者在数十年后编写的，他根本不可能获知如此冷僻的历史细节，而只会理所当然地认为大马士革一直处于罗马直接管辖下（正如公元 37 年之前和公元 54 年之后那样）。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Paul’s passing, casual mention of \"the ethnarch under Aretas the king\" reflects the natural style of a firsthand witness who lived through the event. It is a detail that could only have been written by the historical Paul, recounting his actual escape in a basket down the stone walls of Damascus.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

保罗在书信中极其自然、随手提及的“亚略达王手下的提督”，完全显明了这出自一位亲历大逃亡的第一手目击者的口述笔迹。这种看似与宏观历史冲突、实则与微观历史严丝合缝的细节，只能是由历史上的保罗本人所写。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. Conclusion
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、结语
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. From Conflict to Consilience
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 从冲突到契合的跨越
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The case of King Aretas demonstrates the value of historical and archaeological research in confirming biblical truth. Many alleged \"contradictions\" in Scripture are simply the result of modern ignorance regarding the micro-political dynamics of the ancient world. When the soil of archaeology is turned and the full historical picture is brought to light, the integrity of the biblical text is consistently vindicated.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

亚略达王这一经典案例，清晰展示了历史与考古学研究在确证圣经真理方面的独特价值。许多被指控的所谓“圣经矛盾”，往往仅仅是因为现代人对古代世界微观政治动态的无知。当考古的泥土被掘开、完整的历史画卷被展开时，圣经文本的真实与严谨一次又一次地得到了无可辩驳的洗雪。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Pressed in the Soil of History
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 印刻在历史泥土中的足迹
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Ultimately, the historical basket lowered from the walls of Damascus did not just deliver the apostle from his enemies; it delivered a permanent historical anchor to the church. The footnote of Roman border politics has left a firm legal imprint confirming the historic footsteps of the Apostle Paul, testifying to the reality of the gospel he proclaimed.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

归根结底，那只从大马士革城墙上缒下的竹筐，不仅在当时救出了遭遇围捕的使徒，也为后世教会留下了稳固的历史锚点。这段罗马边防政治的微小插曲，在古代历史的泥土中留下了无法抹杀的手印，见证了使徒保罗的历史真实足迹，也见证了他所传扬的真实福音。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
