## 1. Introduction
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Enigma of the Cynocephalic Saint
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 狗头圣徒的魔幻之谜
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Within the vast tapestry of church history and iconography, few figures are as visually shocking or theological puzzling as the "dog-headed saint." In the ancient icons of the Eastern church—particularly in Greek and Russian Orthodox traditions—Saint Christopher (often called Saint Reprebus or Hristofor) is frequently depicted not with a human countenance, but as a towering Roman warrior bearing the head of a dog.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在大公教会与东正教历史的浩瀚画卷中，极少有哪个形象像“狗头人身圣徒”那样在视觉上令人震撼，又在神学上引人深思。在东方教会（尤其是希腊和俄罗斯东正教传统）的古老圣像画中，圣克里斯多福（在东方常被称为圣华多罗）频繁地被描绘成一个身穿罗马军装、脖子上却长着一颗狗头的惊人形象。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Fact, Fiction, and Faith
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 事实、虚构与信仰的交织
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This striking depiction is not the product of modern fantasy or a pagan intrusion into the church. Rather, it represents a fascinating historical phenomenon where ancient geographical misunderstandings, translation errors, and profound salvation-historical metaphors converge. By analyzing this legend through historical, linguistic, and theological lenses, we can uncover the valuable spiritual truths hidden behind this bizarre iconography.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这一惊人的描绘并非现代奇幻文学的臆造，亦非异教思想对教会的野蛮入侵。相反，它代表了一个极具魅力的历史文化现象，交织了古代地理学误解、语言学翻译笔误以及深刻的救恩历史隐喻。通过从历史、语言和神学的多维视角来剖析这一公案，我们可以揭示隐藏在这个奇特视觉符号背后的宝贵属灵真理。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 2. Etymology and Historical Context: The Myth of the Cynocephali
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、词源与历史背景：“狗头人”的地理幻想
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Ancient Geographical Misunderstandings
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 古代科学局限下的地理误解
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The term "sinospholi" or "cynocephali" derives from the ancient Greek word *Cynocephali* (κυνοκέφαλοι), which literally translates to "dog-headed ones." In the Greco-Roman world, before the advent of modern geography, writers like Pliny the Elder and Herodotus populated the unexplored edges of the earth—such as India, Libya, and the deep wilderness of Scythia—with mythical races of beastly humanoids.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在语源上，“Cynocephali”（κυνοκέφαλοι）字面意思即“狗头人身种族”。在古希腊和古罗马时期，由于缺乏现代地理学和实际考察手段，像大普林尼（Pliny the Elder）与希罗多德（Herodotus）这样的古代学者，经常在游记中将那些遥远未知的蛮荒边缘地带（如印度、利比亚或黑海深处）虚构为居住着半人半兽的奇特物种。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

These legendary peoples were described as having the heads of dogs, barking to communicate, wearing animal skins, and eating raw meat. They were the ultimate symbol of the untamed, uncivilized, and non-human world.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这些传说中的族群被描述为长着狗的头部、靠吠叫进行交流、身披兽皮并以生肉为食。在古代文化中，他们代表了未驯服、未开化且远离人性的蛮荒世界。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. A Symbol for the Barbarian Tribes
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 蛮族部落的妖魔化代称
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Modern historians suggest that the myth of the Cynocephali was likely a Roman caricature of hostile, heavily-bearded barbarian tribes living on the imperial frontiers. Because these tribes spoke languages incomprehensible to Romans (sounding like barking) and practiced religions that may have involved canine totems, they were demonized in the popular imagination as literal monsters.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

现代历史学家指出，所谓“狗头人身”的说法，在当时极可能是罗马人对帝国边疆那些满脸胡须、手段残暴的敌对野蛮人部落（Barbaric Tribes）的夸张与妖魔化称呼。由于这些部落的语言在罗马人听来犹如犬吠，且崇拜某种犬类图腾，因而在民间的猎奇想象中，他们被直接具象化成了生理上的怪物。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. How Christopher Became the Dog-Headed Saint
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、圣克里斯多福是如何变成“狗头圣徒”的？
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Canaanite vs. Caninite Mistranslation
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 迦南人与犬类战士的翻译笔误
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

One of the most widely accepted linguistic explanations for the origin of the dog-headed saint is a translation error that occurred during the Latin transmission of early Greek hagiographies.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

关于“狗头圣徒”起源的语言学解释中，最广为接受的观点是早期希腊文圣徒传记在拉丁文抄写和翻译过程中的一次“翻译乌龙”。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

According to ancient texts, the martyr was originally described as a fierce warrior who came from the region of Canaan—a **Canaanite** (Greek: *Chananaios*). During the transcription process, later Latin scribes likely misread or misspelled the word as **Caninite** (derived from the Latin *canis*, meaning dog-like or canine). Over centuries of medieval folklore, this simple copyist error was taken literally by iconographers, turning the giant Canaanite soldier into a literal dog-headed beast.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

根据古代文献，这位殉道战士原本被记录为来自迦南地区（Canaanite，希腊文作 *Chananaios*）。然而，在后世拉丁文抄写员的传抄过程中，此词被误读或笔误拼写成了“Caninite”（源自拉丁语 *canis*，意为“犬科的、像狗一样的”）。经过中世纪民间传说的层层放大，这一简单的文字笔误被圣像画师们付诸笔端，最终将这位魁梧的迦南战士直接画成了长着狗头的形象。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Eastern Legend: From Cannibal Monster to Apostle
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 东方圣传：从食人怪物到基督使徒
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the official hagiography of the Eastern Orthodox church, rooted in the 5th-century Syrian text *Acts of Reprebus*, the dog-head is not explained away as a mistake, but embraced as a powerful testament to redemption.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

然而，在东正教官方圣传（源自西元5世纪的叙利亚语文本《华多罗行传》）中，狗头形象并未被解释为笔误，而是被赋予了极具救赎张力的传奇生命。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

According to this tradition, Christopher was originally named **Reprebus** (meaning "reprobate" or "the castaway"). He belonged to the wild, cannibalistic tribe of the Cynocephali. He possessed terrifying strength and could only communicate in wild grunts. During the reign of Emperor Decius (c. 249–251 AD), Reprebus was captured by Roman legions and drafted into a military unit.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在这一传统中，该圣徒原名叫做**勒普雷布斯（Reprebus，意为“恶棍”或“被弃绝者”）**，属于野蛮吃人的狗头人部族。他身材无比魁梧，面相凶恶，只能发出野兽般的吠叫。在罗马皇帝德西乌斯（Decius，约公元 249–251 年在位）执政期间，勒普雷布斯被罗马军队俘虏，并被编入了军营。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the camp, the Holy Spirit supernaturally touched his tongue, granting him human speech and reason. His first words were a prayer of praise to the true God. Converted by Christian soldiers, he was baptized by a bishop, receiving the name **Christopher** ("Christ-bearer").
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在军营里，圣灵超自然地抚摸了他的舌头，赐予他人类的言语和清醒的理性。他开口说出的第一句话便是对真神的赞美。在基督徒士兵的感召下，他接受了洗礼，并改名为**克里斯多福（Christopher，字面意为“背负基督之人”）**。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Christopher went on to perform bold miracles, including a wooden staff that budded and bore fruit, which led to the conversion of two hundred soldiers sent to arrest him. Later, when Decius sent two courtesans, Aquilina and Nicea, to seduce him in prison, Christopher's holy character converted them instead. After surviving fire, boiling iron chairs, and arrows, he was ultimately beheaded in 251 AD, offering his life as a faithful martyr.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

得救后的克里斯多福展现了非凡的宣教热情。他曾行了木质手杖生根开花的神迹，令前去逮捕他的两百名士兵当场悔改。当皇帝派两个美艳的妓女（阿奎莉娜与尼西亚）进牢房试图诱惑他时，他反而用纯正的真理使她们痛哭悔改。在历经烈火、通红铁椅及乱箭伤害而毫发无损后，他最终于公元 251 年慷慨殉道。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Theological Iconography of Redemption
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 圣像背后的神学隐喻
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The retention of the dog-headed motif in Eastern iconography served a profound theological purpose: it was a graphic illustration of the scope of God's redemptive grace. By depicting the saint as a redeemed Cynocephalus, the church visually proclaimed that no human being—no matter how savage, beastly, or culturally alienated—is beyond the reach of Christ's cleansing blood. It is a visual representation of how sovereign grace can transform a howling monster into a holy son of God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

东方教会长期保留“狗头圣徒”的画风，其核心目的并非宣扬怪力乱神，而是为了传达一个宏大的神学真理：连最野蛮、最污秽、在世人看来犹如禽兽般的“狗头人（蛮族）”，一旦被基督的宝血买赎并重生，都能成为圣洁的使者。这用最极端的视觉反差，宣告了上帝主权恩典跨越一切族群、文化与自然限制的拯救大能。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. The Western Tradition: The Giant Who Carried Christ
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、西方教会的传统：背负基督的巨人
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Offerus the Seeker of the Strongest King
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 寻找至高君王的巨人奥弗鲁斯
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In Western Christian tradition, particularly as recorded in the medieval compilation *The Golden Legend*, the dog-head element was omitted. Instead, the focus shifted to a beautiful, allegorical narrative of a giant seeking the most powerful king.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在西方基督教传统中（以中世纪著名的圣徒传记《黄金传说》为代表），狗头的魔幻元素被完全过滤，取而代之的是一段广为流传的“巨人寻找至高君王”的美丽寓言。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The giant, named **Offerus**, possessed immense physical strength and resolved to serve only the greatest monarch in the world. He first served a mighty king, but left when he noticed the king crossed himself in fear whenever the name of the Devil was mentioned. Offerus then sought out the Devil, but abandoned him when he saw the Devil tremble and flee at the sight of a roadside cross. Offerus realized that Jesus Christ, the King of the Cross, was the only sovereign Lord of all.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这位巨人原名叫做**奥弗鲁斯（Offerus）**，他拥有无双的体魄，并立志只服事世上最强大、最强盛的君王。他起初投奔了一位国王，但发现国王因害怕魔鬼而在听到其名字时画十字；他转而投靠魔鬼，却发现魔鬼在路旁的十字架前吓得面色苍白、疯狂绕道。奥弗鲁斯由此明白，只有在十字架上得胜的耶稣基督，才是这世上至高无上的君王。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Service at the Raging River
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 湍急河畔的顺服服事
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

An old hermit instructed Offerus on how to serve this unseen King. "Since you have great strength," the hermit said, "go live by the nearby river, which is swift and dangerous, causing many travelers to drown. Carry the poor and weak across on your shoulders. In doing this, you will serve the Lord Jesus."
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

一位隐修士指引他去寻找这位看不见的君王：“基督给了你无双的力量，你应当用这份力量去服事祂。你去住在附近那条水流湍急、常淹死行人的深河边，用你的肩膀把那些过河的弱者背过去。”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Offerus built a hut by the river, used a giant palm branch as a staff, and spent his days carrying travelers across the turbulent waters out of love for Christ.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

奥弗鲁斯遵命前往，在河边搭起茅屋，手握一根巨大的棕树枝当作手杖，日复一日、风雨无阻地将所有过河的旅人背在自己宽阔的肩膀上，不收分文。他将这种枯燥、劳累的体力劳动，化作自己对基督的顺服与礼拜。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Weight of the World
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 惊心动魄的黑夜与世界的重量
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

One stormy night, a small child called out to Offerus, begging to be carried across. The giant placed the boy on his shoulders and stepped into the rising river.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在一个暴风雨交加的深夜，一个几岁的小男孩在岸边呼唤着他的名字。奥弗鲁斯抱起孩子，安置在自己宽厚的肩膀上，迈步走进了波涛汹涌的河水中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

As he waded deeper, the child became heavier and heavier, weighing down on the giant like a mountain of stone. The waves crashed around them, and the burden was so immense that Offerus feared they would both drown. Straining with all his might, he pressed his staff into the riverbed and staggered to the opposite bank.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

然而，当他走到河中央时，肩膀上的孩子突然变得重如泰山。奥弗鲁斯感觉自己仿佛背着一座沉重的花岗岩山岳，双腿颤抖，河水几乎漫过脖子。他一生从未承受过如此恐怖的重量，但他咬紧牙关，拼尽最后一丝气力，终于踉跄着走到了对岸。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 4. The Revelation of the Christ-Bearer
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 4. 显明的启示：背负基督之人
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Placing the child down, Offerus gasped, "You have put me in great danger. You weighed so heavy that had I carried the whole world on my shoulders, it could not have been heavier!"
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

将小男孩放下来后，奥弗鲁斯疲惫地叹道：“孩子，你刚才差点让我丢了性命。你为什么这么重？我觉得我刚才仿佛把整个世界都背在肩膀上了！”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The child shone with a brilliant divine light and replied:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这时，那个小男孩突然散发出耀眼的神圣光芒，回答道：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Marvel not, Christopher, for thou hast not only borne all the world upon thy shoulders, but thou hast borne Him that created and made all the world, upon thy shoulders."
— *The Golden Legend*
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“克里斯多福，不要惊奇。你刚才不仅把整个世界背在肩上，你更把那位创造这个世界的君王，背在你的肩膀上了。”
—— 《黄金传说》
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The child revealed Himself as Jesus Christ, carrying the sins of the world. To prove His identity, He told Christopher to plant his staff in the earth; the next morning, it had grown into a fruit-bearing palm tree. From that day, the giant took the name **Christopher** ("Christ-bearer") and dedicated his life to martyrdom.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

孩子表明自己就是耶稣基督，而那难以承受的重量正是祂在十字架上为全人类所背负的罪恶之重。孩子令其将手杖插在地上，次日一早，枯木手杖果然发芽开花，变成了一棵结满果实的棕榈树。奥弗鲁斯从此正式改名叫**克里斯多福（Christopher，背负基督之人）**，并最终在坚守信仰中英勇殉道。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. Ecclesiastical Correction: The Suppression of the Dog-Headed Icon
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、教会历史对“狗头圣徒”的纠偏与禁令
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Synod of Moscow (1667)
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 莫斯科大公会议的禁止
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

As theological education progressed and the church confronted rationalist critiques, church leadership recognized that the literal "dog-headed" icons could foster superstition among the uneducated and invite ridicule from critics.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

随着教会神学的发展以及面对理性主义的审视，教会高层意识到，“狗头人身”的魔幻形象不仅容易在未受教育的平信徒中引发迷信，也容易成为非信徒嘲讽的对象。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In 1667, the Russian Orthodox Church convened the famous **Synod of Moscow**. The council formally decreed that depicting Saint Christopher with a dog's head was "contrary to reason and holy history." The church officially banned the creation of new cynocephalic icons and ordered existing ones to be painted over, replacing the dog heads with normal human faces. Despite the ban, some traditional icons survived in remote Old Believer communities and monasteries in Greece and Russia.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在公元 1667 年，俄罗斯东正教会召开了著名的**莫斯科大公会议（Synod of Moscow）**。会议正式判定：将圣克里斯多福画成狗头人身是“违反理性与神圣历史事实的”。教会下令正式禁止绘制新的狗头圣像，并要求将现存圣像中的狗头涂改、修正为正常的人类面孔。尽管如此，在俄罗斯的一些古仪教派社群以及希腊的一些古老修道院中，仍有少数历史圣像得以保留至今。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Western Roman Catholic View
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 西方教会的历史淡化与纪念
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the West, Roman Catholicism had filtered out the bizarre Cynocephali folklore much earlier, focusing exclusively on the narrative of the giant child-carrier. Though the historical details of Christopher's life were deemed too legendary to remain on the universal Roman calendar during the reforms of 1969, he remains widely venerated as a historical martyr and the traditional patron saint of travelers and motorists.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在西方，罗马天主教会很早就过滤掉了“狗头人身”的野蛮传说，转而完全聚焦于“巨人背负基督”的灵修叙事。尽管在 1969 年的天主教历法改革中，由于其生平细节的传说色彩过浓而被移出了普世瞻礼单，但他仍被广泛尊为一位真实的历史殉道者，并作为旅行者和驾驶员的主保圣徒在民间饱受敬仰。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 6. Theological Reflection: A Mirror of Sovereign Grace
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 六、系统神学与历史基督教信仰视角下的“狗头圣徒”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Guarding Against the Mythologizing of Truth
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 警惕真理的“神话化”与迷信
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

For today's Christians who hold to biblical truth and reject folklore, the history of the "dog-headed saint" serves as a warning against the mythologizing of faith. History and science confirm that there was never a biological race of dog-headed humans. The legend was born of translation errors and ancient geographical ignorance. The church must base its apologetics and historical witness on verifiable truth, not on pagan mythology or linguistic accidents.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

对于今天持守圣经真理、抗拒民间迷信的基督徒来说，“狗头人身圣徒”的历史是一面明镜。历史与科学证明，世上从未真正存在过狗头人身的生理族群，这纯粹是古代科学不发达时的地理幻想与翻译笔误拼凑出的民间巧合。这警示我们，绝不能将神怪传说当作严肃的启示历史去宣扬，信仰必须立足于真实、可考的真理之上。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Visual Representation of Total Depravity
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 全然败坏与不可抗拒恩典的视觉具象化
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

However, when viewed as a theological allegory, the story of the dog-headed monster redeemed by Christ holds deep spiritual resonance. In the light of historic Christian doctrine, every fallen sinner—dead in their trespasses and sins—is spiritually no different from a wild, barking beast, enslaved by animal passions and hostility toward God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

然而，若从大公教会正统神学的属灵隐喻来看，这个故事却提供了一幅极其深刻的画面。在圣经真理的审视下，每一个未被圣灵重生的罪人，在道德与灵魂的“全然败坏（Total Depravity）”上，与那狂吠、吃生肉、茹毛饮血的“狗头人身怪兽”并无本质区别。我们都曾是死在过犯中、顺从肉体私欲的属灵怪物。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

It is only through the sovereign, irresistible grace of God that we are transformed. Just as Reprebus’s tongue was touched by the Spirit to speak human praise, it is God who initiates regeneration, giving us a new heart, human speech to praise Him, and a new name written in the Lamb's Book of Life.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

正如勒普雷布斯的舌头被圣灵触碰后方能吐露理性的赞美，我们灵魂的苏醒也完全是由上帝主权、不可抗拒的恩典（Irresistible Grace）所开启的。是祂单方面施行了重生的善工，剥离了我们灵魂中的兽性，洗净了我们的罪，并赐给我们信心去拥抱基督。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Grandeur of the Sanctified Life
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 世俗天职中的圣洁生活
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Whether we look at the Eastern martyr who budded a wooden staff or the Western giant who carried travelers across a swollen river, the lesson is clear: true saving faith manifests in sacrificial service. Offerus did not find Christ through monastic isolation, but by using his physical strength to carry the weak across a dangerous river. This highlights a central biblical truth: that any ordinary, mundane vocation—when done out of love for God and neighbor—is a holy act of worship.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

无论是在火中歌颂神的东方殉道者，还是在激流中背负行人的西方巨人，他们的生命都印证了一个真理：真正的信心必然结出成圣与舍己的果子。奥弗鲁斯没有通过超凡的苦修来遇见基督，而是在“帮人渡河”这一极其平凡、低下、甚至肮脏的体力劳动中服事了主。这呼应了宗教改革时期马丁·路德所强调的“天职”观——只要是出于爱神与服事邻舍的心，鞋匠修鞋与牧师讲道在神面前同样具有圣洁的价值。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

As Scripture declares, God uses the foolish, weak, and despised things of this world to display His glory:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

正如圣经所宣示的，上帝常拣选世上愚拙的、软弱的与卑贱的，以彰显祂莫大的恩典与荣耀：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"but God has chosen the foolish things of the world to shame the wise, and God has chosen the weak things of the world to shame the things which are strong, and the base things of the world and the despised God has chosen, the things that are not, so that He may nullify the things that are,"
— 1 Corinthians 1:27-28
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“神却拣选了世上愚拙的，叫有智慧的羞愧；又拣选了世上软弱的，叫那强壮的羞愧。神也拣选了世上卑贱的，被人厌恶的，以及那无有的，为要废掉那有的，”
—— 哥林多前书 1:27-28
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The legend of the dog-headed saint stands as a vivid caricature of our own redemption. We, who were once outcasts and monsters in our sins, have been washed in the waters of baptism, made to bear the name of Christ, and called to cross the turbulent rivers of this life under the protective weight of the Savior who carries us.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

因此，狗头圣徒的奇特公案在历史中无意间成了一幅绝妙的救赎漫画。它用最荒诞的视觉反差见证了那份能让万物更新、让最污秽者成为圣洁的“唯独恩典”（*sola gratia*）。我们这群原本在罪中咆哮的怪物，如今都已在洗礼中洗净，被称为“背负基督之人”，在人生风暴的洪流中，被那背负了我们全部罪孽的救主安稳托住，渡往永恒的彼岸。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
