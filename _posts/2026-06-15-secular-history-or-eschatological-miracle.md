## 1. Introduction: The Modern Evangelical Paradigm
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言：现代福音派的释经范式
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

For many modern believers, the year 1948 stands as the most significant milestone in modern redemptive history. The re-establishment of the State of Israel, followed by the dramatic military victories of the 1967 Six-Day War, is frequently heralded by Evangelical pulpits—especially those influenced by Dispensationalism—as a modern theological miracle. Within this framework, modern secular Israel is directly equated with the covenant people of the Old Testament, and these geopolitical events are viewed as the "prophetic starting gun" for the rebuilding of the Third Temple, the secret rapture, and the battle of Armageddon.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

对于许多现代信徒而言，1948年被视为现代救赎历史上最重大的里程碑。随着以色列国的重新建立，以及随后在1967年六日战争中取得的戏剧性军事胜利，现代福音派的讲台——特别是受到时代论（Dispensationalism）深刻影响的宗派——频繁地将这些事件宣告为“现代最大的神学神迹”。在这种框架下，现代世俗的以色列国被直接等同于旧约圣经中上帝的立约选民，而这些地缘政治事件则被看作是重建第三圣殿、秘密被提以及哈米吉多顿大战的“末世发令枪”。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

However, when examined through the lens of the Historic Christian Faith and Orthodox Catholic Theology, a radically different understanding emerges. The ecumenical confessions and Reformation standards do not provide theological endorsement for the geopolitical actions of a secular state. Instead, historic theology evaluates these events through four rigorous covenantal and biblical dimensions.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

然而，当透过历史基督教信仰（Historic Christian Faith）与大公教会正统神学（Orthodox Catholic Theology）的透镜来审视时，我们会发现一个截然不同的理解。大公教会信经与宗教改革时期的正统信条，并不会为任何世俗国家的政治与军事胜利做神学背书。相反，历史正统神学通过四个严密的神学维度，对这一问题进行了审视。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

---

## 2. The Substance of the Covenant: The Church as the True Israel
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、圣约的实体：作为真以色列的教会
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

At the heart of the Historic Christian Faith is the reality of covenantal fulfillment. The old covenant types—the physical land of Canaan, the earthly city of Jerusalem, and the bloodline of Abraham—were shadows pointing to a grander spiritual reality. These shadows find their absolute and final fulfillment in the person and work of Jesus Christ and His body, the Church.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

历史基督教信仰的核心在于“圣约的成全”。旧约圣经中的预表与影子——属地的迦南地、肉身的耶路撒冷城以及亚伯拉罕的血统——本质上都是指向更宏大属灵实体的影子。当基督复活并建立新约之后，这些影子在基督里得到了完全且终极的成全。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Scripture never defines the citizens of the New Covenant by genealogical descent or geographic borders. Instead, the Apostle Paul declares with absolute clarity that all who belong to Christ are the true seed of Abraham and heirs of the promise:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

新约圣经从未将新约的选民定义为拥有“犹太人血统”或“居住在特定地理边界内”的人。相反，使徒保罗极其斩钉截铁地宣告，所有属乎基督的人，才是亚伯拉罕真正的后裔，是照着应许承受产业的选民：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"And if you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham's descendants, heirs according to promise."
— Galatians 3:29
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“你们既属乎基督，就是亚伯拉罕的后裔，是照着应许承受产业的了。”
—— 加拉太书 3:29
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Consequently, the Historic Christian Faith recognizes the Church—the unified body of elect Jews and Gentiles united by faith in Jesus Christ—as the only true, spiritual Israel on earth today. A secular state established in 1948 that rejects Jesus Christ and practices secular Judaism has no divine status as a holy theocracy. As the historic confessional standards declare, the true heirs of the promises are those who have been washed in the blood of the Lamb.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

因此，大公教会正统神学认定，教会（由一切因信称义的犹太人和外邦人所组成的属灵国度）才是今天地上唯一的、真正的、属灵的以色列。1948年建立的那个拒绝耶稣基督、信奉世俗犹太教的世俗国家，在神学法统上并不享有旧约神权选民国的特殊地位。正如历史信条所声明的，应许的真正承受者是那些在羔羊之血中洗净的人。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. Distinguishing God's Working: Providence vs. Redemptive Miracle
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、区分上帝的作工：普遍摄理与救赎神迹
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

How then do we explain the remarkable survival of the Jewish people and the military victories of the Six-Day War? Biblical Truth makes a clear distinction between God's general providence (His sovereign rule over history) and His special redemptive work (His salvific acts).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

那么，该如何解释犹太民族在历史中的奇迹生存以及六日战争中不可思议的军事胜利呢？圣经真理在上帝的普遍摄理（主宰历史发展的权力）与特殊救赎作工（指向拯救的神迹）之间作出了明确的区分。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

We affirm that God governs all historical events, from the rise and fall of the Roman Empire to the birth of modern nations. The establishment of Israel in 1948 and the victory of 1967 are indeed historical marvels under God’s sovereign control. However, they are works of general providence—comparable to the rise of the British Empire or the American Revolution—rather than sacred redemptive miracles.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

我们确信上帝主宰着人类历史的每一个细节，从罗马帝国的兴衰到现代国家的诞生。1948年以色列的建国以及1967年的战争胜利，确实是上帝在历史中主权摄理的结果。然而，这些事件属于历史普遍摄理的范畴——正如大英帝国的崛起或美国独立战争的胜利一样——而不应被赋予圣洁的“救赎历史神迹”之光环。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To equate modern military operations with the miraculous fall of Jericho is to conflate the secular with the holy. The soldiers of a modern secular nation, using advanced military hardware and geopolitical alliances, are not fighting a "holy war" under the direct command of the Lord of Hosts.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

将现代地缘政治军事冲突等同于旧约“约书亚攻打耶利哥”的神迹，是把世俗事务与圣洁历史混为一谈。一个现代世俗国家的士兵利用高超的军事战略、现代武器和国际博弈所取得的胜利，并不等同于旧约中万军之耶和华直接带领的“圣战”。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. The Priority of Conversion: Faith in Christ over Territory
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、救赎的前提：回转信靠基督而非占领土地
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Dispensational eschatology posits a "land-first, salvation-later" order, arguing that the physical return of ethnic Jews to Palestine is a prerequisite for their end-time conversion. The Historic Christian Faith rejects this focus on bloodline and geography, emphasizing that there is no covenantal blessing apart from union with Jesus Christ.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

时代论的末世论提出了“先回归土地，后得救”的顺序，认为肉身犹太人回归巴勒斯坦是其末世归信的前提。然而，历史基督教信仰拒绝这种只看血统和土地、忽视信仰的倾向，并强调在与耶稣基督联合之外，没有任何圣约的祝福。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Orthodox Catholic Theology has long nurtured a hope for the future salvation of ethnic Jews, praying for their spiritual awakening and turning to the Messiah. However, this awakening is not defined by geopolitical domination or building a physical temple. Rather, the true miracle is when Jewish people weep over the One they pierced, confess Jesus as Lord, and are grafted back into the single olive tree—the Church.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

大公教会正统神学一直对犹太民族未来的救赎抱有指望，并为他们能归向弥赛亚而迫切祷告。然而，这种属灵的觉醒绝非表现为在中东占领了多少土地或建造一座实体的第三圣殿。相反，真正的神迹是当犹太人痛哭流涕地承认他们当年钉死的耶稣就是弥赛亚，悔改信靠福音，并被接回到那棵唯一的橄榄树——教会之中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

As the Westminster Larger Catechism instructs, our prayer must be for their calling to the gospel:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

正如历史信条所教导的，我们应当为他们归信福音而祷告：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"We pray... that the gospel be propagated throughout the world, the Jews called, the fullness of the Gentiles brought in..."
— Westminster Larger Catechism, Q. 191
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“我们祷告……愿福音传遍天下，犹太人被召归主，外邦人的丰盛得以填满……”
—— 威斯敏斯特大要理问答，第191问
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. Ethical Discernment: Guarding Against Geopolitical Legalism
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、道德分辨：警惕地缘政治的律法主义
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

A major pitfall of dispensational thinking is "geopolitical legalism"—the blind theological endorsement of every action taken by the modern State of Israel, regardless of international law or human rights. This bias frequently causes believers to ignore the suffering of local populations, including Palestinian Christians.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

时代论思维的一个重大陷阱在于“地缘政治的律法主义”——即对现代以色列国所采取的每一项行动进行盲目的神学背书，而无视其是否符合国际法或基本公义。这种偏见往往导致信徒忽视中东当地平民的痛苦，甚至对巴勒斯坦的基督徒兄弟缺乏基本的关怀。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Historic Christian ethics require that justice be applied impartially. The Church's primary loyalty lies not with any earthly nation-state, but with the Kingdom of God and our brothers and sisters in Christ who share the one cup and one loaf, regardless of their ethnicity. Elevating a secular political entity to holy status is a regression into a form of physical legalism that contradicts the spiritual nature of Christ's Kingdom.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

历史基督教伦理要求不偏不倚地践行公义。教会的首要忠诚不属于地上的任何世俗政权，而属于上帝的国度，以及我们在基督里同饮一杯、同擘一饼的弟兄姊妹，无论其种族如何。将一个世俗的政治实体神圣化，本质上是退回到了某种肉体律法主义之中，违背了基督国度的属灵本质。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Jesus answered, 'My kingdom is not of this world. If My kingdom were of this world, then My servants would be fighting so that I would not be handed over to the Jews; but as it is, My kingdom is not of this world.'"
— John 18:36
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“耶稣回答说：‘我的国不属这世界；我的国若属这世界，我的臣仆必要争战，使我不至于被交给犹太人。只是我的国不属这世界。’”
—— 约翰福音 18:36
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## Conclusion: Elevating Our Vision to the New Jerusalem
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 结论：抬起目光，仰望新耶路撒冷
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

While many modern eschatological theories remain fixated on geopolitical conflicts and physical land in the Middle East, the Historic Christian Faith presents a far grander redemptive vision. The reign and sovereignty of God do not depend on modern tanks or geopolitical agreements.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

当许多现代末世论理论将目光紧紧盯着中东的飞弹、疆域和地缘政治冲突时，历史基督教信仰展现出了一幅远为宏大和永恒的救赎图景。上帝的王权与祂圣约的信实，从来不需要靠现代的武器或世俗的政治博弈来证明。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The events of 1948 and 1967 represent the unfolding of history under God's general providence, but they are not redemptive miracles. The only true Israel today is the Church, purchased by the blood of Christ. As believers, we do not place our hope in the dust of the earthly Jerusalem; instead, we look forward to the city that has foundations, whose designer and builder is God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

1948年建国与六日战争是上帝掌管历史之“普遍摄理”的结果，但它们绝非救赎历史的“圣经神迹”。今天地上唯一的真以色列，是那群在十字架下因信称义、被基督宝血买赎回来的教会。作为信徒，我们的盼望不在于地上耶路撒冷的尘土，而在于那座有根有基的城，就是上帝所建造和经营的。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"For he was looking for the city which has foundations, whose architect and builder is God."
— Hebrews 11:10
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“因为他等候那座有根有基的城，就是神所经营所建造的。”
—— 希伯来书 11:10
```
