## 1. Introduction: The Alleged Failure of the Covenant
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言：圣约“失败”的困惑
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

A profound and weighty question has historically stood as a "load-bearing wall" in orthodox theology. It stems from a perplexing passage in the Apostle Paul's letter to the Romans, quoting the prophet Isaiah:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在正统神学历史上，有一个极其深刻、被称为“神学承重墙”的重磅命题。这个命题源于使徒保罗在《罗马书》中引用先知以赛亚的一段令人困惑的经文：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Isaiah cries out concerning Israel, 'THOUGH THE NUMBER OF THE SONS OF ISRAEL MAY BE LIKE THE SAND OF THE SEA, ONLY THE REMNANT WILL BE SAVED;'"
— Romans 9:27 (NASB)
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“以赛亚指着以色列人喊着说：‘以色列人虽多如海沙，得救的不过是剩下的余数。’”
——罗马书 9:27（和合本）
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If all of Israel were chosen as God's covenant people, why does the final entrance into the heavenly kingdom belong only to a remnant? Has God's election failed, or did His decree of salvation somehow fall short? To answer this, historic Christian theology draws a clear biblical distinction between two dimensions of election: **outward covenantal election** and **inward salvific election**.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

如果以色列全体都是上帝所拣选的圣约子民，为什么最终进入天国的却仅仅是少数的“余数”？上帝的拣选难道会半途而废，或者祂的救赎计划最终失败了吗？为了回答这个问题，大公教会正统神学与圣约神学（Covenant Theology）根据圣经做出了清晰的区分，指明了两种不同维度的“拣选”：**外在的圣约拣选**（Outward Covenantal Election）与**内在的救恩拣选**（Inward Salvific Election）。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

---

## 2. Outward Covenantal Election: The Historical Instrument
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、外在的圣约拣选：历史中的救赎工具
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The first dimension of election is historical and collective. It is the action of God in choosing the physical descendants of Abraham, the nation of Israel, out of all the nations of the earth.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

第一种维度的拣选是历史性与集体性的。这是指上帝在历史中，将亚伯拉罕肉身血统的后裔（以色列民族）从万国中挑选出来，归祂自己。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The primary purpose of this election was not to guarantee automatic salvation for every citizen, but to establish a historical instrument of redemption. Through this covenant community, God preserved His law, maintained the temple worship, and kept the Scriptures. Most importantly, Israel was chosen to be the matrix and conduit for the incarnation of the Messiah, Jesus Christ.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这种拣选的首要目的，并非为了让这个民族的每一个成员都“集体自动上天堂”，而是为了在地上设立一个历史性的救赎工具。通过这个圣约群体，上帝赐下律法、设立圣殿、保存旧约圣经，并最为重要地——使以色列成为弥赛亚（耶稣基督）肉身降生的母体通道。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This is an outward, collective, and conditional covenant. Being born into this nation bestowed immense spiritual privileges—akin to being placed in the best preparatory class with the finest instruction. However, being in the classroom does not guarantee passing the final examination. If those within this covenant rejected Christ, they were cut off under the historical judgment of God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这是一种外在的、集体性的、有条件的圣约关系。生在这个国家，意味着你拥有极高的属灵特权，如同坐在设备最齐全的考前辅导班里；然而，坐在辅导班里，并不等同于自动通过了最终的考试。如果身在圣约中的人拒绝了基督，他们同样在上帝历史性的审判中被废弃。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

---

## 3. Inward Salvific Election: The Eternal Decree
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、内在的救恩拣选：永恒的恩典定旨
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The second dimension is the eternal, personal election of God. This is the divine decree, established before the foundation of the world, wherein God chose a specific people in Christ unto eternal salvation.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

第二种维度则是上帝永恒且是个体性的拣选。这是上帝在创世以前、在基督里预定得救的“真选民”的定旨。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To resolve the apparent contradiction of a "failed" covenant, the Apostle Paul delivers a decisive theological judgment in his letter to the Romans:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

为了解释圣约看似“失败”的表面矛盾，使徒保罗在《罗马书》中扔下了一句决定性的神学判词：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"But it is not as though the word of God has failed. For they are not all Israel who are descended from Israel;"
— Romans 9:6 (NASB)
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“这不是说神的话落了空。因为从以色列生的不都是以色列人，”
——罗马书 9:6（和合本）
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Paul's point is that within the large, visible, and national body of Israel—which is as numerous as the sand of the sea—there exists an inner, invisible, and true "spiritual Israel" sealed by the Holy Spirit.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

保罗的意思是，在那个庞大的、肉身的、多如海沙的“外在以色列国民”内部，还包含着一个由圣灵盖印的、核心的、真正的“属灵以色列”。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

We can visualize this relationship with a simple concentric structure:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

我们可以用一个简单的同心圆结构来直观呈现这两种拣选的关系：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```text
+---------------------------------------------+
|                Outward Israel               |
|         (Physical Bloodline, Sand of Sea)   |
|                                             |
|        +---------------------------+        |
|        |        Inward Israel      |        |
|        |  (Spiritual Remnant, Elect) |        |
|        |                           |        |
|        +---------------------------+        |
+---------------------------------------------+
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```text
+---------------------------------------------+
|                外在的以色列                 |
|         （肉身血统、多如海沙、历史工具）     |
|                                             |
|        +---------------------------+        |
|        |       内在的真以色列       |        |
|        |     （凭应许重生、得救余数） |        |
|        |                           |        |
|        +---------------------------+        |
+---------------------------------------------+
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This true election is unconditional, irresistible, and eternally secure. Those who belong to this remnant are saved not by virtue of their physical pedigree, but by the supernatural regenerating grace of God, which produces saving faith.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

内在的拣选则是无条件的、不可抗拒的，且是永远不失落的。这群“得救的余数”之所以获救，并非因着他们肉身的血统，而是因为上帝超自然的重生恩典动工在他们心里，使他们能因信称义。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This dynamic has always operated throughout redemptive history. In the days of the prophet Elijah, when the visible nation fell into total apostasy, God declared that He had preserved a true remnant:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在整个救赎历史中，这一法则一直在运行。在先知以利亚的时代，当整个有形国家都陷入背道时，上帝表明祂在其中保留了真正的余数：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"But what is the divine response to him? 'I HAVE KEPT FOR MYSELF SEVEN THOUSAND MEN WHO HAVE NOT BOWED THE KNEE TO BAAL.'"
— Romans 11:4 (NASB)
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“神的回话是怎么说的呢？他说：‘我为自己留下七千人，是未曾向巴力屈膝的。’”
——罗马书 11:4（和合本）
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

These seven thousand individuals were the spiritual remnant within the outwardly elect nation, chosen and sustained by sovereign grace.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这七千人，就是多如海沙的国民中，那真正蒙主主权恩典保守的“属灵余民”。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. The Wisdom of the Twofold Administration
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、双重治理的智慧与慈爱
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Why did God arrange history in this manner? Why did He not simply elect the saved remnant directly, rather than establishing a vast, visible nation where many would ultimately fall away? Historic Christian theology recognizes God's wisdom and love in this design.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

上帝为何要如此行？祂为何不直接只拣选那群最终得救的人，而非要先建立一个庞大却在历史中大面积失败的肉身以色列国呢？大公教会正统神学从这种设计中认出了上帝治世的伟大智慧与慈爱。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Preparation for All Nations
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 为万国所作的历史预备
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The historical nation of Israel served as the crucial preparation for the gospel of Christ to be received by all nations. Without a concrete, visible nation with historical laws and prophecies, the coming of Christ would have appeared as a rootless event lacking historical validation. Because this nation preserved the Old Testament Scriptures for centuries, people from every nation could examine the prophecies and recognize Jesus as the Savior.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

首先，历史中的以色列国为万国接受基督福音奠定了不可或缺的基石。如果上帝不建立一个历史中的肉身以色列国，不给他们律法与先知，耶稣基督的降生就成了一个没有任何历史根基和圣经预言支持的偶发事件。正是因为有这个民族在历史中作为“保管员”，当基督钉十字架并复活时，全世界的外邦人才能拿着旧约圣经，认出祂就是那位人类的救主。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Magnifying "Grace Alone"
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 极大地凸显“唯独恩典”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This structure magnifies the truth of "grace alone" (Sola Gratia). By demonstrating the historical failure of ethnic Israel to save themselves through pedigree, nationality, or the outward letter of the law, God proved to all humanity that physical lineage, religious culture, and external status cannot save.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

其次，这一构造极大地凸显了“唯独恩典”（Sola Gratia）的真理。上帝借着以色列民族的局部历史失败，向全人类证明了一件事：血统救不了你，国籍救不了你，宗教文化救不了你，甚至连摩西手写的律法字句本身也救不了你。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

John Calvin classically articulated this distinction between the general call of a nation and the special election of individuals:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

对于普遍圣约与特殊拣选的区分，大公教会的神学家约翰·加尔文曾做过经典的论述：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"God, by His own gratuitous election, adopts whom He will as His children... The first degree of election is that He chose all the seed of Abraham... The second degree, more limited class of election, was that in which the grace of God was more specially manifested, when of the same family of Abraham He rejected some, and kept others in His service."
— John Calvin, Institutes of the Christian Religion, 3.21.7
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“上帝以其白白的拣选，收纳祂所愿意的人为后嗣……第一种维度的拣选是，祂拣选了亚伯拉罕的所有后裔……第二种维度是更受限制的拣选，其中上帝的恩典表现得更为特殊——同样是在亚伯拉罕的家族中，祂废弃了一些人，而保留了另一些人来事奉祂。”
—— 约翰·加尔文，《基督教要义》，3.21.7
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. The Modern Application: "Not All Who Are of the Church Are the Church"
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、现代应用：“从教会生的，不都是真门徒”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Blind Spot of Modern Movements
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 现代运动的盲区
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This distinction exposes a critical blind spot in modern theology. Many contemporary movements place an unbalanced emphasis on the modern secular nation of Israel, as if physical lineage still confers a spiritual status apart from Christ. But Paul's theology is clear: physical lineage does not save. One must possess the faith of Abraham and be united to Christ.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这一区分直接指出了现代神学的盲区。当今许多流行的世俗化思潮对中东那个现代世俗的以色列国抱有某种盲目的迷信，仿佛只要拥有犹太血统就天然享有特权。然而，保罗的论证非常清晰：肉身血统救不了任何人，人必须拥有亚伯拉罕的信心，并在基督里联结。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Guarding Against False Security
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 警惕虚假的安全感
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Even more urgently, this tension between the outward and inward dimensions of election applies directly to the visible church today.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

更为紧迫的是，这两种拣选的张力同样复制在今天的有形教会中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

We must guard against a false sense of security based on outward membership. Some might assume that because they attend church, are baptized, or were raised in a Christian home, their salvation is secure.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

我们必须时刻警惕基于外在身份而产生的虚假安全感。正如有人曾错误地认为：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

> "As long as one has Jewish blood, or as long as one is baptized in the church, salvation is secure."
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

> “只要有犹太人血统，或者只要在教会受了洗，救恩就是稳妥的。”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

But Scripture warns that "not all who are descended from Israel are Israel." Similarly, "not all who are of the visible church are of the invisible church." External participation in church life is a profound blessing, but it is not regeneration. We must ask ourselves: Are we merely members of the outward assembly, or are we part of the inward remnant, regenerated by the Holy Spirit and relying solely on the cross of Christ?
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

但圣经警告我们：“从以色列生的，不都是以色列人。”同样，“从有形教会生的，不都是无形教会的真子民。”外在参与教会生活虽是极大的恩典特权，但这本身不等同于重生。我们必须审视自己：我们究竟只是外在集会中的一个旁观者与消费者，还是那个真正被圣灵换了肉心、对十字架的恩典心存战兢与感恩的“得救余数”？
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
