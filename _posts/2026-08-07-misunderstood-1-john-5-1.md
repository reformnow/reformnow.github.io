## 1. Introduction: The Cart Before the Horse in Modern Soteriology
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言：现代救恩论中的“本末倒置”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In contemporary evangelicalism, it is commonly preached that faith is the cause of regeneration. The sinner is presented with an invitation, and upon the exercise of their free will to "accept Jesus," God responds by regenerating their soul and granting them eternal life. This decisionistic paradigm places the ultimate initiative of salvation in the hands of the creature, rendering God's grace a passive resource waiting to be activated by human choice.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在现代福音派讲台中，人们普遍宣称信心是重生的原因。罪人被给予一份邀请，一旦他们行使自由意志去“接受耶稣”，上帝便会做出回应，重生他们的灵魂并赐予他们永生。这种决志主义范式将救恩的最终主动权交在了受造物手中，使上帝的恩典成为一种等待人类选择去激活的被动资源。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

> **A Common Humanistic Misreading:** "If you only choose to believe in Jesus Christ, you will be born of God and receive eternal life. The choice is yours; God has done His part, and now you must do yours."
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

> **常见的人本主义误读：** “只要你选择相信耶稣基督，你就能从神而生并得着永生。选择权在你手中；上帝已经完成了祂的那部分，现在你必须完成你的部分。”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

However, when we return to the original Greek scriptures, particularly comparing the famous declaration of John 3:16 with its close theological parallel in 1 John 5:1, we discover a syntax that completely reverses this humanistic order. The Greek text presents a beautiful, grammatically precise testimony to the historic Christian faith: regeneration is not the reward of faith, but its divine cause. The initiative is not in the willing of man, but in the sovereign mercy of God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

然而，当我们回归希腊文圣经原文，特别是将著名的《约翰福音》3:16 与其神学平行经文《约翰一书》5:1 进行对比时，我们会发现其语法逻辑完全颠覆了这种人本主义秩序。希腊文原典对历史大公教会正统信仰作出了极其优美且语法精准的见证：重生绝非信心的奖赏，而是信心的神圣原因。主动权不在于人的意愿，而在于上帝主权的怜悯。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

As this article unfolds, we will reconstruct the divine, monergistic order of faith and regeneration in Scripture through rigorous Greek grammatical analysis and the testimony of historic church theology.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

随着这篇博文的展开，我们将通过严格的希腊文文法剖析与大公教会历史神学的证言，还原圣经中关于信心与重生的神圣独作秩序。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 2. Grammatical Parallels: Substantival Participles and the Identity of "Believing Ones"
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、语法平行：名词性分词与“相信之人”的身份界定
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To understand the connection between these two Johannine texts, we must first examine their structural similarity. Both passages utilize a characteristic Greek construction to define the subjects under discussion.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

要理解约翰这两处文献之间的联系，我们首先必须审视它们的结构相似性。两处经文都使用了一种特征性的希腊文结构来定义所讨论的主体。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"ἵνα πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων εἰς αὐτὸν μὴ ἀπόληται ἀλλ᾽ ἔχῃ ζωὴν αἰώνιον."
— John 3:16 (Greek New Testament)
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“……叫凡信他的，不至灭亡，反得永生。”
—— 约翰福音 3:16 (中文和合本)
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων ὅτι Ἰη修斯 ἐστιν ὁ Χριστὸς ἐκ τοῦ Θεοῦ γεγέννηται..."
— 1 John 5:1 (Greek New Testament)
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“凡信耶稣是基督的，都是从神而生……”
—— 约翰一书 5:1 (中文和合本)
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In both verses, the phrase translated as "whoever believes" or "everyone who believes" is **πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων** (*pas ho pisteuōn*). This phrase consists of the adjective **πᾶς** (every/all), the masculine singular definite article **ὁ** (the), and the present active participle **πιστεύων** (believing).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在这两节经文中，被翻译为“凡相信的”或“每一个信的”的短语是 **πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων** (*pas ho pisteuōn*)。该短语由形容词 **πᾶς**（每一个/所有）、阳性单数定冠词 **ὁ**（这）以及现在时主动语态分词 **πιστεύων**（正在相信）组成。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In Greek grammar, when a definite article (*ὁ*) is placed directly before a participle (*πιστεύων*), it forms a **substantival participle**. It functions not as a conditional clause indicating potential choice ("if anyone decides to believe"), but rather as a noun describing a specific, existing group of people. 
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在希腊文语法中，当定冠词（*ὁ*）直接置于分词（*πιστεύων*）之前时，便构成了**名词性分词（Substantival Participle）**。它的功能并不是表示某种潜在选择的条件从句（“如果有人决定去信”），而是作为一个名词，用以描绘一类特定的、已然存在的群体。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Therefore, a more precise grammatical translation is:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

因此，更精准的文法直译应当是：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

> **Literal Exegesis:** "Every believing one..." or "All the believing ones..."
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

> **字面直译：** “每一个正在持续相信的人……” 或 “所有这群相信的人（believing ones）……”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Furthermore, the participle *πιστεύων* is in the **present tense**, which in Greek denotes continuous, habitual, and ongoing action. It does not refer to a single, past decision made by the natural human will, but rather to a permanent, ongoing state of life. 
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

此外，分词 *πιστεύων* 处于**现在时态**，这在希腊文中表示持续性、习惯性且进行中的动作。它指的不是受造物肉体意志在过去某一个瞬间所做出的单次决志，而是一种恒常的、持续不断的生命状态。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In John 3:16, the "believing ones" are those who will not perish but have eternal life. It defines the scope of Christ's saving efficacy. But how does one become a "believing one" in the first place? It is in 1 John 5:1 that the apostle provides the ultimate grammatical and theological answer.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在《约翰福音》3:16 中，“信他的人”是那些不至灭亡反得永生的人。它界定了基督救赎功效的范围。然而，一个人最初是如何成为“信他的人”的？使徒约翰在《约翰一书》5:1 中给出了最终的文法与神学答案。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. The Tense Contrast: How the Perfect Tense Establishes the Cause
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、时态的对比：完成时态如何确立先在因
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The theological crux of 1 John 5:1 lies in the temporal relationship between its two key verbs. The text juxtaposes the act of believing with the act of being born of God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《约翰一书》5:1 的神学核心在于其两个关键动词之间的时态时间关系。经文将“相信”的动作与“从神而生”的动作并置在了一起。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων... ἐκ τοῦ Θεοῦ γεγέννηται..."
— 1 John 5:1
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“凡信……的，都是从神而生……”
—— 约翰一书 5:1
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Notice the precise grammatical breakdown of these two verbs:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

请注意这两个动词极其精准的语法细分：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

*   **"Believes" (πιστεύων - pisteuōn):** A present active participle. It describes an ongoing, current action happening in the present ("every one who is now believing").
    {: .lang-en lang="en"}
*   **“信”（πιστεύων - pisteuōn）：** 现在时主动语态分词。它描述的是当下正在发生、持续进行的动作（“每一个现今正在持续相信的人”）。
    {: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
*   **"Born of God" (γεγέννηται - gegennētai):** A perfect passive indicative verb. In Greek, the **perfect tense** represents a past completed action with abiding, ongoing results in the present. The **passive voice** indicates that the subject is the recipient of the action, which is performed by an external agent—in this case, God.
    {: .lang-en lang="en"}
*   **“从神而生”（γεγέννηται - gegennētai）：** 完成时被动态直陈语气动词。在希腊文中，**完成时（Perfect Tense）**表示一个在过去已经完成、其影响和结果一直持续到现在的动作。**被动态（Passive Voice）**则表明动作的承受者是被动的，动作完全由外部施动者（在此即上帝）所施加。
    {: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

When we combine these two tenses, the grammatical sequence is absolute and unidirectional:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

当我们结合这两种时态时，文法的先后顺序是绝对且单向的：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

> **Strict Grammatical Translation:** "Everyone who is [presently] believing that Jesus is the Christ has [already] been born of God."
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

> **严谨语法直译：** “凡是［现今持续］相信耶稣是基督的人，［此前］都已经由神所生了。”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If the apostle John wanted to teach that human faith triggers divine regeneration, he would have been grammatically compelled to use either a present tense (e.g., "is being born") or a future tense (e.g., "will be born") for the act of birth. But by using the perfect passive *γεγέννηται*, John asserts that the new birth is a past, accomplished fact relative to the present exercise of faith.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

如果使徒约翰想要教导人的信心会触发神圣的重生，他在语法上就必须对“生”这个动作使用现在时（例如“正在被生”）或将来时（例如“将要被生”）。然而，通过使用完成时被动态的 *γεγέννηται*，约翰斩钉截铁地宣告：相对于当下信心的行使，新生是一个在过去已经达成的既定事实。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This is not an isolated grammatical anomaly. Throughout the First Epistle of John, this exact grammatical pattern is employed to show that holy actions are the fruit—not the cause—of the new birth:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这绝非孤立的文法特例。在整本《约翰一书》中，使徒约翰始终贯穿着这套完全一致的语法模式，用以表明圣洁的行事是新生命的果子，而非新生命的原因：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

*   **Righteousness:** "If you know that He is righteous, you know that everyone also who practices righteousness **has been born** (*γεγέννηται* - perfect passive) of Him." (1 John 2:29) — Practicing righteousness is the evidence, not the cause, of having been born of God.
    {: .lang-en lang="en"}
*   **公义：** “你们若知道他是公义的，就知道凡行公义之人都是他**所生的**（*γεγέννηται* - 完成时被动态）。”（约一 2:29）—— 行公义是此前已被神所生的凭据，而非原因。
    {: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
*   **Love:** "Beloved, let us love one another, for love is from God; and everyone who loves **has been born** (*γεγέννηται* - perfect passive) of God and knows God." (1 John 4:7) — Loving others is the evidence, not the cause, of having been born of God.
    {: .lang-en lang="en"}
*   **爱心：** “亲爱的弟兄啊，我们应当彼此相爱，因为爱是从神来的。凡有爱心的，都是**由神而生**（*γεγέννηται* - 完成时被动态），并且认识神。”（约一 4:7）—— 彼此相爱是此前已被神所生的凭据，而非原因。
    {: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
*   **Faith:** "Whoever believes that Jesus is the Christ **has been born** (*γεγέννηται* - perfect passive) of God." (1 John 5:1) — Believing is the evidence, not the cause, of having been born of God.
    {: .lang-en lang="en"}
*   **信心：** “凡信耶稣是基督的，都是**从神而生**（*γεγέννηται* - 完成时被动态）。”（约一 5:1）—— 坚守信心是此前已被神所生的凭据，而非原因。
    {: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To claim that faith precedes and causes regeneration is as logical as claiming that breathing precedes and causes physical life, or that bearing fruit precedes and causes the planting of the tree. The scriptural testimony is clear: the divine birth is the source; the human faith is the fruit.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

声称信心先于并导致重生，其逻辑就如同声称呼吸先于并导致了肉体生命，或者结出果子先于并导致了树木的栽种一样荒谬。圣经的见证清清楚楚：神圣的诞生是源头，人类的信心是果子。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. Divine Monergism: Active Creator, Passive Creature
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、神圣独作论：主动的造物主与被动的受造物
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This grammatical sequence leads directly to a profound soteriological reality: salvation is monergistic. Monergism (from the Greek *mono*, meaning "one," and *ergon*, meaning "work") is the doctrine that the Holy Spirit is the sole agent who effects the regeneration of the human heart, without any cooperation from the natural man.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这一文法逻辑直接指向了一个深邃的救恩论真相：救恩是独作的。独作论（Monergism，源自希腊文 *mono*意为“单一”，以及 *ergon*意为“工作”）是指：圣灵是使人心重生的唯一施动者，在此过程中完全没有自然人的合作。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In physical birth, the child plays an entirely passive role. A child does not choose to be conceived, nor do they cooperate in their own birthing process. They are simply born. The scriptures purposefully adopt this metaphor of "new birth" (*regeneration*) to illustrate the transition from spiritual death to spiritual life.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在肉身的出生中，婴儿扮演的是完全被动的角色。婴儿既无法选择被孕育，也无法在自己的出生过程中协助母亲。他们只是单纯地“被生下来”。圣经刻意采用“新生”（*重生*）这一隐喻，正是为了说明从属灵死亡到属灵生命的跨越。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"But as many as received Him, to them He gave the right to become children of God, even to those who believe in His name, who were born, not of blood nor of the will of the flesh nor of the will of man, but of God."
— John 1:12-13
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“凡接待他的，就是信他名的人，他就赐他们权柄，作神的儿女。这这些人不是从血气生的，不是从情欲生的，也不是从人意生的，乃是从神生的。”
—— 约翰福音 1:12-13
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The apostle John explicitly rules out human agency in the new birth: it is "not of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God." The verb *γεγέννηται* in 1 John 5:1, being in the passive voice, reinforces this truth. God is the active Father who begets; the sinner is the passive recipient who is begotten. 
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

使徒约翰在此明确排除了重生的任何人本因：它“不是从血气生的，不是从情欲生的，也不是从人意生的，乃是从神生的”。《约翰一书》5:1 中动词 *γεγέννηται* 的被动态形式再次强化了这一真理。上帝是主动生养的父，罪人是被动被生的承受者。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Once regenerated, the newborn soul is given a new nature, a heart of flesh replacing the heart of stone (Ezekiel 36:26). It is this new, living nature that immediately, inevitably, and actively responds to the Gospel in faith. We believe, and we act in believing, but our action is the effect of God's prior, sovereign quickening.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

一旦获得了重生，新生儿的灵魂便被赋予了新的性情，肉心取代了石心（以西结书 36:26）。正是这种崭新的、活泼的生命性情，驱使人立即、必然且主动地在信心里回应福音。我们相信，并且在相信中做出了实际的动作，但我们的这一动作，仅仅是上帝先在的、主权性赐生命工作的必然结果。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. The Witness of Historic Christian Theology
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、历史大公教会与正统神学的见证
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This understanding of grace preceding faith is not a novel construction, but the heartbeat of historic orthodox theology. It was defended vigorously by the early Church Fathers against Pelagianism, and was later codified in the great confessions of the Church.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这种“恩典先于信心”的理解并非标新立异的理论，而是历史正统神学的脉搏。早期教父在对抗伯拉纠主义时曾极力捍卫这一真理，其后更被确立在教会伟大的信条之中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. St. Augustine of Hippo (354–430 AD)
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 圣奥古斯丁（公元 354–430 年）
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In his theological controversies, St. Augustine initially held the common view that faith was man's own contribution to salvation, while grace was God's response. However, through deep meditation on Scripture, he famously retracted this view, realizing that even the very beginning of faith is a gift of God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在早期的神学探讨中，圣奥古斯丁曾一度倾向于普遍的看法，认为信心是人类对救恩做出的自身贡献，而恩典则是上帝的回应。然而，在对圣经进行了深入的反思之后，他在晚年公开修正了这一看法，意识到甚至连信心的发端本身也是上帝的恩赐。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

St. Augustine confessed his early theological misstep regarding human ability in his treatise on predestination:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

圣奥古斯丁在其关于预定的著作中，真诚地忏悔了自己早期对人类意志能力的误判：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"I carried this error still further in my thinking, believing that faith was not preceded by the grace of God, so that by it we might receive what we asked for aright... but that we should consent when the gospel was preached to us I thought was our own doing, and came to us from ourselves... But the Holy Spirit opened my eyes when He said: 'What do you have that you did not receive?'"
— Augustine, *On the Predestination of the Saints*, Book I, Chapter 3
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“在我的思考中，我曾把这个错误推得更远，认为信心并没有上帝恩典的先行引导，以便我们能藉此正确地求得所要的……我曾以为，当我们听到福音宣讲时予以同意，这是我们自己的作为，是从我们自身而来的……但圣灵开导了我的眼目，祂说：‘使你与人不同的是谁呢？你有什么不是领受的呢？’”
—— 奥古斯丁，《论圣徒的预定》，第一卷，第三章
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. St. John Chrysostom (347–407 AD)
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 圣约翰·金口（公元 347–407 年）
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Similarly, St. John Chrysostom, writing in the East, recognized that the human soul could not generate faith on its own apart from the prior, external call and work of God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

同样，在东方教会牧养的圣约翰·金口也认识到，若没有上帝先行且外部的呼召与动工，人类的心灵根本无法凭自己生出信心。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

St. John Chrysostom expounded on the origin of saving faith in his homilies on Ephesians:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

圣约翰·金口在其《以弗所书注释》中阐述了得救信心的源头：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Even faith, he means, is not of ourselves. Because had He not come, had He not called us, how had we been able to believe? For how, he says, shall they believe on Him of whom they have not heard? So that even the act of faith is not our own achievement, but is the gift of God."
— John Chrysostom, *Homilies on Ephesians*, Homily IV
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“他的意思是，甚至连信心也不是出于我们自己。因为如果祂没有来，如果祂没有呼召我们，我们又怎能相信呢？正如经上所说：‘未曾听见他，怎能信他呢？’因此，连信心的行动也不是我们自己的成就，而是上帝的恩赐。”
—— 约翰·金口，《以弗所书讲道集》，第四篇讲道
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Canons of Dort (1619)
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 《多特信经》（1619 年）
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This historic consensus of the Church was preserved and systematically codified in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries. Historic Christian confessions, when defining the nature of conversion, took great care to locate the power of faith not in moral suasion or human cooperation, but in God's sovereign, supernatural recreation.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

大公教会的这一历史共识在十六和十七世纪得到了系统性的重申与法理规范。当历史大公信条定义悔改归正的本质时，极力将信心的动力归于上帝主权、超自然的新造之工，而非道德上的劝导或人类的协助。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Synod of Dort articulated the supernatural and monergistic nature of regeneration in its articles:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

多特会议在其关于归正之工的法理条文中，极其清晰地陈明了重生的超自然性与神圣独作本质：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"This is that regeneration so highly celebrated in Scripture, and denominated a new creation: a resurrection from the dead, a making alive, which God works in us without our aid. But this is in no wise effected merely by the external preaching of the gospel, by moral suasion... It is evidently a supernatural work, most powerful, and at the same time most delightful, astonishing, mysterious, and ineffable... so that all those in whose hearts God works in this marvelous manner, are certainly, infallibly, and effectually regenerated, and do actually believe. And then the will, being now renewed, is not only actuated and influenced by God, but in consequence of this influence, becomes itself active. Wherefore also, man himself is rightly said to believe and repent, by virtue of that grace received."
— Canons of Dort, Third and Fourth Head of Doctrine, Article 12
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“这就是圣经中极力赞美的重生、新造、从死里复活和赐予生命的工作，这是上帝在我们里面动工，却无需我们协助的。这工作绝不仅仅是通过外在的福音宣讲、或道德劝服来达成的……这显然是一个超自然的工作，极有能力，同时又是最令人喜悦、惊叹、奥秘和无可言喻的……因此，凡是上帝以这奇妙方式在其心中动工的人，都必定无误且有效地重生了，并且实际地相信了。随后，那已被更新的意志，不仅被上帝所驱动和影响，而且由于这种影响，它自身也变得主动了。因此，因着所领受的恩典，人自身也被正确地称为是在相信和悔改。”
—— 《多特信经》，第三与第四教义部分，第十二条
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 6. Conclusion: The Comfort of Unconditional Grace
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 六、结语：无条件恩典的终极安慰
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

When we reconstruct the Ordo Salutis according to the syntax of 1 John 5:1 and John 3:16, we move away from the anxiety of humanism and step into the rest of sovereign grace. If faith were the cause of regeneration, our salvation would ultimately hinge on our own frail, fluctuating, and spiritually dead wills. We would live in constant doubt, wondering if our faith was strong enough to keep us born of God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

当我们根据《约翰一书》5:1 与《约翰福音》3:16 的语法结构重新构建救恩秩序时，我们便彻底脱离了人本主义带来的救恩焦虑，步入了上帝主权恩典的安息之中。若信心是重生的原因，我们的救恩最终将维系在我们自己脆弱、起伏不定且属灵上早已死亡的意志之上。我们将活在永无止境的怀疑中，不断揣测自己的信心是否足够强大，以至能够维持我们“从神而生”的资格。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

But when we see that regeneration precedes and produces faith, we realize that our faith is the secure anchor of a work already completed by God. The fact that you are believing in Christ today is the proof that God has already begotten you into an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading. The initiative was His, the work was His, and therefore, the glory belongs to Him alone.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

但当我们看明重生先于并产生信心时，我们就会确知，我们的信心是上帝早已在我们生命中完成之工的安全锚定。你今天能持续仰望、信靠基督，这一事实本身就是上帝早已生下你，使你进入那不能朽坏、不能玷污、不能衰残之基业的铁证。主动权在祂，工作在祂，因此，荣耀也唯独归于祂！
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
