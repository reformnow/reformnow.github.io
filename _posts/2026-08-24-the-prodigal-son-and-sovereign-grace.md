## 1. Introduction: The Myth of the Autonomous Prodigal
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言：自主之子的现代神话
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}


In many contemporary evangelical pulpits, the Parable of the Prodigal Son (or the Parable of the Loving Father) in Luke 15:11-32 is frequently misread as a prime illustration of human free will. Preachers focus heavily on verse 17, "But when he came to himself," arguing that the starting point of salvation lies in man's autonomous decision to repent, while God merely stands in the distance, passively waiting for the sinner to choose Him.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在许多现代福音派讲坛中，路加福音 15:11-32 的“浪子的比喻”（或称“慈父的比喻”）常被误读为人类自主自由意志的经典例证。讲道者往往将焦点放在第 17 节的“他醒悟过来”（或译“他醒悟自己”），进而得出结论：救恩的起点在于罪人自主做出的悔改决定，而上帝只是在远方被动等待，期盼着罪人能够做出选择归向祂。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This reading, however, completely tears the parable out of its narrative context in Luke 15, ignores the immediate audience of Jesus' teaching, and runs contrary to the unified soteriology of the entire Scripture. When examined under the light of historic Christian orthodoxy, this synergism is shown to be exegetically and dogmatically untenable.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

然而，这种解读完全脱离了路加福音 15 章的叙事上下文，忽略了耶稣讲论的直接针对对象，且与全本圣经一贯的救恩论背道而驰。若在历史性新教信仰与大公教会正统神学的亮光下进行审视，这种“神人合作论”的解经在学术和教义上都完全站不住脚。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

---

## 2. Exegetical Analysis: The Context of Divine Initiative
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、文本解经分析：神圣主动性的文脉
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To properly interpret the parable, we must respect the narrative structure and context in which Christ spoke. It cannot be isolated into proof-texting for human agency.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

要正确解读这则比喻，我们必须尊重基督讲论时的叙事结构和上下文脉，决不能将其孤立，作为粉饰人类自主能动性的“断章取义”工具。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Trinitarian Context of Seeking (Luke 15:1-10)
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 寻回的“三位一体”式上下文 (路加福音 15:1-10)
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Luke 15 is not a collection of three disconnected stories, but one continuous discourse. Jesus delivers this address in response to the Pharisees and scribes who grumbled, "This man receives sinners and eats with them" (Luke 15:2). He responds with a threefold parable showing how the lost are found:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

路加福音 15 章并不是三个彼此孤立的散落故事，而是一个连续的讲论。面对法利赛人和文士对祂“接待罪人并与他们同吃”的指责（路 15:2），耶稣通过一连串由三个部分组成的比喻，显明失落者是如何被寻回的：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

First, the Parable of the Lost Sheep (Luke 15:4-7) shows the shepherd actively searching for the straying sheep until he finds it. This highlights the redemptive work of Christ, the Good Shepherd, who initiates the search.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

首先是“失羊的比喻”（路 15:4-7），描绘牧人主动去寻找那只迷失的羊，直到找着。这显明了基督——好牧人——主动寻找的救赎之功。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Second, the Parable of the Lost Coin (Luke 15:8-10) depicts the woman lighting a lamp and diligently sweeping the house until she retrieves the coin. This serves as an allegory for the work of the Holy Spirit, who illuminates the sinner's heart through the light of the gospel.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

其次是“失钱的比喻”（路 15:8-10），描绘妇人点上灯，细细打扫屋子，直到找回那枚银币。这象征着圣灵的工作，祂借着福音之光照亮并洁净罪人的内心。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Finally, the Parable of the Prodigal Son (Luke 15:11-32) focuses on the Father’s heart and the warm reception of the returned child.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

最后是“浪子的比喻”（路 15:11-32），焦点在于天父的心肠以及对归家之子的接纳。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the first two parables, the sheep does not find the shepherd, and the silver coin has no capacity to jump back into the pocket. Both are entirely passive in their recovery. It is hermeneutically inconsistent to isolate the third parable to argue for human initiation when the entire chapter establishes a Trinitarian portrait of salvation where God is the sole seeker.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在前两个比喻中，迷途的羊没有能力寻找牧人，失落的银币更无可能自行跳回口袋。在被寻回的过程中，它们完全是被动的。如果将第三个比喻割裂开来，用以论证罪人能自主开启救恩，这在解经上显然是前后矛盾的。因为整章经文共同描绘了一幅三一上帝拯救罪人的宏大图景，而神才是唯一的寻找者。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

As Saint Augustine of Hippo noted in his pastoral reflections on this chapter:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

正如圣奥古斯丁在针对本章的牧养反思中所指出的：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Who is that shepherd who sought the lost sheep? Who is that woman who swept the house to find the coin? Who is that father who welcomed the wayward son? This is Christ, this is the Holy Spirit, this is God the Father! The sinner cannot return unless he has first been sought."
— Augustine of Hippo, *Sermon 112A*
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“谁是那去找羊的牧人？谁是那点灯寻找银币的妇人？谁是那迎接收拾残局之子的父亲？这就是基督，这就是圣灵，这就是父上帝！罪人若非先被寻找，绝无可能自行归回。”
—— 圣奥古斯丁，《讲道集》第 112A 篇
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The True Nature of "Coming to Himself" (ἔρχομαι εἰς ἑαυτόν)
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. “醒悟过来”（ἔρχομαι εἰς ἑαυτόν）的真正含义
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Those who advocate for autonomous free will place the pivot of the narrative on the son's change of mind:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

那些主张自主自由意志的人，将整个叙事的转折点放在了小儿子心思的转变上：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"But when he came to his senses, he said, 'How many of my father’s hired laborers have more than enough bread, but I am dying here from hunger!'"
— Luke 15:17
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“他醒悟过来，就说：‘我父亲有多少的雇工，口粮有余，我倒在这里饿死吗？’”
—— 路加福音 15:17
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

However, a closer look at the syntax and the narrative development reveals that this "awakening" is not a spiritual spark of inherent human goodness, but the result of total existential bankruptcy.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

然而，仔细观察语法结构和叙事走向就会发现，这番“醒悟”绝非人类内在自发之善的属灵火花，而是生存层面彻底破产后的无奈挣扎。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

First, the awakening is triggered by extreme desperation—a severe famine and the refusal of anyone to give him even pig food (Luke 15:14-16). It represents the utter collapse of the sinner's autonomy under the weight of God's providential judgment, not a self-generated movement toward holiness.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

首先，这一“醒悟”是被推向绝境的直接产物——遭遇大饥荒，甚至连猪吃的豆荚都没人施舍（路 15:14-16）。这展示了罪人在上帝护理性的管教与审判之下，其受造性的自主权彻底坍塌，而非其内心生发出了渴望圣洁的自发向往。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Second, the son's planned confession reveals a legalistic, transaction-based mindset. He plans to say: "make me as one of your hired servants" (Luke 15:19). In the ancient Near East, a hired servant (*misthios*) was a day laborer paid for work, distinct from household slaves who were part of the family structure. The prodigal thought he could pay off his debt and earn his survival through labor. This shows that the unregenerate mind, even when "awakened" to its misery, naturally defaults to self-salvation and merit.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

其次，小儿子预先设计的忏悔词暴露出一种律法主义的、基于交易的心态。他计划说：“把我当作一个雇工吧”（路 15:19）。在古近东文化中，“雇工”（*misthios*）是按工计酬的临时日工，与作为家庭内部成员的奴仆不同。浪子以为自己可以通过做工换取报酬，来偿还债务并赚取生存的资源。这恰恰说明，未得重生的心思即使在“醒悟”到自身痛苦时，依然本能地诉诸自我拯救与行为功劳。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Third, the father interrupts the son's plan. When the son begins his speech (Luke 15:21), the father does not allow him to finish the transaction-oriented phrase "make me as one of your hired servants." Instead, the father commands the servants to bring the best robe, the ring, and the sandals—symbols of restored sonship and unconditional grace. The father's preemptive mercy shatters and rewrites the son's self-righteous plan.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

第三，父亲在叙事中打断了儿子的计谋。当儿子开始背诵那套说辞时（路 15:21），父亲甚至没有让他说出那句带有交易性质的“把我当作一个雇工吧”，而是直接吩咐仆人拿来上好的袍子、戒指和鞋子——这代表了儿子身份的恢复和无条件的恩典。父亲先发制人的怜悯，击碎并重塑了儿子自以为义的计划。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

John Chrysostom, writing in the early church, illuminated this dynamic:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

早期教会的约翰·金口在阐释这一动态时写道：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"He came to himself, not because he suddenly possessed any righteousness, but because extreme want forced him to his wits' end. Even before he took his first step toward home, the mercy of the compassionate Father was already drawing him in secret."
— John Chrysostom, *Homilies on Luke*
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“他醒悟过来，并非因为他突然拥有了某种义，而是因为极度的困乏将他逼到了绝境。甚至在他尚未迈出归家第一步时，慈父的怜悯就早已在暗中牵引他了。”
—— 约翰·金口，《路加福音释义》
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The True Protagonist of the Narrative
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 叙事中的真正主角
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The title "Parable of the Prodigal Son" shifts the weight to the sinner, but the text places the spotlight entirely on the Father.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

“浪子的比喻”这一传统名称将叙事重心移到了罪人身上，但经文的聚光灯实际上完全打在父亲身上。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"So he set out and came to his father. But when he was still a long way off, his father saw him and felt compassion for him, and ran and embraced him and kissed him."
— Luke 15:20
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“于是起来，往他父亲那里去。相离还远，他父亲看见，就动了慈心，跑去抱着他的颈项，连连与他亲嘴。”
—— 路加福音 15:20
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In ancient Near Eastern culture, a wealthy patriarch running in public was a deeply humiliating act. By running, the father absorbed the shame of the village, shielding the rebellious son from the public hostility and rejection (the *Kezazah* ceremony) he deserved.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在古近东文化中，一位尊贵富有的族长在公共场合奔跑是极其屈尊、甚至是有损尊严的举动。父亲通过奔跑，独自担当了本应落在这叛逆之子身上的羞辱与村民的指责（即古代社会中驱逐败子的“绝交礼”），保护了他免受本该承受的公开唾弃。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Every action verb here—"saw," "felt compassion," "ran," "embraced," "kissed"—belongs to the father. Salvation is a work of unilateral divine passion. Long before the son formulated a proper confession, the father was already acting on his behalf.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这里的所有动作动词——“看见”、“动慈心”、“跑”、“抱”、“亲”——全部属于父亲。救赎是一个神圣单方发动的大能之工。早在儿子构思出一份妥当的认罪书之前，父亲就已经在为他而行动了。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This priority of grace is consistent with the historic confessions of the church, such as the *Heidelberg Catechism*:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

恩典的这种首要性与教会的历史性信经高度一致，正如《海德堡要理问答》所宣示的：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Question 8: But are we so corrupt that we are totally unable to do any good and inclined to all evil?
Answer: Yes, unless we are regenerated by the Spirit of God."
— Heidelberg Catechism, Q&A 8
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“问：那么，我们是否陷入彻底的败坏，以致完全无法行任何善，而倾向于万恶吗？
答：是的，除非我们藉着上帝的圣灵重生。”
——《海德堡要理问答》，第 8 问
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. Theological Exposition: The Monergistic Framework of Salvation
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、神学系统剖析：神圣独作的救恩框架
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

When we align this passage with the broader counsel of Scripture, the humanistic premise of "free will initiating repentance" crumbles under three major theological realities of the historic Christian faith.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

当我们把这段经文置于整本圣经的整全启示中时，“人凭自由意志开启悔改”的人本主义假设，在历史基督教信仰的三大系统神学真理面前，立时瓦解。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Spiritual Death and Total Depravity
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 灵性死亡与罪人的全然败坏
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The synergistic view assumes that the sinner possesses a "neutral" free will capable of making a decisive move toward God. But Scripture describes the unregenerate person not merely as sick, hungry, or lost, but as spiritually "dead."
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

神人合作论假设罪人依然拥有一种“中立的”自由意志，能够在关键时刻自发地移向神。但圣经对未重生之人的定性，并非只是患病、饥饿或迷路，而是灵性上的“死亡”。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"And you were dead in your trespasses and sins,"
— Ephesians 2:1
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“你们死在过犯罪恶之中，”
—— 以弗所书 2:1
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

A corpse cannot choose to be resurrected; resurrection is an act of sovereign, unilateral power. The father in the parable explicitly confirms this when he declares the status of his son:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

一具尸体绝不可能主动选择让自己复活，复活完全是单方面主权施恩的大能。比喻中的父亲在宣告儿子的灵性状态时，也亲自印证了这一点：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"for this son of mine was dead and has come to life again; he was lost and has been found.' And they began to celebrate."
— Luke 15:24
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“因为我这个儿子是死而复活，失而又得的。’他们就快乐起来。”
—— 路加福音 15:24
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If the son was "dead," his awakening could not have been self-caused. In the economy of salvation, regeneration must logically precede faith. God must first breathe life into the dead soul, transforming the hostile human will so that it willingly responds to His call.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

如果儿子是“死”的，他的“醒悟”就绝不可能源于自发。在救恩的施行秩序中，重生在逻辑上必须先于信心。上帝必须首先将生命之气吹入死寂的灵魂中，更新罪人那顽梗、敌对的意志，使他能够甘心乐意地回应神的呼召。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This is beautifully stated in the historic *Canons of Dort*:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

历史性的《多特信经》对此有极佳的阐述：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Therefore all men are conceived in sin, and are by nature children of wrath, incapable of saving good, prone to evil, dead in sin, and in bondage thereto; and without the regenerating grace of the Holy Spirit, they are neither able nor willing to return to God, to reform the depravity of their nature, or to dispose themselves to reformation."
— Canons of Dort, Third and Fourth Head of Doctrine, Article 3
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“因此，所有的人都怀胎在罪中，本为可怒之子，不能行任何得救的善，倾向邪恶，死在罪中，作罪的奴仆；若没有圣灵重生的恩典，他们既不能也不愿归向上帝，甚至不能矫正其败坏的本性，或预备自己去接受矫正。”
——《多特信经》，第三与第四要点，第 3 条
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

As Augustine noted in his debate against the early humanists of his day:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

正如奥古斯丁在与当时早期人本主义者的辩论中所指出的：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"That we will is of our own; but that we will what is good is from the gift of God. Unless God makes us alive, how can a man who is dead in sins come to his senses and turn to the living God by his own power?"
— Augustine of Hippo, *On Grace and Free Will*
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“我们能够意志，是出于我们自己；但我们能够拥有善意志，则是出于上帝的赐予。若不是上帝使我们活过来，死在罪中的人如何能自发地‘醒悟’并转向活神？”
—— 圣奥古斯丁，《论恩典与自由意志》
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Prevenient and Effectual Grace
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 先在恩典与有效呼召
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

A common mistake in decisionistic theology is separating the son's internal thoughts from the father's external influence, placing the father in a position of passive waiting. But this violates the doctrine of prevenient (preceding) grace and effectual calling.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

决志神学的一个常见谬误，是将小儿子的内心转变与父亲的隐秘影响完全割裂，使父亲处于一种被动等待的尴尬境地。然而，这违背了“先在恩典”（Prevenient Grace）与“有效呼召”（Effectual Calling）的真理。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The son's memory of the father's house, his sorrow over his sins, and his willingness to return were not random acts of human willpower. They were the result of the Holy Spirit's quiet, inward work drawing him home. Left to ourselves, the natural man in crisis only grows more bitter or runs deeper into idolatry. It is only because of God's preceding grace that a sinner ever desires the holy presence of God.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

小儿子对父家的记忆、对自身罪恶的忧伤、以及回家的意愿，绝非人类自发意志的偶然产物。这乃是圣灵在暗中动工、引人归家的内在果效。若任凭我们自己，处在危机中的自然人只会变得更加苦毒，或者跑向更深的偶像崇拜。罪人之所以会产生朝向圣洁天父的渴望，完全是因为上帝那双看不见的手在先运行。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The *Westminster Confession of Faith* defines this effectual work:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

《威斯敏斯特信条》对这种有效作工做出了精确定义：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"This effectual call is of God's free and special grace alone, not from anything at all foreseen in man, who is altogether passive therein, until, being quickened and renewed by the Holy Spirit, he is thereby enabled to answer this call, and to embrace the grace offered and conveyed in it."
— Westminster Confession of Faith, Chapter 10, Article 2
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“此有效呼召完全是出于上帝无条件和特别的恩典，绝非由于在人里面预先见有什么善行。人在此呼召上完全是被动的，直到他被圣灵赐予生命和更新，才使他能够回应此呼召，并接受其中所宣告和赐予的恩典。”
——《威斯敏斯特信条》，第 10 章，第 2 条
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Furthermore, the ecumenical Church historically ruled against humanism at the Second Council of Orange in AD 529:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

此外，大公教会早在公元 529 年的第二次奥兰治会议上，就曾对类似的人本主义思想做出权威判决：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"If anyone says that not only the increase of faith but also its beginning and the very desire for belief... belongs to us by nature and not by a gift of grace, that is, by the inspiration of the Holy Spirit amending our will... he is proved to be an enemy of the teaching of the Apostles."
— Second Council of Orange, Canon 5
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“若有人说，我们归向上帝的起头或信心的愿望是出于我们自身本性的善，而非圣灵在我们里面的感动与预备，这就是违背了使徒的教导。”
—— 第二次奥兰治会议，第五条告白
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Elder Brother: The Delusion of Self-Righteousness
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 大儿子：自以为义的虚妄幻觉
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To focus only on the younger son is to ignore the primary target of Jesus' sermon: the self-righteous Pharisees and scribes (Luke 15:2). The elder brother represents this very group.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

若只关注小儿子，就会忽略耶稣讲论这则比喻的直接针对对象：那些自负的法利赛人和文士（路 15:2）。大儿子正是这一群体的化身。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

He remained in the father's house, outwardly compliant, but his heart was far from the father. His obedience was transactional, revealing that he served the father only to gain a "young goat" to celebrate with his friends. When the father extended grace to the returned prodigal, the elder son was filled with anger and refused to enter.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

他看似留在父家，外在行为无可挑剔，但他的心却和父亲极其疏远。他的顺服全然是交易性质的，这从他埋怨父亲没有给他“一只山羊羔”同朋友欢欣便可见一斑。而当父亲把白白的恩典赐给归家的浪子时，大儿子却怒气冲天，拒绝进门。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If repentance and fellowship were matters of human free will, the elder brother, who possessed the outward advantages, should have been the first to rejoice. Instead, he proved that both the wild rebel in the far country and the religious moralist in the father's field are equally lost. Without the father actively going out to entreat *both*—just as the father went out to plead with the elder son (Luke 15:28)—no human will can remain in the covenant of grace.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

如果悔改与团契是人类自由意志的事，那么占尽外在优势的大儿子理应最先欢喜快乐。然而，他却证明了：无论是远方放荡的浪子，还是家门前自以为义的道德主义者，同样都是失丧的。若没有父亲“走出家门”去劝导这两者——正如父亲也出来劝大儿子一样（路 15:28）——没有任何人能靠自己留在恩典的盟约中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

St. Ambrose of Milan summarized this dynamic:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

米兰的圣盎博罗削概括了这一动态：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"The elder son and the younger son reveal two modes of human lostness: one is the licentiousness of fleeing the law, the other is the pride of self-righteous law-keeping. Without the Father actively going out to call them—for the Father also went out to entreat the elder son—neither could remain in grace by his own strength."
— Ambrose of Milan, *Exposition of the Gospel according to Luke*
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“大儿子与小儿子揭示了人性的两种迷失：一种是远离律法的放荡，一种是自以为守律法的骄傲。两者若没有父主动走出家门去呼唤他们（父也出来劝大儿子），没有一个人能凭自己留在恩典之中。”
—— 圣盎博罗削，《路加福音释义》
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

---

## 4. Conclusion: Salvation is of the Lord
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、结论：救恩唯独属乎耶和华
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To read the Parable of the Prodigal Son as a story about the power of human choice is to miss the entire point of the gospel. The prodigal son did not save himself by a masterstroke of free will.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

将“浪子的比喻”解读为一个宣扬人类自由抉择的故事，实在是错失了福音的核心。浪子绝非靠着自由意志的神来一笔自我救赎。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

He was dead, and the Father made him alive. He was lost, and the Father sought him. He came to himself because God’s sovereign grace broke through his rebellion. And he was restored because the Father ran, took the shame, and clothed him in the righteousness of another.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

他原是死的，天父使他活了；他原是失落的，天父将他寻回。他能够醒悟，是因为神的主权恩典击碎了他的悖逆；他能够恢复身份，是因为父亲奔跑着替他承受了羞辱，并将基督的义袍披在他身上。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Salvation is never a cooperative venture between a weak God and a willing man. From its beginning, middle, to its end, salvation is of the Lord. Soli Deo Gloria.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

救恩绝非软弱之上帝与主动之人类之间的合作。从起始、过程到终结，救恩唯独出于耶和华。唯独天父得着荣耀。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
