## 1. Introduction: The Modern Dogma of "Unforced Love"
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、引言：“强迫不是真爱”的现代教条
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In many contemporary evangelical pulpits and popular anthropocentric theologies, a particular slogan has been elevated to an unquestionable axiom:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在许多福音派讲坛与当代人本主义神学中，有一句口号已被捧为不证自明的“铁律”：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

> "Coercion is not true love; therefore, God must give man autonomous free will to reject the gospel, and He will never violate human choice."
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

> “强迫不是真爱，所以神必须给人自由意志去拒绝福音，祂绝不强求人的选择。”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This sentiment sounds intuitively appealing to modern individualistic ears. It paints God as the ultimate "gentleman" who politely knocks on the heart's door, patiently waiting for the autonomous creature to grant Him permission. However, when this assertion is weighed on the balances of historic Christian orthodoxy, biblical exegesis, patristic consensus, and historic confessions, it is revealed to be not only logically self-defeating, but a catastrophic misunderstanding of divine love, human freedom, and the dead state of fallen humanity.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这一论调在现代个体主义的文化语境中极具亲和力，它将上帝塑造成一位彬彬有礼的“绅士”，在人心门外被动等候受造物的垂青与许可。然而，若将这一论调置于历史基督教信仰与大公教会正统神学的天平上，就会发现它不仅在逻辑上充满荒谬，在神学上更是对“真爱”、“自由”与“死在罪恶过犯中的人性”的彻底误读。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To dismantle this pervasive myth, we must move beyond sentimental rhetoric and examine the issue across multiple rigorous dimensions: conceptual category distinction, biblical exegesis, the patristic refutation of Pelagianism, eschatological logic, and historic confessional theology.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

对此论调的全面驳斥，必须超越情绪化的讲台修辞，在大公教会统续、圣经解经、教父教导、终末论逻辑与信条裁决的多重维度下彻底展开。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 2. The Category Error: Confusing "Coercion" with "Resurrection and Healing"
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、错谬根源：对“强迫”与“医治”的范畴混淆
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The fundamental fallacy of the "coercion is not true love" argument lies in a severe category mistake (*fallacia accidentis*). It falsely equates God's supernatural **regeneration and healing** (*regeneratio et sanatio*) with **external violent coercion** (*coactio*).
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

“强迫不是真爱”这一论调的最大荒谬，在于它犯了严重的范畴混淆（Category Error）。它将上帝大能的**重生与医治（Regeneration & Healing）**，假想成了**外在的暴力胁迫（Coercion）**。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Redemption Is Not Kidnapping, but Resurrection and Sight to the Blind
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 救赎不是绑架，而是死人复活与瞎眼得看见
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Consider a concrete analogy: When a person collapses unconscious in a raging fire, does the firefighter lack "true love" because he carries the victim out of the flames without first waiting for his conscious "consent"? When a psychotic patient in the grip of severe delusions attempts self-harm, is the physician violating human rights by administering an injection that restores the patient to sanity?
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

当一个人昏迷倒在火场中，消防员不顾他的“意愿”直接将他抱出火海，难道是“缺乏真爱”吗？当一个精神错乱、自残求死的人被医生强行施救并治愈了大脑，难道是“侵犯人权”吗？
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the biblical diagnosis of the Fall, fallen human will is neither neutral nor healthy. Man is not merely spiritually ill; he is **dead in trespasses and sins**, and his natural disposition is **active hostility toward God**:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在圣经对堕落人性的诊断中，人的意志绝非“中立且健康”的。罪人不仅是灵性微恙，而是**死在过犯罪恶之中**，其本性更是**与神为仇**的：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"And you were dead in your trespasses and sins... But God, being rich in mercy, because of His great love with which He loved us, even when we were dead in our transgressions, made us alive together with Christ (by grace you have been saved)."
— Ephesians 2:1, 4-5
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“你们死在过犯罪恶之中，祂叫你们活过来。……然而，神既有丰富的怜悯，因祂爱我们的大爱，当我们死在过犯中的时候，便叫我们与基督一同活过来。（你们得救是本乎恩。）”
—— 以弗所书 2:1, 4-5
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"because the mind set on the flesh is hostile toward God; for it does not subject itself to the law of God, for it is not even able to do so,"
— Romans 8:7
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“原来体贴肉体的，就是与神为仇；因为不服神的律法，也是不能服，”
—— 罗马书 8:7
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

God's effectual grace is never an external weapon held to the throat of someone who "does not want heaven," forcing him kicking and screaming into paradise. Rather, it is the divine Surgeon performing a spiritual heart transplant: **removing the heart of stone and giving a heart of flesh**, opening blind eyes to behold supreme Beauty, and restoring spiritual taste buds to savor supreme Goodness.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

上帝不可抗拒的恩典（Irresistible Grace），绝非架着一把刀强迫一个“不想去天堂的人”去天堂，而是神圣的大医师亲自施行属灵的心脏移植手术：**除去石心，赐下肉心**（结 36:26），开启瞎眼得以瞻仰至高之美，恢复属灵味蕾得以品尝至善之甘甜。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"Moreover, I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you; and I will remove the heart of stone from your flesh and give you a heart of flesh."
— Ezekiel 36:26
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“我也要赐给你们一个新心，将新灵放在你们里面，又从你们的肉体中除掉石心，赐给你们肉心。”
—— 以西结书 36:26
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

When this supernatural healing takes place, the person runs to Christ not through external coercion, but **with the utmost spontaneity, delight, and willing joy**.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

当这一超自然的医治临到时，罪人奔向基督绝非出于外在的被迫，而是**极其自愿、甘心乐意**地投奔救主怀抱。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

As Saint Augustine of Hippo brilliantly distinguished between external compulsion and internal liberation in his treatise against the Pelagians:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

正如圣奥古斯丁在抗击伯拉纠主义的著作《论精神与字句》中，精准地区分了外在的“强迫”与内在的“释放”：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Do we then through grace make void free will? By no means; but we rather establish free will. For even as the law is not made void, but rather established through faith, so also is free will not made void, but established through grace. For the law brings knowledge, but grace brings love. Are we not free when we love God? When healing comes, the will is made truly free."
— Augustine of Hippo, *On the Spirit and the Letter*, Chapter 30
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“恩典并非摧毁自由意志，而是建立自由意志。因为正如律法带来知识，恩典带来对律法的喜爱。难道我们在拥有对神的喜爱时，意志反倒不自由了吗？当医治临到时，意志才真正获得了自由。”
—— 圣奥古斯丁，《论精神与字句》，第 30 章
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Divine Sovereignty and the True Definition of Freedom
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 精华借力：主权与自由的分歧
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Modern libertarian definitions of freedom define "liberty" as the ability to choose otherwise (*libertas indifferentiae*), meaning that one is only free if one retains the power to reject God. But as the Christian thinker Arthur Custance pointed out, if the ability to resist God is made the essential prerequisite of human freedom, its logical consequence is **elevating the creature's will above the Creator's will**.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

正如亚瑟·库斯唐斯（Arthur Custance）深刻指出的，如果把“能够抗拒神”定义为自由的前提，其本质就是**让受造物的意志凌驾于造物主的意志之上**。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"To assert that man must be free to reject God in order to be free to love Him is to place the creature's will above the Creator's. By definition, perfect freedom is perfect conformity to a perfect law. Divine sovereignty is not the enemy of human freedom, but its only true foundation."
— Arthur Custance, *The Sovereignty of Grace*
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“断言人必须拥有拒绝上帝的自由才能自由地爱祂，本质上是让受造物的意志凌驾于造物主的意志之上。按定义而言，完美的自由就是对完美律法的完美顺服。上帝的主权绝非人类自由的仇敌，而是其唯一的真正基石。”
—— 亚瑟·库斯唐斯（Arthur Custance），《恩典的主权》
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If salvation ultimately hinged on human cooperation or non-resistance, the gospel would cease to be good news—it would become a burden of human merit. But Scripture proclaims that true freedom is found in complete harmony with God's holy character:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

救恩若最终取决于人的配合或不抗拒，福音就完全不再是好消息，而变成了变相的人类功德。但圣经宣告，真正的自由乃在于与上帝圣洁性情的完全相称：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"The law of the LORD is perfect, restoring the soul; The testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple."
— Psalm 19:7
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“耶和华的律法全备，能苏醒人心；耶和华的法度确定，能使愚人有智慧。”
—— 诗篇 19:7
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

God's sovereign grace does not violate true freedom; it creates the only environment in which true freedom can exist.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

上帝救赎的至高主权，恰恰是真自由唯一的源头与保障。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. Patristic and Ecumenical Condemnation: The Ghost of Pelagianism
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、大公教会与教父的破除：古老的伯拉纠主义幽灵
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The notion that "God stands respectfully at the threshold, powerless to act until human free will gives the green light" is nothing new. It is simply the modern resurrection of 5th-century Pelagianism and Semi-Pelagianism (Massilianism). These ancient heresies posited that fallen human nature retained an untouched island of autonomy capable of initiating the desire for salvation.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

“强迫不是真爱”本质上是 5 世纪伯拉纠主义（Pelagianism）与半伯拉纠主义（Semi-Pelagianism）在现代的翻版——假设人心中还保留着一小块未经玷污的自由意志，可以自行决定是否向神“伸出手”，而上帝只能在门外被动观望。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Human Spiritual Impotence and the Priority of Sovereign Grace
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 人性的彻底瘫痪与恩典的先在性
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If God were merely to "respect freedom and wait at a distance," the inevitable outcome for all humanity would be universal, irrevocable damnation. A corpse does not reach out for medicine; a blind man cannot choose to enjoy the sunrise until light is restored to his retinas.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

如果神只是“尊重自由，站在远处等候”，那么全人类的结局只能是集体沉沦。死尸不会主动伸手拿药，生来瞎眼的人在视力恢复之前根本不可能选择去欣赏日出。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The early Church Fathers and historic synods settled this debate decisively: Sinners cannot generate the slightest holy motion toward God apart from the prior, unilateral, and supernatural work of the Holy Spirit.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

大公教会早在早期大公会议与教父教导中就已明确裁定：罪人的意志若没有圣灵**单方面的超自然重塑**，绝不可能产生任何爱神的动机与愿望。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

At the Second Council of Orange in AD 529, the ecumenical orthodoxy of the Western church formally condemned the idea that God waits for human initiative:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在公元 529 年的第二次奥兰治会议上，大公教会正统正式宣告并裁决：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Canon 4: If anyone maintains that God awaits our desire to be cleansed from sin, but does not acknowledge that even our desire to be cleansed is produced in us by the infusion and operation of the Holy Spirit, he resists the Holy Spirit Himself...

Canon 13: The free will weakened in the first man cannot be repaired except through the grace of baptism and regeneration; for what is lost cannot be returned unless it is first given."
— Second Council of Orange (AD 529), *Canons 4 and 13*
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“第4条：若有人断言，上帝在等待我们的意愿去拯救我们脱离罪恶，而非承认：即使是我们‘想要被拯救’的愿望本身，也是藉着圣灵的倾倒与预备而在我们里面产生的，这就是抵挡了圣灵。

第13条：亚当犯罪后所丧失的自由意志，唯有藉着洗礼与重生的恩典才能得以恢复；丧失的东西，若非先被赐予，自身绝无法重获。”
—— 第二次奥兰治会议（AD 529），告白第 4 与第 13 条
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Saint John Chrysostom, the golden-mouthed preacher of the Eastern church, echoed this absolute priority of divine grace in his homilies on Romans:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

东方教会的著名教父圣约翰·金口（John Chrysostom）在《罗马书讲道集》中同样强调了神圣恩典的绝对先在性：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"We have nothing to boast of. For before we could respond to God, it was He who poured out His love into our hearts. It is not that we first stretched forth our hand, but His sovereign grace seized us who were already dead."
— John Chrysostom, *Homilies on Romans*
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“我们没有任何可夸之处。因为在我们能够回应神之前，是祂先将爱倾倒在我们心里。不是我们先伸出手，而是祂的主权恩典抓住了我们这已死的人。”
—— 圣约翰·金口，《罗马书讲道集》
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. Biblical Exegesis: Salvation Is Not of Human Will
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 圣经解经：救恩本非出于人意
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Apostolic witness leaves no room for the modern theory that salvation originates in human self-determination. The Apostle John explicitly contrasts spiritual birth with human volition:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

使徒的见证丝毫没有给现代“救恩源于人类自我决断”的理论留下任何立足之地。使徒约翰明确将属灵重生的源头与人类意志对立开来：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"who were born, not of blood nor of the will of the flesh nor of the will of man, but of God."
— John 1:13
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“这等人不是从血气生的，不是从情欲生的，也不是从人意生的，乃是从神生的。”
—— 约翰福音 1:13
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Likewise, the Apostle Paul directly addresses the root of human agency in salvation:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

同样，使徒保罗在论及得救的终极根源时毫不含糊地指出：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"So then it does not depend on the person who wants it nor the one who runs, but on God who has mercy."
— Romans 9:16
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“据此看来，这不在乎那定意的，也不在乎那奔跑的，只在乎发怜悯的神。”
—— 罗马书 9:16
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```bible
"No one can come to Me unless the Father who sent Me draws him; and I will raise him up on the last day."
— John 6:44
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```bible
“若不是差我来的父吸引人，就没有能到我这里来的；到末日我也要叫他复活。”
—— 约翰福音 6:44
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To claim that God's love is invalid unless conditioned upon autonomous human willingness is to contradict the plain, repeated testimony of Holy Scripture.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

若声称上帝的爱必须以人类自主的意愿为前提才算“真爱”，这直接与圣经明晰而反复的启示相冲突。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. The Eschatological and Theological Paradox: Absolute Holiness in Heaven
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、终末论与真理的悖论：天堂里的绝对圣洁
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

When modern evangelicals define "true love" as requiring the perpetual capacity to choose evil and reject God, they trigger an immediate and catastrophic crisis in their **Eschatology**.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

当现代人本主义神学将“能够选择敌对神”定义为“真爱”的必要条件时，这直接在终末论（Eschatology）与救赎论的核心逻辑上导出了自相矛盾的荒谬结论。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. Is There Freedom to Reject God in Heaven?
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 天堂里还有“拒绝的自由”吗？
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In historic Christian theology and biblical revelation, the glorified saints in the New Jerusalem will **no longer have the possibility of sinning or rejecting God** (*non posse peccare et non posse cadere*). In the state of glory, human nature is permanently solidified in holiness, beholding God face-to-face in the Beatific Vision.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

在大公信仰与圣经的启示中，新天新地里的圣徒将**再无犯罪或拒绝神的可能**（*Non posse peccare*）。在永恒荣耀的状态中，圣徒的意志将在上帝的无限荣耀中被永远坚固在善中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"The will of man is made perfectly and immutably free to good alone, in the state of glory only."
— *Westminster Confession of Faith*, 9.5
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“人唯有在荣耀状态中，其意志才被赋予彻底的、不可改变的善，完全不可能转向恶。”
—— 《威斯敏斯特信条》，第 9 章第 5 条
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Inescapable Logical Absurdity of the Free-Will Premise
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 终末逻辑的反讽与真相
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If the free-will premise were true—that "love is only authentic if one can reject it"—we are left with two fatal conclusions:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

如果按照“必须能拒绝才算真爱”、“不能拒绝就是强迫”的逻辑，我们必然陷入两个荒谬的推论：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

1. **Does God "coerce" the saints in heaven?** If saints in heaven can never choose to reject God, have they lost their "freedom"? Has heaven become a tyranny devoid of true love?
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

1. **难道神在天堂里对圣徒施加了“强迫”吗？** 如果天堂里的圣徒完全失去了拒绝神的可能性，难道他们就失去了“真自由”？难道天堂里的爱反倒降格为不是“真爱”了吗？
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

2. **Or is heaven forever insecure?** If heaven requires the ability to reject God to preserve "genuine love," then eternity is perpetually vulnerable to a second Fall, and Christ's final victory is never secure.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

2. **难道天堂永远危机四伏？** 如果天堂必须保留拒绝神的自由才算有爱，那么永恒国度随时可能爆发第二次堕落，基督的救赎胜利将永远处于不确定之中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Both conclusions are absurd and unbiblical. The profound truth is that **absolute union with God's will is the highest pinnacle of love and freedom**. When God regenerates a sinner on earth, He sovereignly inaugurates that very same heavenly trajectory, freeing the captive will from the suicidal bondage of sin.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这两个结论显然都是极其荒谬且违反圣经的。真理的真相在于：**与上帝的圣洁旨意完全合一，才是自由与爱的最高峰。** 神在今生重生罪人时所施展的大能，正是预尝并启动了这终末的荣耀合一——将受造物从自取灭亡的罪之枷锁中彻底释放出来。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

---

## 5. The Pinnacle of Confessional Orthodoxy: Monergism and True Human Dignity
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、历史大公信条的集大成：单效救恩与真正的尊严
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The historic Christian confessions synthesize the teachings of Scripture, the Church Fathers, and ecumenical councils, providing the most rigorous and pastoral response to the "gentleman God" fallacy.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

历史上的正统信条将圣经、教父与大公信条的教导融会贯通，对这种将上帝降格为无能旁观者的“尊重论”给予了最严密的击破：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Monergistic and Delightful Nature of Regeneration
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 重生的单效性与甘心乐意
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Regeneration is strictly monergistic (*monergism*): It is the unilateral work of God alone. Yet, God's operation upon the human soul is neither mechanical nor violent; it is **supremely powerful, secret, and exquisitely delightful**.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

救恩在重生环节完全是上帝单方面的工作（Monergism），神对罪人意志的改变是**隐秘、温柔且极其大能的**。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Article 12: And this is the regeneration, the new creation, the raising from the dead, and the making alive so effectively proclaimed in the Scriptures, which God works in us without our aid... It is a supernatural work, most powerful, and at the same time most delightful, marvelous, mysterious, and inexpressible... Whereupon the will thus renewed is not only actuated and influenced by God, but in consequence of this influence becomes itself active. Wherefore also, man is himself said to believe and repent, by virtue of that grace received."
— *Canons of Dort*, Third and Fourth Heads of Doctrine, Article 12
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“第12条：这重生绝不是单靠外在的宣讲、劝勉……而是上帝在人心里施展超自然、极其大能、又极其柔和、奇妙、隐秘和不可言喻的工作……由此，被更新的意志不仅被神感动和推动，并且在神的感动下，自己也产生行动。因此，可以说人藉着所承受的恩典，自己产生了相信与悔改。”
—— 《多特信条》，第三与第四要道第 12 条
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

This confessional clarity shatters the false dilemma of coercion: **God does not force the old rebel will to obey against its desires; He resurrects and recreates a new will that passionately desires obedience.**
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这一条文彻底粉碎了“强迫”的虚假二分法：**神不是强迫旧意志去顺服，而是创造了一个渴望顺服的新意志；祂不是强拽死尸，而是赐予活泼的生命。**
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The True Nature of Divine Love and Election
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 爱与神圣拣选的真谛
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Genuine love does not "politely respect" a drowning child's irrational struggle to stay underwater; it plunges in and pulls him to safety. The human race is in total ruin, incapable of desiring divine good apart from sovereign rebirth:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

真正的爱，不是“尊重”一个落井沉溺之人继续自毁，而是一举将他从深渊中拯救出来。全人类陷入彻底的败坏，若非主权的重生，绝无可能向神产生善的渴慕：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

```quote
"Q. But are we so corrupt that we are totally unable to do any good and inclined to all evil?
A. Yes, unless we are regenerated by the Spirit of God."
— *Heidelberg Catechism*, Q&A 8
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

```quote
“问：那么，我们是否陷入彻底的败坏，以致完全无法行任何善，而倾向于万恶吗？
答：是的，除非我们藉着上帝的圣灵重生。”
—— 《海德堡要理问答》，第 8 问
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

God's unconditional election and irresistible grace do not demean human dignity; they restore the true dignity that was shattered in the Fall.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

上帝无条件的拣选与不可抗拒的恩典绝非贬损受造物的尊严，而是彻底医治并重建了在堕落中分崩离析的真正人性尊严。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

---

## 6. Conclusion: Not Coerced Puppets, but Resurrected Sons
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 六、总结：不是胁迫的傀儡，而是死而复活的生命
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The populist slogan "coercion is not true love" pretends to champion God's character and protect human dignity. In reality, it degrades God into an impotent spectator bound by creaturely whims, and abandons dead sinners to the absolute tyranny of their self-destructive depravity.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

“强迫不是真爱”这一论调，看似在维护上帝的爱与人的尊严，实则是将上帝降格为受制于受造物意志的无能者，并将罪人遗弃在他那通往灭亡的“自主性”与罪恶奴役中。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

As historic Christian orthodoxy and the Great Tradition have proclaimed throughout the ages:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

正如大公教会与历史基督教正统信仰所共同宣告的：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

God's redeeming love is not the timid, hands-off "respect" of a spectator; it is the **invincible, door-breaking rescue of the Sovereign King**. He does not coerce us; He **makes us alive**. He does not violate our freedom; He **breaks the shackles of sin and grants us true freedom**.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

上帝的爱不是袖手旁观的“尊重”，而是**破门而入的救赎**。祂没有强迫我们，祂乃是**活化了我们**；祂没有剥夺我们的自由，祂乃是**打破了罪的枷锁，赐给了我们真正的自由**。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Salvation belongs entirely to the Lord: **Grace Alone (*Sola Gratia*), to the Glory of God Alone (*Soli Deo Gloria*)**.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

救恩完全出于耶和华：**唯独恩典（Sola Gratia），唯独上帝得荣耀（Soli Deo Gloria）！**
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
