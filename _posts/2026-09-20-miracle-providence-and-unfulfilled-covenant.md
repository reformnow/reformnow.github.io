> **Abstract:** This article redefines the three most frequently conflated categories when evaluating the 1948 establishment of the State of Israel: biblical miracle (*miraculum*), divine providence (*providentia*), and prophetic fulfillment. Drawing upon Augustine of Hippo, Thomas Aquinas, John Calvin, and B. B. Warfield, it articulates the strict definition, theological significance, and ecclesiastical impact of miracles in historical theology. It subsequently surveys the exegetical history of Romans 11:26 ("all Israel will be saved") across the Early Church, the Reformation, the Puritans, 19th-century Old Princeton, and 20th-century Westminster and Dutch Reformed traditions. Finally, it demonstrates that even while affirming God's ongoing faithfulness to the Jewish people, the 1948 establishment of modern Israel should neither be designated a miracle nor a fulfillment of Romans 11 or Old Testament return prophecies, yet remains rightly understood as an act of God under the doctrine of providence.
> 
> **Keywords:** *miracle; providence; Romans 11:26; all Israel; John Murray; William Hendriksen; Augustine; Calvin; 1948 Israel*
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

> **摘要：** 本文重新界定讨论一九四八年以色列建国时最易混淆的三个范畴：圣经神迹（miracle）、上帝的护理作为（providence），以及预言应验（prophetic fulfillment）。文章首先依据 Augustine of Hippo、Thomas Aquinas、John Calvin 与 B. B. Warfield 等传统，说明神迹在历史神学中的严格定义、意义与影响；同时，接着梳理罗马书十一章二十六节“以色列全家都要得救”自早期教会、宗教改革、清教徒、十九世纪普林斯顿到二十世纪 Westminster 与荷兰改革宗的释义史；最后论证：即便承认上帝对犹太民族的持续信实，一九四八年建国仍不宜称为神迹，亦不宜称为罗马书十一章或旧约回归预言的应验，却仍可在护理教义下称为上帝的作为。
>

## 1. Introduction: Three Non-Interchangeable Judgments
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 一、导论：三个不可互换的判断
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

"Is this a miracle?" and "Is this the fulfillment of biblical prophecy?" are often treated on popular pulpits as if they were the exact same assertion. Historical theology permits no such conflation. A miracle concerns how God attests revelation in a manner transcending the order of nature; prophetic fulfillment concerns whether a specific canonical text has been realized according to its contextual horizon; and providence concerns God's sovereign preservation and government of ordinary history, including events that are extraordinarily unusual yet fully operative through secondary causes.

*(On the distinction between miracle and special providence, see B. B. Warfield, Counterfeit Miracles [New York: Charles Scribner's Sons, 1918], opening chapter).*
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

“这是不是神迹？”与“这是不是圣经预言的应验？”在通俗讲坛上常被当作同一句话。历史神学不允许这种合并。神迹关乎上帝如何以超乎受造秩序的方式印证启示；预言应验关乎某段经文按其正典上下文是否已经兑现；护理则关乎上帝在普通历史中的掌管，包括极不寻常却仍运行于第二因之中的事件。

（关于三者的区分，见 B. B. Warfield, *Counterfeit Miracles* [New York: Charles Scribner’s Sons, 1918]，开篇对 miracle 与 special providence 的划分）。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To label the statehood of May 14, 1948 simultaneously as a miracle, a fulfillment, and an act of providence is to deploy three entirely different theological instruments all at once. When these instruments are blurred, conclusions inevitably polarize: either a modern secular state is inscribed directly into the pages of Isaiah, or the sovereign hand of God is expunged entirely from the history of Jacob's physical descendants.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

把一九四八年五月十四日的立国同时说成神迹、应验与护理，等于一次使用了三种不同的教义工具。工具一乱，结论必然两极化：或把现代主权国家写成以赛亚书，或把上帝从雅各后裔的历史上抹去。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The objective of this survey is to distinguish these tools, and then examine how much weight each can legitimately bear concerning the events of 1948. For this inquiry to succeed, we must first establish a rigorous historical definition of miracle, and then recover the exegetical depth of Romans 11. Without the former, any astounding geopolitical event can be elevated into a modern Pentecost; without the latter, the promise that "all Israel will be saved" will either be contorted into a nationalistic geopolitical timetable or evaporated into a bloodless, abstract ecclesiology.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

本篇综述重点是把工具分开，再问它们各自能承担多少关于一九四八年的判断。为此必须先有神迹的精确定义，再有罗马书十一章释义史的厚度。没有前者，任何惊人历史都可以被升格为五旬节；没有后者，“全家得救”会被任意改写成复国时间表或被蒸发成抽象的教会论。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 2. The Definition of Miracle: From Augustine to Warfield
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 二、神迹的定义：从奥古斯丁到沃菲尔德
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Latin *miraculum* derives from *admiratio* (wonder or astonishment). In *De utilitate credendi*, Augustine of Hippo provided the classical definition:

```quote
"I call that a miracle which, being arduous and unusual, appears beyond the expectation or capacity of the one who marvels at it."
— Augustine of Hippo, De utilitate credendi, 16.34
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

拉丁文 *miraculum* 源于 *admiratio*（惊奇）。Augustine of Hippo 在 *De utilitate credendi* 中给出古典定义：

```quote
“我称之为神迹的，是显得艰难而不寻常、超出惊奇者的预期与能力之事。”
—— 奥古斯丁，《论信心的益处》，16.34
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In his anti-Manichaean writings (*Contra Faustum*), Augustine further added that when God acts contrary to the customary and familiar course of nature, we designate it a miracle. For Augustine, the possibility of a miracle does not imply that God is at war with nature, but rather that the "seminal reasons" (*rationes seminales*) embedded in creation can be activated by the Creator through hidden causes. Both the ordinary daily sunrise and the extraordinary resurrection from the dead ultimately proceed from the very same God; the distinction lies in whether the cause remains hidden from the observer and surpasses the ordinary capacity of the creature.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

他在反摩尼教著作（*Contra Faustum*）中补充道：当上帝所行之事违反我们所习见的自然秩序，我们便称之为神迹。对 Augustine 而言，神迹的可能性不在于上帝与自然为敌，而在于创造时已置于万物中的“种子理性”（*rationes seminales*）可被创造主以隐藏原因唤醒。日常的日出与非常的复活，最终都出于同一上帝；分别在于原因对观者是否隐藏、是否超出受造物的惯常能力。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Thomas Aquinas formalized this definition within scholastic theology. In the *Summa Theologiae*, he pointed out that not every extraordinary work performed by God is termed a miracle. The creation of the world (*creatio ex nihilo*), the creation of individual human souls, and justification are works that God alone can perform, completely transcending the natural chain of causes; yet they are not classified as miracles because they do not interrupt a process that belongs to secondary causes, but fall wholly outside that order from the start.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

Thomas Aquinas 把这一定义经院化。他在 *Summa Theologiae* 中指出：并非上帝所行的一切非常之事都叫神迹。创造世界、创造灵魂、称义，都是唯独上帝能做、且本不属于自然因果链的作为，却不列入神迹，因为它们并非“打断本应由第二因完成的过程”，而是根本不在那一过程之内。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The formal cause of a miracle is that the effect is sensible and visible, while its cause completely surpasses the order of all created nature (*praeter ordinem totius naturae creatae*); its final cause is to confirm the divine truth being proclaimed or to manifest holiness for human imitation. Therefore, Aquinas rigorously distinguished three categories of "marvels": deceptive illusions; extraordinary phenomena produced by created powers (including demons manipulating nature); and true miracles wrought exclusively by God for the salvation and spiritual benefit of mankind. Only the third category is worthy of the name *miraculum*.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

神迹的形式因是：效果可见，而原因超越整个受造自然的秩序（*praeter ordinem totius naturae creatae*）；其目的因是：印证所宣讲的真理，或显明上帝要人效法的圣洁。因此 Aquinas 严格区分三种“奇事”：幻象欺骗；由受造力量（包括邪灵利用自然）造成的非常现象；以及唯独上帝为救人益处而行的真神迹。只有第三类配得 *miraculum* 之名。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The Reformation did not discard this classical definition, but tethered it firmly to the Divine Word. John Calvin emphasized that miracles are seals given by God to confirm the proclamation of the gospel, their historical occurrence being concentrated in eras of new revelation, rather than functioning as endlessly repeatable spectacles throughout ordinary church history. The theological order of Christ's Ascension and the descent of the Holy Spirit is especially crucial in Calvin's thought: Christ departed according to the flesh precisely so that by His Spirit He might apply His glorified life to all places; that visible wonders give way to the inscribed Word and internal regenerating grace does not signify that God has ceased to act, but that special redemptive revelation has attained its organic completion.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

宗教改革并未抛弃这一定义，而是把它绑紧在圣道上。John Calvin 强调：神迹是上帝用来印证福音宣讲的印记，其时代集中于启示的颁赐，而不是教会历史上可无限复制的奇观。升天与差灵的秩序在他那里尤其重要：基督按肉身离开，正是为了以圣灵把得荣之生命施行于万处；可见的非常之事让位于成文之道与内在恩典，并不表示上帝停止作为，而是启示已经达到有机的完成。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

At Old Princeton, B. B. Warfield gave the Reformed doctrine of miracles its sharpest articulation in *Counterfeit Miracles* (1918). He argued that miracles in the strict sense (*miracle proper*) are direct acts of God without created secondary mediation, inextricably bound to periods of revelation to testify to His commissioned messengers and their message; miracles in Scripture do not appear randomly, but cluster exclusively during eras when God speaks authoritatively to His covenant people through His emissaries.

```quote
"Miracles do not appear on the page of Scripture vagrantly, here, there, and elsewhere indifferently, without assignable reason. They belong to revelation periods, and have for their purpose the authentication of the bearers of new revelation."
— B. B. Warfield, Counterfeit Miracles, p. 25
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

到了老普林斯顿，B. B. Warfield 在 *Counterfeit Miracles*（1918）中把改革宗神迹论说得最为锋利。他主张：严格意义上的神迹是上帝不经受造媒介的直接作为，并且隶属于启示时期，为要为受差的使者及其信息作见证；圣经中的神迹并非随意散落，而只出现在上帝藉蒙差遣者向百姓说话的时代。

```quote
“神迹并非随意散落在圣经的篇页上，漫无边际、随处出现；它们严格属于启示时期，其目的正是为了印证新启示的传递者。”
—— B. B. 沃菲尔德，《伪神迹》，第25页
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Warfield thus rigorously distinguished *miracle proper* from *special providence*, answered prayer, and extraordinary preservative grace. The latter may rightly cause the pious soul to tremble with thanksgiving and awe, yet they possess no revelatory function of authenticating new revelation. Because special revelation reached canonical completion in the apostolic age, the miraculous office intended to authenticate new revelation ceased. Whether one embraces every nuance of Warfield's cessationism or not, mainstream orthodox theology affirms his category distinction: one cannot term every "statistically improbable or geopolitically shocking event" a miracle.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

因此他区分 *miracle proper* 与 *special providence*（特殊护理）、祷告蒙应允、恩典中的非常保守。后者尽可以令敬虔之心战兢，却不具有“印证新启示”的神迹功能。启示既在使徒时代达到正典的完成，为印证新启示而存在的神迹职事便止息。无论是否完全接受 Warfield 的终止论细节，主流正统神学都承认他所作的范畴区分：不能把一切“难以用统计学解释的历史”都称为神迹。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Synthesizing the above, the historic orthodox definition of a miracle contains at least five indispensable elements:

1. **Author:** God alone is the primary cause (demonic powers can work lying wonders, but cannot work true miracles).
2. **Relation to Nature:** It transcends the ordinary power and sequence of the entire created order, rather than merely exceeding an observer's finite knowledge.
3. **Knowability:** Its effects are publicly manifest, while its primary cause is hidden from created human reason.
4. **Purpose:** To authenticate divine revelation or demonstrate divine holiness for the redemptive benefit of man, never as a gratuitous display of raw power.
5. **Criterion of Discernment:** It must harmonize with the already revealed Holy Scripture; marvels tethered to erroneous or apostate doctrines, even if empirically observed, are not true miracles.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

综合以上，正统定义至少包含五要素：

1. **作者：** 唯独上帝（邪灵可造奇事，不能造真神迹）。
2. **与自然的关系：** 超出整个受造秩序的惯常能力与进程，而非仅仅超出某观察者的知识。
3. **可知性：** 效果公开，第一因对受造理性隐藏。
4. **目的：** 印证启示或显明圣洁，为人的益处，而非炫耀权力。
5. **辨伪：** 须与已赐的圣道一致；与错谬教义绑在一起的奇事，即使“真发生”，也不是真神迹。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The theological meaning of a miracle is therefore epistemological and soteriological, rather than nationalistic. It announces to humanity that the God who speaks has the sovereign right to enter His creation; the central focal point toward which it points is the Incarnation, the Cross, and the Resurrection, rather than the political fortunes of any secular nation-state.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

神迹的意义因此首先是认识论与救赎论的，而不是爱国主义的。它告诉人：这位说话的上帝有权进入祂所造的世界；它所指向的中心是道成肉身、十字架与复活，而不是民族命运的每一次转折。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The impact of miracles in church history is twofold: on the one hand, they strengthen faith amid mission and martyrdom; on the other hand, once the definition is loosened, holy relics, military victories, battlefield survivals, and political independence dates become elevated to "divine signatures," causing public theology to lose its prophetic critical distance. Augustine himself warned that if believers become habituated to seeking visible wonders, true faith grows cold (*De vera religione*, 25.47). This historical insight is exceptionally pertinent when evaluating 1948.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

神迹的影响在教会史上是双重的：一方面坚固宣教与殉道中的信心；另一方面，一旦定义松动，圣物、政治胜利、战争幸存、建国日期都会被升格为“上帝签字”，公共神学便失去批判距离。Augustine 自己就曾担心：人若习惯于可见奇事，信心反而冷淡（*De vera religione*, 25.47）。这对讨论一九四八年尤为重要。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 3. The Boundary Between Providence and Miracle: Why an "Act" Is Not a "Miracle"
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 三、护理与神迹的边界：为何“作为”不等于“神迹”
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In John Calvin's doctrine of providence, not a single drop of rain falls, nor does any nation rise or fall, outside the sovereign will of God (*Institutes*, 1.16.5). Yet providence operates precisely *through* secondary causes—diplomacy, warfare, migration, United Nations resolutions, and military organization. Special providence can be extraordinarily "unlike coincidence"—such as a small, scattered people surviving the Holocaust, establishing a sovereign state, and defending its borders against overwhelming odds.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

John Calvin 的护理论认为，无一滴雨、无一国兴衰在上帝之外（*Institutes*, 1.16.5）。然而护理正是透过第二因——战争、外交、移民、联合国决议、军事组织——而工作。特殊护理可以极度“不像巧合”，例如一小民族在大屠杀之后立国并存活。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Louis Berkhof divides divine providence into three aspects: preservation (*conservatio*), concurrence (*concursus*), and government (*gubernatio*). An astonishing event in history represents an intensive manifestation of divine government, but does not automatically change its metaphysical category.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

Louis Berkhof 将护理分为保守（*conservatio*）、协同（*concursus*）与治理（*gubernatio*）；惊人的历史属于治理的强度问题，不自动改变其类别。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Therefore, the following theological taxonomy must be maintained:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

因此可作如下分级：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

| Category | Definition | Example | 1948 Israel? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General Providence** | God sustaining ordinary course through secondary causes | Seasons, birth, rise and fall of nations | Subsumed within it |
| **Special Providence** | God accomplishing His counsel through extraordinary combinations of secondary causes | Cyrus's decree; post-diaspora national preservation | Validly evaluated as such |
| **Strict Miracle** | Transcending created order; serving to authenticate revelation | Parting of the Red Sea; the Resurrection; Pentecost tongues as sign | Inapplicable |
| **Prophetic Fulfillment** | Canonical scripture realized according to its context | Return after seventy years of exile; Christ's death and resurrection | Requires separate proof (see Sections 4–5) |
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

| 类别 | 定义 | 举例 | 一九四八年？ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **普通护理** | 上帝以第二因维持常态 | 季节、生育、国家兴衰 | 含于其中 |
| **特殊护理** | 上帝以非常组合的第二因成就其旨意 | 古列下诏；散居后的民族存续 | 可以如此判断 |
| **严格神迹** | 超乎受造秩序、为印证启示 | 过红海；复活；五旬节方言作为记号 | 不宜 |
| **预言应验** | 正典经文按其上下文兑现 | 被掳七十年后归回；基督的死而复活 | 须另证，见第四、五节 |
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Aquinas already reminded us: the justification of the ungodly is a work of "God alone" far greater than many outward miracles (*Summa Theologiae*, I-II, q. 113, a. 9), yet it is not called a miracle in the strict sense. By the same token, God can sovereignly preserve the physical descendants of Abraham without transforming the geopolitical event of 1948 into a duplicate of the Exodus. 
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

Aquinas 已提醒：称义比许多神迹更“唯独上帝”（*Summa Theologiae*, I-II, q. 113, a. 9），却不叫神迹。同理，上帝可以保存亚伯拉罕的后裔，而不使一九四八年成为出埃及的副本。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

To classify the founding of modern Israel as a miracle carries three grave theological consequences: it inscribes a secular political act that denies Jesus Christ into the category of a revelatory seal; it relocates miracles from the Word of God to secular geopolitics; and it casts an illegitimate aura of divine sacrosanctity over subsequent wars and state policies. Historic orthodox theology rejects these conclusions, not because it denies the sovereign hand of God, but because it refuses to debase and misuse the term "miracle."
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

称立国为神迹，将产生三种教义后果：把不认基督的政治行为写成启示印记；把神迹从圣道挪到地缘；使后续战争与政策都获得不当的神圣光环。改革宗神学拒绝这些后果，不是因为否认上帝的手，而是因为不肯滥用神迹一词。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 4. The Historical Theology of Romans 11: "All Israel Will Be Saved"
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 四、罗马书十一章“以色列全家得救”的历史神学
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

If there is any biblical text most frequently commandeered to provide theological endorsement for 1948—or, conversely, used to entirely abolish any future for the Jewish people—it is Romans 11:25–27:

```bible
"For I do not want you, brethren, to be uninformed of this mystery—so that you will not be wise in your own estimation—that a partial hardening has happened to Israel until the fullness of the Gentiles has come in; and so all Israel will be saved; just as it is written: 'The Deliverer will come from Zion, He will remove ungodliness from Jacob.'"
— Romans 11:25–26 (NASB)
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

若有经文被最频繁地拉来为一九四八年背书，或被反过来用来彻底取消犹太民族的未来，那就是罗马书十一章二十五至二十七节：

```bible
“弟兄们，我不愿意你们不知道这奥秘（恐怕你们自以为聪明），就是以色列人有几分是硬心的，等到外邦人的数目添满了，于是以色列全家都要得救。如经上所记：‘必有一位救主从锡安出来，要消除雅各家的一切罪恶。’”
—— 罗马书 11:25–26（和合本）
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Historical theology demonstrates that the Church's understanding of this passage has never been monolithic, nor can it be captured by the modern invention of "statehood = prophetic fulfillment."
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

历史神学显示：教会对这节经文的理解从来不是单一的，也从来不是“复国＝应验”的现代发明所能概括。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 1. The Early Church: Remnant, True Israel, and the Fullness of the Gentiles
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 1. 早期教会：余民、真以色列与外邦丰满
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Irenaeus of Lyons emphasized that all who fear God and flee to Christ from Israel are saved, linking the apostolic mission to the "lost sheep of the house of Israel" (*Against Heresies*, 3.12.7). Origen of Alexandria discerned a temporal order: Israel according to the flesh is hardened until the fullness of the Gentiles enters, after which "all Israel" shall be saved (*Commentary on Romans*). Ambrosiaster demonstrated from the citation of Isaiah that divine covenant mercy had not been exhausted.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

Irenaeus of Lyons 强调凡敬畏上帝、投奔基督的以色列人都已得救，并把宣教命令连于“以色列家迷失的羊”（*Against Heresies*, 3.12.7）。Origen of Alexandria 则读出时间顺序：按肉体的以色列暂时刚硬如石，直到外邦人的丰满进来，然后“全家”也要得救（*Commentary on Romans*）。Ambrosiaster 藉以赛亚书的引文证明恩典尚未耗尽。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Augustine of Hippo exerted a lasting influence. On the one hand, he maintained that not all Jews were blinded, for an elect remnant had recognized Christ; on the other hand, he interpreted the "truer Israel" as the totality of the elect called from both Jews and Gentiles (*City of God*, 20.29; *Contra Faustum*, 12.24). In his anti-Pelagian and predestinarian polemics, Romans 9–11 was marshaled primarily to defend the absolute sovereignty of grace, submerging ethnic historical drama into the ecclesiological category of "the sum of the elect." This reading paved the way for medieval and certain Reformed interpretations equating "the whole people of God = Israel." It safeguarded "one people, one salvation," yet diminished the chronological terminal sense implied in 11:25 ("until... the fullness of the Gentiles has come in").
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

Augustine of Hippo 的处理具有长远影响。他一方面坚持并非所有犹太人都眼瞎，其中已有人认出基督；另一方面把“更真的以色列”理解为从犹太人与外邦人中蒙召的选民（*City of God*, 20.29; *Contra Faustum*, 12.24）。在反伯拉纠与预定论的语境里，罗马书第九至十一章首先被用来保卫恩典的主权，族群未来的历史戏剧往往被收进“选民总和”的教会论。这一读法为中世纪和部分改革宗“全体上帝子民＝以色列”的解释预备了道路。它保护了“一民、一救恩”，却减弱了十一章二十五节“直到……外邦人的丰满”所含的历史终点感。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 2. The Reformation: Calvin's "Israel of God" and an Underestimated Nuance
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 2. 宗教改革：Calvin 的“上帝的以色列”与被低估的另一面
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In his commentary on Romans, John Calvin extended the "Israel" of Romans 11:26 to encompass the entire people of God: as the Gentiles are grafted in, and the Jews return from their apostasy to the obedience of faith, the salvation of the "entire Israel of God" is consummated; the Jews, as the firstborn in the covenant family, retain their primacy of honor (*Commentary on Romans*, ad loc.). He did not deny that Jews would turn to Christ, but refused to interpret "all Israel" as the ethnic mass of a single future generation segregated from the Church. 

Martin Luther, in his early lectures on Romans, focused primarily on justification by faith and the law, adopting a complex attitude toward the future corporate conversion of the Jewish people mingled with polemical remarks, which cannot be lightly co-opted by modern schools.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

John Calvin 在罗马书注释中把十一章二十六节的以色列扩展为全体上帝之民：外邦人进来的同时，犹太人从背道归回信仰的顺服，于是“整个上帝的以色列”之拯救完成；犹太人作为家中长子仍可居首位（*Commentary on Romans*）。他并非否认犹太人将归回信仰，而是拒绝把“全家”读成某一未来世代的民族整体，与教会分开。Martin Luther 早期对罗马书九至十一章的关注集中于因信称义与律法，对犹太人的未来民族归正态度复杂，且夹杂论战性言论，不宜被现代任何一派轻易征用。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

It must be underscored that Calvin's ecclesiological reading of verse 26 was by no means the unanimous consensus of the Reformed tradition. From the very beginning, a theological tension existed: Romans 9:6 ("they are not all Israel who are descended from Israel") seemed to favor a spiritualizing ecclesiology, whereas Romans 11:25 juxtaposed "Israel" with "Gentiles," demanding an ethnic referent. This internal tension formed the central axis of exegetical history for the next four centuries.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

必须指出：Calvin 对二十六节的“全体上帝子民”读法，并不是此后全部改革宗的定论。改革宗内部从一开始就存在张力：九章六节“不都是以色列”似乎支持属灵化；十一章二十五节“以色列”与“外邦人”对举，又似乎要求族群义。这一张力构成后续四百年释义史的主轴。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 3. The Puritans and Dutch Reformed: Large-Scale Conversion in History
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 3. 清教徒与荷兰改革宗：历史中的大规模归正
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

In the seventeenth century, the vast majority of English Puritans and Dutch *nadere reformatie* theologians adopted a third position: "all Israel" denotes neither every individual Jew nor an abstract Gentile church, but the Jewish nation as a corporate whole (*the mass of the nation*) turning to Christ in a future generation. Iain H. Murray in *The Puritan Hope* (1971) demonstrates that this expectation was intimately bound to their revival theology and postmillennial missionary zeal, entirely distinct from modern political Zionism. Herman Witsius devoted an extensive section in *The Economy of the Covenants between God and Man* to the restoration of the Jews, viewing it as a glorious historical manifestation of the Covenant of Grace. Wilhelmus à Brakel in *The Christian's Reasonable Service* likewise anticipated a corporate turning of ethnic Israel. The marginal note in the *Geneva Bible* on Romans 11:15 interpreted "life from the dead" as an immense spiritual resurrection, rather than a bodily one.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

十七世纪英语清教徒与荷兰 *gereformeerd* 神学家多数采取第三种立场：不是每一个犹太人，也不是抽象的教会，而是将来某一世代中犹太民族作为整体（*the mass of the nation*）归向基督。Iain H. Murray 在 *The Puritan Hope*（1971）中表明，这一盼望与他们的复兴论、普世宣教热情相连，而不是与现代锡安主义国家工程相连。Herman Witsius 在 *The Economy of the Covenants* 中专论犹太人的复兴，视其为恩典之约在历史中的荣耀展示。Wilhelmus à Brakel 等荷兰改革宗同样期待犹太民族的归正。日内瓦圣经在十一章十五节的旁注，将“从死里复活”解为巨大的属灵苏醒，而非身体复活。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

A critical boundary in this tradition is often overlooked by twentieth-century readers: the *restoration* expected by the Puritans was an ingathering into Christ and His Church, not the rebuilding of the Levitical temple or a political nation-state founded in unbelief. They could fervently long for "Zion's glory" while steadfastly maintaining that there is only one Mediator, one covenant, and one olive tree.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这一传统的关键限制常被二十世纪读者忽略：清教徒所说的 *restoration* 是归入基督、归入教会，不是恢复摩西礼仪、也不是以不信为前提的政治复国。他们可以热切盼望“锡安的荣耀时期”，同时坚持只有一位中保、一棵橄榄树。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 4. The 19th and 20th Centuries: Hodge, Berkhof, Hendriksen, and Murray
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 4. 十九至二十世纪：Hodge、Berkhof、Hendriksen、Murray
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Charles Hodge, in his monumental commentary on Romans (1896), argued that the Apostle here predicts an extensive, conspicuous future conversion of the Jewish people, occurring after the fullness of the Gentiles has been brought in; "all Israel" follows Jewish idiom denoting the nation as a collective entity rather than every single individual (*Commentary on Romans*, p. 584). This stood in direct continuity with the Puritans, explicitly defining salvation as faith in Christ, not possession of geographic land.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

Charles Hodge 在罗马书注释中主张：使徒在此预言犹太民族将来有一次广泛而显著的归正，发生在外邦人的丰满进来之后；“全家”按犹太文献习惯指整体而非每一个体（*Commentary on Romans*, 1896, p. 584）。这与清教徒一线相承，且明确把得救定义为信基督，而非得地。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Louis Berkhof, writing in his 1938 *Systematic Theology*, acknowledged that both the Old and New Testaments anticipate a future conversion of Israel, yet asked pointedly: Does the New Testament require that Israel be restored *as a political nation*? He expressed strong skepticism, demonstrating that passages like Matthew 19:28 and Luke 21:24 by no means necessitate a geopolitical restoration (*Systematic Theology*, p. 698). Berkhof wrote a decade before 1948. Following the establishment of the state, dispensationalists frequently cited 1948 as evidence that amillennialists had "misread history." Yet that critique only holds if one presupposes that "return to the land = prophetic fulfillment." If the biblical definition of fulfillment remains the gospel conversion of Romans 11:26, a secular sovereign state has by no means fulfilled the text.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

Louis Berkhof 在一九三八年 *Systematic Theology* 中承认旧约与新约都提到以色列将来的归正，却问：新约是否要求把以色列“作为国家”予以重建？他对此表示怀疑，并认为马太福音十九章二十八节、路加福音二十一章二十四节并不必然蕴含民族国家的复兴（*Systematic Theology*, 1938, p. 698）。伯克富写于一九四八年之前。立国之后，时代论者常以此证明无千禧年论“看走眼”。这一批评只有在“回到土地＝预言应验”的前提下才成立；若应验的定义仍是十一章二十六节那种福音性归正，则土地上的主权国家并未自动兑现经文。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

William Hendriksen in *Israel in Prophecy* (1968) took an even firmer line: Old Testament restoration prophecies intended for literal fulfillment were already fulfilled in the post-exilic return from Babylon; the remainder find their true realization in Christ and the Church; a secular state established in 1948 in unbelief "has nothing to do with biblical prophecy." Hendriksen did not say God had cast off the Jewish people, but categorically refused to enter a secular state into the column of prophetic fulfillment. 

Other theologians, such as Herman Bavinck (*Reformed Dogmatics*, Vol. 4), Anthony A. Hoekema (*The Bible and the Future*), and O. Palmer Robertson (*The Israel of God*), interpreted "all Israel" as the totality of elect Jewish remnants gathered across all generations, rather than a single eschatological generation. The Reformed tradition thus divided into the "future mass conversion" and "cumulative elect remnant" schools—yet both united in rejecting the equation of 1948 with Romans 11:26.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

William Hendriksen 在 *Israel in Prophecy*（1968）中采取更硬的路线：旧约回归预言中宜按字面实现者，已在从巴比伦归回时实现；其余在基督与教会中成全；一九四八年在不信中立国“与神圣预言毫无关系”。他并不说上帝已弃绝犹太人，而是拒绝把不信中的复国写入应验栏。Herman Bavinck（*Reformed Dogmatics*, Vol. 4）、Anthony A. Hoekema（*The Bible and the Future*）、O. Palmer Robertson（*The Israel of God*）等则较多把“全家”解为历代蒙拣选的犹太余民之总和，而非未来某一世代的民族群众。改革宗内部在此分裂为“未来群众归正”与“历代余民总和”两支，但两支都反对把一九四八年等同于十一章二十六节。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

John Murray's commentary, *The Epistle to the Romans* (1959–1965), became the twentieth century's most influential scholarly formulation of the "future mass conversion" view. Murray decisively rejected Calvin's identification of "Israel" in verse 26 as the entire church, arguing that the antithesis between *Israel* and *Gentiles* throughout chapter 11 forbids an abrupt shift in vocabulary. Murray's classic assertion is that "all Israel will be saved" must be interpreted in light of the chapter's grand theme—the restoration of ethnic Israel:

```quote
"It is exegetically impossible to give 'Israel' in this verse any other connotation than that which belongs to it throughout this chapter... It is the restoration of Israel to covenant favor and blessing... 'All Israel' is not necessarily each and every Israelite, but Israel as a whole, a mass conversion of Israel in contrast with the present remnant."
— John Murray, The Epistle to the Romans, II:98–100
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

John Murray 的 *The Epistle to the Romans*（1959–1965）成为二十世纪改革宗中“未来群众归正”最有影响力的学术表述。Murray 力排 Calvin 将二十六节以色列等同全体教会的做法，指出十一章中 Israel 与 Gentiles 的对举不容突然改义。他的经典主张是：必须按本章主题——以色列的复兴——来读“全家都要得救”，即“丰满、收纳、接上”，是“以色列作为一民重新蒙福音恩惠”，是“群众的得救”；其规模须与先前的过犯、丢弃、折断、刚硬在相反方向上相称。

```quote
“在解经上，给这一节中的‘以色列’赋予不同于整章贯穿之涵义是完全不可能的……这是以色列重新蒙受圣约恩惠与祝福的复兴……‘以色列全家’不必然指每一个以色列个体，而是指作为整体的以色列，即与现今的余民相对照的以色列群众性归正。”
—— 约翰·慕理，《罗马书注释》，卷二，第98–100页
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

For Murray, "life from the dead" (Rom 11:15) signifies not a literal physical resurrection, but a monumental spiritual revival impacting the world—aligning with the Puritans while rejecting dispensational tribulation timelines. Contemporary evangelical commentators like Douglas J. Moo and Thomas R. Schreiner closely follow Murray exegetically, while firmly defining salvation as faith in the Deliverer from Zion. 

Geerhardus Vos, in *The Pauline Eschatology* (1930), stressed that the climax of Romans 11 is anchored in the eschatological turning of redemptive history, rather than deciphering secular political incidents of the church age. R. C. Sproul similarly inclined toward a future mass conversion, yet without becoming a Christian Zionist. J. Gresham Machen did not live to see 1948; his focus was on the battle for the gospel against liberalism, leaving no commentary endorsing political restoration as prophetic fulfillment. Jonathan Edwards, George Whitefield, John Owen, and J. I. Packer held a Puritan hope for the conversion of the Jews, yet their textual foundation was faith in Christ, not a secular declaration of independence.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

“从死里复活”（罗 11:15）他不取身体复活之义，而取对世界而言的巨大属灵苏醒，近清教徒而远时代论的患难时间表。Douglas J. Moo、Thomas R. Schreiner 等当代福音派注释家在解经上多靠近 Murray，同时仍把得救定义为信靠那从锡安出来的救赎主。

Geerhardus Vos 从保罗末世论强调：十一章的高潮仍在救赎历史的终末转向，而不是把教会时代的任何政治事件当作该奥秘的揭晓。R. C. Sproul 在通俗改革宗教学中亦倾向未来大规模归正，却不因此成为基督教锡安主义者。J. Gresham Machen 未及见一九四八年；其重点在福音与自由派之争，并未留下将复国写入罗马书的注释。Jonathan Edwards、George Whitefield、John Owen、J. I. Packer 对犹太人归主可以有清教徒式的盼望，其文本依据仍是信心与基督，不是一九四八年的主权宣言。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

### 5. The Common Denominator of Exegetical History
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

### 5. 释义史的公约数
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Historical theology dismantles two pervasive myths: that orthodox theology has always "spiritually dissolved" ethnic Israel, or that it has always been anticipating 1948. The accurate historical picture consists of three coexisting trajectories:

1. **The Whole People of God Trajectory (Augustine–Calvin):** "All Israel" = the total sum of the elect, comprised of the Jewish remnant and Gentile believers.
2. **The Cumulative Remnant Trajectory (Bavinck–Hendriksen–Hoekema):** "All Israel" = all elect ethnic Jews, gathered cumulatively across history.
3. **The Future Mass Conversion Trajectory (Puritans–Hodge–Murray–Moo):** "All Israel" = following the fullness of the Gentiles, ethnic Israel turns corporately as a mass to faith in Christ.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

历史神学不允许一种神话：正统神学从来都把以色列“属灵化掉了”，或从来都在等待一九四八年。更真实的图景是三条线并存：

1. **全体上帝子民线（偏 Augustine–Calvin）：** 全家＝选民总和，含犹太余民与外邦信徒。
2. **历代余民总和线（偏 Bavinck–Hendriksen–Hoekema）：** 全家＝所有蒙拣选的犹太人，历时完成。
3. **未来群众归正线（偏清教徒–Hodge–Murray–Moo）：** 全家＝外邦丰满之后，族群以色列作为整体归向基督。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

All three trajectories share an immutable common denominator: **salvation is exclusively through Jesus Christ turning ungodliness from Jacob (Rom 11:26–27); there is only one olive tree, and there is no alternate, gospel-less track of ethnic salvation.** Therefore, regardless of which trajectory one adopts, the establishment of modern Israel in 1948—as the birth of a secular state that does not acknowledge Christ—cannot be designated the fulfillment of Romans 11:26. What the third trajectory awaits is precisely the fulfillment of verse 23: *"if they do not continue in their unbelief."* The political founding of 1948 did not fulfill that covenantal condition.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

三条线有一共同边界：**得救是藉耶稣基督脱离不虔（罗 11:26–27），橄榄树是同一棵，没有第二条不经福音的民族救赎轨道。** 因此，无论取哪一条，一九四八年作为不认基督的现代国家之诞生，都无法被称作罗马书十一章二十六节的应验。第三条线所等待的，恰恰是十一章二十三节的条件：“他们若不是长久住在不信里。”立国并未满足该条件。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 5. Returning to 1948: Miracle? Act? Fulfillment?
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 五、回到一九四八年：神迹？作为？应验？
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

When we superimpose the theological definition of miracle from Section 2 onto the exegetical history of Section 4, our conclusions regarding 1948 converge with clarity.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

将第二节的神迹定义与第四节的释义史叠在一九四八年上，结论可以收敛。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

**Regarding Miracle:** The establishment of the modern State of Israel was accomplished through diplomacy, mass immigration, military conflicts, and international resolutions. It is a historical event saturated with secondary causes. It is not an act in the sense of Aquinas and Warfield—one transcending the entire created order to authenticate new revelation. It carried no apostolic seal of the Word, nor does its founding charter point to the Triune God's redemption in Jesus Christ. In terms of Augustine's psychological *admiratio*, human observers may indeed marvel; but under Warfield's theological categories, human wonder does not equal a miracle. If 1948 is deemed a miracle, then any twentieth-century war of independence or miraculous military survival could claim the same title, rendering the theological term bankrupt.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

**就神迹而言：** 立国经由外交、移民潮、战争与国际组织完成，属于第二因高度密集的历史，并非 Aquinas–Warfield 意义上“超出整个受造秩序、为印证新启示”的作为。它没有使徒性的圣道印记功能，立国文本也不指向三一上帝在基督里的救赎。按 Augustine 的“惊奇”心理，人可以惊奇；按 Warfield 的范畴，惊奇不等于神迹。若称其为神迹，则二十世纪任何民族独立与军事幸存都可竞相领取同一头衔，神迹一词便告破产。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

**Regarding Divine Providence:** Romans 11:1–2 and 28–29 remain in full theological force: God has not rejected His people whom He foreknew; from the standpoint of God's choice they are beloved for the sake of the fathers, for the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable. That an ethnic people, after nearly two millennia of diaspora and horrific extermination campaigns, preserved their identity and established a homeland can and should be affirmed by historical orthodoxy: *this did not occur outside the sovereign hand of God.* The logical prerequisite that allows John Murray to contemplate a future corporate conversion is that the ethnic people still exists. Designating 1948 an act of special providence requires no prior passage through a "prophetic fulfillment test."
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

**就护理作为而言：** 罗马书十一章一至二节、二十八至二十九节仍然有效：上帝没有弃绝预先知道的百姓，为列祖的缘故他们是蒙爱的，恩赐与选召没有后悔。一个民族在近两千年散居与种族灭绝企图之后仍保持认同并建立国家，正统神学可以、也应该说：这不是在上帝之外发生的。John Murray 之所以还能认真讨论“将来的群众得救”，逻辑前提正是这民族还在。称一九四八年为特殊护理中的作为，并不需要先通过“应验审查”。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

**Regarding Prophetic Fulfillment:** Ezekiel 36–37 inextricably links the physical return to the land with the bestowal of a new heart and the indwelling Holy Spirit; the temple vision of Ezekiel 40–48 was given after the destruction of the first temple, its cosmic dimensions and river of life never materializing in the Second Temple, much less in a secular parliament building in modern Jerusalem. Isaiah 66:8 ("Shall a land be born in one day?") serves the canonical theology of return and Zion giving birth to her children, and cannot be uncritically mapped onto the 1948 Declaration of Independence. The mystery unveiled in Romans 11 concerns a temporary hardening and the divine manner of saving Israel after the fullness of the Gentiles, not a timetable of UN resolutions. It was not accidental that F. F. Bruce concluded his history of Israel at AD 70: the prophetic clock of the New Testament canon does not tick to the beat of modern nation-states. George Eldon Ladd similarly recognized God's preservation of the Jewish people while demanding that Old Testament land and kingdom language be re-read through the Christ-event.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

**就预言应验而言：** 以西结三十六至三十七章把归回与新心、圣灵进入连写；以西结四十至四十八章的圣殿异象发于圣殿被毁之后，其尺度与生命河从未落实为第二圣殿，更非现代议会建筑。以赛亚六十六章八节的“一国是否一日而生”在正典中首先服务于归回与锡安产子的神学，不能无媒介地等于一九四八年独立日。罗马书十一章所揭的奥秘，是部分刚硬有期限、外邦丰满之后以色列蒙拯救的方式，不是联合国决议的日期。F. F. Bruce 将以色列民族史写到公元七十年为止，不是偶然：新约正典的预言时钟，在他的历史方法里，不以现代国家为下一格。George Eldon Ladd 承认上帝保存这民族，却要求旧约土地与国度语言经过基督事件重读。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Therefore, four precise, simultaneous judgments must be affirmed:

1. **1948 is not a miracle in the strict theological sense;**
2. **1948 can be recognized as an act and extraordinary preservation within God's sovereign providence;**
3. **1948 is not the fulfillment of Romans 11:26;**
4. **1948 is not the consummation of the Ezekiel-style return-and-new-heart prophecy.**
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

因此，精确的四句判断是：

1. **一九四八年不是严格神迹；**
2. **一九四八年可以是上帝护理中的作为与非常保守；**
3. **一九四八年不是罗马书十一章二十六节的应验；**
4. **一九四八年不是以西结式回归—新心预言的完成式。**
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

These four statements stand together coherently. The enduring value of historical theology lies precisely in equipping believers to articulate all four simultaneously, refusing to let pulpit rhetoric force them into a false either-or dilemma.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

这四句可以同时成立。历史神学的价值正在于训练人同时说出它们，而不被讲坛修辞逼成非此即彼。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

## 6. Significance and Implications: How the Church Speaks After the Definition Tightens
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

## 六、意义与影响：定义收紧之后，教会当如何言说
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

Once the definition of a miracle is properly tightened, its practical implications for the life and witness of the Church are at least fourfold:
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

神迹定义一旦收紧，对教会的影响至少有四：
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

**First, Christian worship is safeguarded from being hijacked by geopolitical events.** The miracles of Pentecost point solely to the exalted, glorified Christ (Acts 2:33). The worship of the Church must remain centered on Word, Sacrament, and the proclamation of the gospel to all nations, rather than hosting annual prophetic celebrations around modern geopolitical anniversaries.

```bible
"Therefore having been exalted to the right hand of God, and having received from the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, He has poured forth this which you both see and hear."
— Acts 2:33 (NASB)
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

**第一，敬拜不被事件劫持。** 五旬节的神迹指向得荣的基督（徒 2:33）；教会的中心仍是圣道、圣礼与向万民传福音，而不是每年为独立日举行预言纪念。

```bible
“他既被神的右手高举，又从父受了所应许的圣灵，就把你们所看见所听见的，浇灌下来。”
—— 使徒行传 2:33（和合本）
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

**Second, antisemitism and racial hostility are stripped of theological pretexts.** Denying prophetic fulfillment or strict miracle status to 1948 by no means denies Romans 11:28 ("beloved for the sake of the fathers"). William Hendriksen, in the final chapter of *Israel in Prophecy*, fervently called Christians to show genuine gospel love and an unceasing sense of spiritual indebtedness toward the Jewish people (*Israel in Prophecy*, pp. 60–63).

**Third, secular state politics are not given illegitimate baptism.** Divine preservation in providence does not render territorial occupation, warfare, or domestic legislation into "holy crusades."

**Fourth, eschatological hope is not cancelled.** Those who walk the Hodge–Murray line will continue to pray for that glorious corporate turning of Israel that will be as "life from the dead"; those who walk the cumulative remnant line will continue to proclaim the unadulterated gospel of Christ to Jews in every generation. Both paths remain far more faithful to biblical and historical theology than prematurely inscribing 1948 into Romans 11:26.
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

**第二，反犹与仇视被切断神学借口。** 否定应验与神迹，并不否定十一章二十八节的“蒙爱”。Hendriksen 在同一本小书末章仍要求基督徒对犹太人存福音的爱与亏欠感（*Israel in Prophecy*, pp. 60–63）。

**第三，政治不被洗礼。** 护理中的存留不使占领、战争或立法成为圣战。

**第四，盼望不被取消。** 走 Hodge–Murray 路线的人，仍当祈求那将来与“从死里复活”相称的归正；走余民总和路线的人，仍当在一切世代向犹太人传同一福音。两条路都比把一九四八年写成十一章二十六节更忠于释义史。
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}

The ultimate meaning of a miracle is to reveal Jesus Christ. Any "marvel" that fails to lead sinners to the co-essential, co-glorious Son of God, or fails to unite them to His glorified humanity through the Holy Spirit—regardless of how historically staggering it may be—is disqualified from bearing the name used by the Apostles. 

The events of 1948 may astonish the secular historian and lead the pious believer to acknowledge the marvelous mystery of divine providence; but they cannot rewrite the definition of a miracle, nor can they prematurely cash the divine check of *"all Israel will be saved."* On that heavenly check, the specified method of payment remains forever inscribed:

```bible
"The Deliverer will come from Zion, He will remove ungodliness from Jacob."
— Romans 11:26b (NASB)
```
{: .lang-en lang="en"}

神迹的终极意义是启示耶稣基督。凡不把人引向这位同质同荣的圣子、不把人引入与得荣人性联合的圣灵工作的“奇事”，无论多么震撼，都不够资格领受使徒所用的那个词。

一九四八年可以叫历史学家震惊，叫敬虔之人承认护理；它不能改写神迹的定义，也不能提前支取“以色列全家得救”的支票。支票上写的付款方式，仍是：

```bible
“必有一位救主从锡安出来，要消除雅各家的一切罪恶。”
—— 罗马书 11:26b（和合本）
```
{: .lang-zh lang="zh-CN"}
